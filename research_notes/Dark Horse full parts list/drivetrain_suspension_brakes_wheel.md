## Key fitment facts
- **Axle ratio:** Torsen limited-slip, 3.73:1 with the Tremec 6-speed manual, 3.55:1 with the 10R80 automatic (Ford spec sheets; MotorTrend/TREMEC). The rear end is the Super 8.8 IRS, 34-spline.
- **Two Dark Horse wheel/tire setups:**
  - Standard: 19x9.5 front and 19x10 rear, with Pirelli P Zero PZ4 tires, 255/40ZR19 front and **275/40ZR19 rear**.
  - Handling Package: carbon fibre wheels, 19x10.5 front and **19x11 rear**, with Pirelli P Zero Trofeo RS tires, 305/30ZR19 front and **315/30ZR19 rear**. Michelin lists Pilot Sport Cup 2 for this size.
  - The note "19x10.5 / 305/30 rear" is wrong. Those are front figures. Neither Michelin PS4S nor a PZ4 in 305 is an OEM fitment.
- **Rear brakes:** Brembo 4-piston fixed rear calipers on the GT Performance Pack and Dark Horse, with the electric parking-brake actuator **built into the caliper assembly** (the PR3Z-2552-D listing says "Includes Parking Brake Actuator"). There are separate parking-brake pads. One CJ Pony listing says "6-piston rear", which is wrong; the 6-piston calipers are at the front.
- **Recall 24V593 (Ford 24S49):** rear knuckle casting flaw on about 4,595 2024 Mustangs built 7/25/23 to 11/15/23. The remedy knuckles are **PR3C-5B758-BB (RH) and PR3C-5B759-BB (LH)** (engineering numbers; the service numbers are PR3Z-5B758/759-B). Before buying, check the VIN on NHTSA. Any knuckle sourced used must not be a suspect-casting unit.
