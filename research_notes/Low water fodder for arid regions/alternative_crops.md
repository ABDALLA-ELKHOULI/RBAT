# Alternative drought- and salt-tolerant forage crops to replace or complement berseem (arid Egypt and MENA)

Scope note: about 12 searches. Snippets came from search summaries; few full texts were fetched. Treat numbers as "as reported" and check them against the primary papers before using them in a final report. Unit warning: many Egyptian water use efficiency (WUE) figures are fresh-weight based.

## Which crops give the best dry matter per m3 of water?

### Takeaway
CAM cactus (Opuntia) stands out by a wide margin, at roughly 3 to 6 kg DM per m3 transpired. C4 summer grasses (pearl millet, sorghum) are the best irrigated annuals. Berseem looks good per m3 on a fresh basis, but it is a winter crop with a high total water demand. Perennial irrigated grasses such as Rhodes grass and alfalfa use so much water that Saudi Arabia has restricted them.

### Cited Findings
- Spineless Opuntia 'Ellisiana': 285 mm transpired (out of 662 mm rain) produced 17,670 kg DM/ha. That is about 62 kg DM per ha per mm, or about 6.2 kg DM/m3. The water-to-DM ratio is reported as low as 162 kg water per kg DM. — [CGIAR MEL repository](https://mel.cgiar.org/reporting/downloadmelspace/hash/e93bd596c556981ce90316a0662ccb76/v/2f75c6cf9793152c6b6ee91b8595b5b5)
- A more general cactus estimate is 1 kg DM per 300 kg water, about 3.3 kg DM/m3 (Le Houérou & El Barghati 1982, as cited). — same source
- Higher planting density of Opuntia stricta raised yield and WUE linearly (Brazil). — [Redalyc](https://www.redalyc.org/journal/3030/303071489005/303071489005.pdf)
- Pearl millet on sandy soil under sprinklers (Ismailia, Egypt, 2020–21): WP was 7.04, 6.73, 6.21 and 4.08 kg/m3 at 125%, 100%, 75% ETo and under farmer practice. Irrigating at 100% ETo saved 38% of water compared with farmer practice. The basis (fresh or dry) is likely fresh; this needs checking. — [EJDR/EKB](https://ejdr.journals.ekb.eg/article_466356.html)
- Pearl millet cv. Shandaweel-1 (Matrouh, 2023–24): hole planting gave higher yield and WUE than drilling or broadcasting. — [Agro EKB](https://agro.journals.ekb.eg/article_465877.html)
- System WP: sorghum–Egyptian clover rotation 1.80 kg/m3, and sorghum–multi-cut clover 1.74 kg/m3. — [Crop & Pasture Science](https://publish.csiro.au/CP/CP22139)
- Berseem: Upper Egypt planning value of about 1,197 m3/feddan per season (JICA). Consumptive use at Sakha was 39.8 to 66.6 cm, which is about 1,670 to 2,800 m3/feddan (author's conversion). Yield per applied water was 16.58 kg/m3 and per consumed water 24.80 kg/m3, both fresh. — [JICA](https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/11705639_16.pdf); [JSSAE EKB](https://jssae.journals.ekb.eg/article_51928_b8171bd1b30e2dd189cd30b9f74aa619.pdf)
- Irrigation need for Rhodes grass is about 21,000 to 23,000 m3/ha per season, against 36,000 to 45,000 m3/ha for alfalfa. — [BFA EKB](https://bfa.journals.ekb.eg/article_231893_660333e04db23f319e3d4c52cbe1302e.pdf)
- A Giza study compared the WUE of sorghum, pearl millet, cowpea and guar (sole and mixed) across three irrigation rates. The numbers were not retrieved. — [EKB](https://journals.ekb.eg/article_85637.html)

### Inferences
- If berseem fresh WP of about 16 to 25 kg/m3 is converted at roughly 12 to 15% DM, it comes to about 2 to 3.5 kg DM/m3. That is comparable to C4 millet and below cactus. Berseem's advantage is protein, not water efficiency.
- Ranking by DM per m3, roughly: Opuntia > pearl millet ≈ sorghum > berseem (DM) > Rhodes grass and alfalfa. This ranking is approximate and comes from mixed study bases.

### Gaps
- No same-site dry-matter WP comparison of berseem vs. millet vs. sorghum vs. cactus was found.
- No WP figures were found for blue panic, Sesbania, quinoa, guar, cowpea, moringa or Leucaena.

## Which crops can be grown rainfed or with seawater or brackish water?

### Takeaway
- Rainfed: Opuntia, forage kochia, buffel grass and saltbush.
- Brackish water (about 5 to 20 dS/m): blue panic, Sesbania, salt-tolerant pearl millet, quinoa, triticale and barley.
- Seawater: only halophytes such as Salicornia and Atriplex, and their very high ash content limits how much animals will eat.

### Cited Findings
- Southern Morocco multilocation trial (ICARDA/ICBA): cereals lost biomass as salinity rose, while blue panicum and Sesbania yielded more under moderate and high salinity. — [ICARDA](https://ftp.icarda.org/node/18655)
- Blue panic under saline irrigation in Morocco: cutting every 40 days gave the highest annual fresh and DM yield. — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10282189)
- In ICBA UAE trials (2012–14), Sesbania yield was barely affected as salinity rose from 0.2 to 5 dS/m. — [CGIAR repo](https://repo.mel.cgiar.org/items/6c204308-2592-44f5-914d-59c30a5d0090/full)
- ICBA–ICRISAT screened 30 pearl millet lines in Oman at 7.5 to 8.25 dS/m and selected 10 for forage yield. ICBA has distributed seed to 12 countries. — [ICBA brief](https://iiqp.biosaline.org/sites/default/files/project_brief_plant_genetic_resources_for_marginal_environments_v1-eng-web.pdf)
- Quinoa: ICBA trials since 2007 at 2 to 20 dS/m; five salt- and heat-tolerant lines developed. In Egypt, CICA-17 grain yield fell to about 47% on 20 dS/m soil. The whole plant can be used as green fodder. — [ICBA presentation](https://quinoaconference.biosaline.org/sites/default/files/Quinoa%20-%20Conference%20presentation-nk%20rao.pdf); [PMC7926718](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7926718/)
- Triticale in Deir Ezzor, Syria, showed a relatively low biomass decrease at 19.61 dS/m irrigation water. — [AGRIS](https://agris.fao.org/search/es/records/67daf5d2677d8be0233ca92e)
- Dual-purpose barley at Ras Sudr, Western Sinai, with saline water and soil: late-October sowing gave more than mid-November or December sowing. — [AGRIS](https://agris.fao.org/search/en/records/65df05adb766d82b18ffc641)
- Atriplex nummularia irrigated with seawater yielded 1.53 to 2.89 kg DM/m2/yr, with 25 to 40% ash. Low intake was the main limit. — [BGU / Plant and Soil](https://cris.bgu.ac.il/en/publications/developing-the-seawater-agriculture-concept-2/)
- Salicornia: S. persica fresh yield was about 9.3 kg/m2 (Bushehr) and 22.9 kg/m2 (Yazd), with ash of 48 to 54%. Under Persian Gulf water, the best DM was 651 g/m2, and only S. bigelovii and S. europaea yielded across all planting dates. — [WRA AREEO](https://wra.areeo.ac.ir/article_124782.html?lang=en)
- Forage kochia gives 1,000 to 1,800 kg/ha at 100 to 200 mm rainfall. Under salinity its crude protein (CP) fell from 26% to 15% of DM and salt rose to 19% of DM at 600 mM NaCl (hydroponic study). — [AGRIS](https://agris.fao.org/search/en/records/65de17306eef00c2ce9fb215); [USU](https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/forage_pubs/6)
- Saudi Arabia restricted green fodder from November 2018. Alfalfa and Rhodes grass are listed as prohibited. Perennial fodder is being phased out in favour of seasonal plots of 50 ha or less. Forage used about 12% of Saudi water in 2020. — [Qanoniah](https://qanoniah.com/en/File/Jy4DGw9da7vj1vg78Qv65RrKX-Mechanism-for-Implementing-Regulations-on-Halting-the-Cultivation-of-Green-Fodder); [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/na/2232977); [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4395/15/2/371)
- ICARDA has worked on scaling out water-harvesting and rangeland work in Saudi Arabia, with cactus as a drought supplement. 38 spineless cactus accessions were introduced to Oman in 2015. — [ICARDA](https://icarda.org/node/10613)

### Inferences
- Halophytes irrigated with seawater work only as a partial ration (blended with energy feed), not as a stand-alone replacement for berseem.

### Gaps
- No rainfed yield data for buffel grass in Saudi Arabia was found.
- No Egypt Toshka forage deployment data was found.
- No guar or cowpea salinity thresholds were retrieved.

## Practical establishment and feeding guidance, and costs

### Takeaway
Each alternative has a known feeding limit: Opuntia must be balanced with fibre and protein, sorghum carries a prussic acid (HCN) risk, Leucaena contains mimosine, and halophytes are high in salt and ash. No reliable cost data was found.

### Cited Findings
- Opuntia: when sheep eat about 300 g DM/day of cactus, their drinking water intake drops to nearly zero (from about 2.4 L to 0.1 L; Tunisia, Nefzaoui & Ben Salem). Cactus can replace up to 60% of pasture hay. Cactus silage up to 42% of the diet was fine in goats. — [FAO](https://www.fao.org/4/Y2808E/y2808e0b.htm); [Encyclopedia.pub](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/history/show/60324)
- Sorghum and sudangrass: graze sudangrass from 15 to 20 inches and hybrids from 18 to 30 inches. Do not turn hungry animals onto it. Wait 7 to 14 days after drought-breaking rain or frost. Leave 6 to 8 inches of stubble to limit nitrate. Pearl millet produces no prussic acid (graze from 12 to 15 inches). — [SDSU](https://extension.sdstate.edu/prussic-acid-precautions); [UNL](https://cropwatch.unl.edu/2024/pasture-and-forage-minute-forage-insect-and-water-quality-issues-grazing-livestock/)
- Leucaena: keep it below about 30% of the diet for animals not adapted to it (mimosine/DHP). Adapted cattle, or cattle given DHP-degrading rumen bacteria, can tolerate more than 50%. — [Feedipedia](https://www.feedipedia.org/Node/282); [Trop. Subtrop. Agroecosystems](https://www.revista.ccba.uady.mx/ojs/index.php/TSA/article/view/2026)
- Old man saltbush has low palatability. A seawater-fodder trial needed added corn meal and got intake of about 400 g DM/day in sheep. Reported stocking is about 125 sheep/ha. — [Feedipedia](https://feedipedia.review.fao.org/node/184); [BGU](https://cris.bgu.ac.il/en/publications/developing-the-seawater-agriculture-concept-2/)
- Moringa is described as drought-tolerant with high biomass, but no Egyptian field forage yields were found. — [Nutrinews](https://nutrinews.com/en/moringa-a-drought-proof-forage-for-ruminants/)

### Inferences
- A practical arid ration could combine cactus (water and energy), saltbush or Sesbania (protein) and straw. Summer C4 millet could cover the summer gap that berseem leaves.

### Gaps
- No establishment costs (USD/ha) were found for any crop.
- No numeric seeding rates or establishment protocols were retrieved.
- No nutritive tables (CP, digestibility) were compiled for most crops.
