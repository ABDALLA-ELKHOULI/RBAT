## Apple warranty in Saudi for US-bought products

### Takeaway
Apple's standard 1-year limited warranty lets Apple **restrict hardware warranty service to the country or region of original sale**. Apple Community answers say a US-bought iPhone has no warranty in KSA. I could not retrieve Apple's product-by-product statement (iPhone country-limited versus Mac/iPad/AirPods international) in this session.

### Cited Findings
- The warranty text says Apple may restrict warranty service for hardware products to the country or region where Apple or its Authorized Resellers originally sold the device — [Apple 1-Year Limited Warranty (ROW)](https://www.apple.com/legal/warranty/products/ios-warranty-row-english.html); [EMEA version](https://www.apple.com/legal/warranty/products/ios-warranty-emea-english.html)
