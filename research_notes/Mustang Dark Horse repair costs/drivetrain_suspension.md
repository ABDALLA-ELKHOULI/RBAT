# Mustang Dark Horse (S650, 2024-2026) Drivetrain & Suspension Parts Prices

Research date: 2026-11-06. FX: 1 USD = 3.75 SAR. All prices are retailer listings seen in search results (fordpartsgiant, tascaparts, and other dealer catalogue sites could NOT be fetched directly; network access to them was blocked in this session). So prices come from search-result snippets and need checking against the VIN at a Ford dealer before quoting. Unless a listing said otherwise, "OEM" means a Ford genuine part sold by an online Ford dealer: MSRP is list price, and "sale" is the discounted online price.

## 1. Rear differential / carrier / subframe (cradle)

### Takeaway
The Dark Horse uses a Super 8.8 IRS with a Torsen differential: 3.73 on the manual (TR-3160) and 3.55 on the 10R80 automatic. Ford Performance sells the Torsen differential unit (M-4204-MT) at $775, and a 3.55 loaded housing goes for about $1,415. Search found no new OEM complete 3.73 Dark Horse assembly price. A used rear subframe from a Dark Horse costs about $500.

### Cited Findings
- Dark Horse gearing: 3.73:1 with a standard Torsen differential on manuals, 3.55:1 on automatics. — [Tremec / MotorTrend first look](https://tremec.com/?p=275)
- Ford Performance M-4204-MT "Mustang GT Super 8.8 Torsen Differential" (the differential unit only, 34-spline). Retail $775.00. Ford says it fits 2015-2025 Mustang GT, V6 and I4 with 3.31/3.55/3.73/4.09 gears. One retailer's fitment list names 2024-25 Dark Horse and Dark Horse Premium. Other retailers list it at about $750-$985. It needs 4 oz of CM-19546-A1 friction modifier at the first fill. — [Ford Performance](https://performanceparts.ford.com/part/M-4204-MT); [CJ Pony Parts](https://www.cjponyparts.com/ford-racing-differential-torsen-34-spline-8-8-2015-2017/p/M4204MT/); [Steeda](https://steeda.com/ford-racing-mustang-torsen-differential-2015-gt-v6-ecoboost-161-m0-4204-mt)
- Ford Racing Super 8.8 IRS loaded differential housing, 3.55 ratio, FRPM-4001-88355B: $1,415.00. Its fitment table includes 2024-2025 Dark Horse. Note that it ships with a Traction-Lok differential, not a Torsen. — [Coyote Direct](https://coyotedirect.com/products/ford-racing-2019-ford-mustang-super-8-8in-irs-loaded-differential-housing-3-55); [AmericanMuscle](https://www.americanmuscle.com/ford-performance-irs-loaded-differential-housing-355-1519-gt-v6.html)
- Ford Performance M-4001-88373T, an IRS loaded housing with 3.73 and Torsen, is listed only for 2015-2017. No price was captured. — [Ford Performance](https://performanceparts.ford.com/part/M-4001-88373T)
- Ford Performance Super 8.8 Traction-Lok differential: $230. — [Coyote Direct](https://coyotedirect.com/products/ford-performance-traction-lok-limited-slip-irs-differential-34-spline-8-8-inch)
- OEM carrier/case numbers seen on a 2020 Mustang page (the retailer's fitment list includes Dark Horse). Carriers: FR3Z-4310-B $327.40, FR3Z-4310-C $419.50, JR3Z-4310-A $367.06. Differential case JR3Z-4026-B $322.50. — [fordpartsdeal](https://www.fordpartsdeal.com/parts-select/2020-ford-mustang-differential)
- Used: an eBay listing for a "24 Ford Mustang GT 5.0 MT Torsen rear differential carrier 3.55". The ratio does not match a Dark Horse manual (3.73). Search found no used 3.73 Dark Horse unit on eBay; used 3.73 Torsen units from 2015-2020 GT350 cars do turn up. — [eBay](https://ebay.com/itm/156880168248); [eBay](https://www.ebay.com/itm/156735375056)
- General repair-cost site: an average range of $1,573-$2,921 for a Mustang differential assembly replacement including labour (not specific to the S650). — [Bruisers](https://bruisers.com/repairs/Differential%20Assembly/Ford/Mustang)
- Rear subframe/cradle, used OEM: Redline Auto Parts FM201, "2024-2025 Mustang GT 5.0 / Dark Horse S650 (w/ manual transmission)" rear subframe crossmember, pulled from a 2024 Dark Horse with about 6k miles: $499.99. You reuse your existing hardware. — [Redline Auto Parts](https://www.redlineautoparts.com/ford/2024-current-s650-mustang/2024-2025-ford-mustang-gt-5-0-s650-oem-rear-subframe-crossmember-fm201)
- Used rear cradle JR3Z-5035-J (from a 2024 2.3T EcoBoost car, so it may not fit a V8): about $450. Search snippets cite JR3Z-5035-L as the 2024-25 GT/Dark Horse rear subframe crossmember. — [AH Parts](https://ahparts.com/buy-used/2024-ford-mustang-crossmember-rear-sub-frame-cradle-2-3t-jr3z-5035-j-jr3z5035j/578031-1)
- PR3Z-5025-A is a front/lower crossmember (K-member brace) that fits Dark Horse/GT350/GT500. It is not the rear cradle. — [Mustang7G forum](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/threads/lower-k-member-cross-member-for-s650.160472/latest)

### Inferences
- A new OEM complete rear axle for a Dark Horse (housing, 3.73 gears and Torsen) is not sold as a Ford Performance kit for the S650. A dealer would supply it as a carrier assembly by VIN. A rough parts-only rebuild costs about $775 (Torsen) + ~$1,415 (loaded housing, 3.55 Traction-Lok) or + carrier/case and gears ≈ **$1,500-$2,500 (SAR 5,600-9,400)**. This is an estimate, not a quote.
- Used Dark Horse 3.73 Torsen diffs are rare. GT 3.55 Torsen MT units are more common, but fitting one changes the gearing.
- New OEM rear cradle: no new price was found. The used cradle at $450-$500 (SAR 1,690-1,875) is the main data point.

### Gaps
- No new MSRP was found for the Dark Horse rear differential assembly, the JR3Z-5035-L cradle, or the 3.73 ring and pinion. Fordpartsgiant/tascaparts could not be fetched.

## 2. Rear half shafts (axles)

### Takeaway
OEM rear halfshafts list for about $193-$395 MSRP each ($132-$269 online). Ford Performance replacement/HD shafts cost $1,300-$1,440 each. A used 2024 left-hand shaft costs about $225.

Ford Performance HD kit M-4130-MA: $2,275 (LMR). — [Ford Performance](https://performanceparts.ford.com/part/M-4138-MA); [AmericanMuscle](https://americanmuscle.com/ford-performance-mustang-half-shaft-passenger-side-m-4138-frpma.html); [CJ Pony](https://www.cjponyparts.com/2024-mustang-rear-axle/c/60140000/)

## 3. Rear suspension: control arms, toe links, knuckles, MagneRide shocks

### Takeaway
The OEM arms are cheap: $83-$363 each. Knuckles cost about $438-$463 each, with an MSRP of $643-$795. A 2024 rear knuckle recall exists. Rear MagneRide shocks are roughly $350-$400 each at OEM-type prices. No true aftermarket copy of the S650 MagneRide damper was found; the alternatives are used units or a "shock simulator" bypass kit.

### Cited Findings

The snippets did not map these numbers to side or trim. — [fordpartsgiant](https://fordpartsgiant.com/oem-2024-ford-mustang-control_arm.html); [fordpartsdeal PR3Z-5500-C](https://www.fordpartsdeal.com/oem/ford~arm-asy-rear-suspension~pr3z-5500-c)

- Rear arm set (both sides, uppers and lowers): roughly $600-$1,500 OEM (SAR 2,250-5,600), depending on which PR3Z-5500 variants are needed.

### Gaps

## 6. Labour hours, alignment, rust treatment, Saudi/Gulf prices

### Takeaway
Search found no S650-specific flat-rate labour times. Proxies: dropping the IRS cradle for a gear swap was quoted at about 13 hours on a 2018 GT, and a subframe bushing job is about 4 hours. Search found no prices for Saudi parts or underbody rust-treatment services. Products only: about SAR 74-578.

### Cited Findings
- 2018 GT gear swap that required dropping the rear cradle and pulling the halfshafts: a shop quoted about 13 hours labour. — [StangNet](https://stangnet.com/mustang-forums/threads/13-hr-labor-to-swap-gears.903707/?amp=1)
- IRS subframe bushing kit (removing and reinstalling the subframe, on an older Cobra IRS): listed install time 4 hours. Steeda S550 IRS subframe brace: 1 hour. — [AmericanMuscle](https://www.americanmuscle.com/ftb-subframe-bushings-9904cobra-cust-install-384096.html); [AmericanMuscle Steeda](https://www.americanmuscle.com/steeda-irs-subframe-braces-2015-coupe-manu-install.html)
- A generic (low-reliability) guide gives 6-16 hours at $70-$125 per hour for subframe replacement. — [cost guide](https://new.risingsunartscentre.org/news/subframe-replacement-cost-guide-2026.html)
- Underbody coating products sold in Saudi Arabia (desertcart.com.sa): Blaster Surface Shield 1 gallon SAR 578; Rust-Oleum rubberized undercoat 15 oz SAR 74; Permatex rust treatment 8 oz SAR 120; Eastwood internal frame coating SAR 317. — [desertcart](https://www.desertcart.com.sa/products/663662943-blaster-surface-shield-black-series-professional-automotive-undercoating-for-rust.md); [desertcart](https://www.desertcart.com.sa/products/326861588-248657-rubberized-undercoating-spray-15-oz-black.md)
- Haraj searches returned only whole cars (e.g., a 2024 GT for sale in Taif), not differential or suspension part listings. — [Haraj](https://haraj.com.sa/en/11189479540/2024_50_8/)

### Inferences (estimates, not sourced quotes)
- Rear differential plus cradle R&R: probably 6-13 hours. Rear suspension rebuild (arms, knuckles, shocks): probably 4-8 hours. 4-wheel alignment: about 1 hour. At US dealer rates of about $150-$200 per hour, labour for a full rear drivetrain and suspension job is around $1,500-$3,500. Gulf independent workshops are typically cheaper per hour than this; the agency (the Ford dealer) is not.
- Gulf parts prices for US-sourced Ford parts are commonly the US MSRP plus shipping, customs (5% duty plus 15% VAT in KSA) and margin. As a working rule, US MSRP × 1.3-1.6 is a plausible estimate of the SAR price. This multiplier is not sourced.
- Rust treatment: product cost alone is SAR 100-600. Workshop service pricing was not found.

### Gaps
- No Saudi dealer prices for these parts. Ford's KSA dealer is Al Jazirah Vehicles Agencies; they would need to be called. No Haraj part listings were found.
- No Mitchell/Alldata S650 labour times.
- Fordpartsgiant, tascaparts and haraj could not be fetched in this session (network blocked), so part-to-trim mapping is unverified.
