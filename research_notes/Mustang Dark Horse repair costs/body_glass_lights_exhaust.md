## Body/structure: quarter panels, rear body panel, bumper, trunk floor — OEM vs copy?

### Takeaway
OEM Ford quarter panel assembly for the S650 coupe runs about $1,575 (dealer online) / $2,244 MSRP per side, so both sides is roughly $3,150–$4,490 (~11,800–16,800 SAR) for the outer skins alone; no aftermarket/copy S650 quarter panels were found. The Dark Horse lower rear bumper cover is cheap OEM (~$331), and EOS sells a Dark Horse-style polyurethane conversion bumper ($650–$700).

## Exhaust: Dark Horse active valve mufflers, tips, X-pipe

### Takeaway
OEM Dark Horse active mufflers are ~$1,175–$1,580 each (dealer online) / $1,461–$2,106 MSRP; a pair is ~$3,100–$4,200. A complete Ford Performance Extreme Active cat-back (M-5200-DHE) at ~$2,800 can be cheaper than two OEM mufflers.

## PPF removal + repaint, interior trim, Saudi market, and labor

### Takeaway
No Mustang-specific or Saudi-specific PPF-removal/repaint or quarter-panel labor quotes were found. US industry benchmarks: full quarter panel replacement 8–12 h (some sources 15–30 h) per side at $75–$150/h, $3,000–$6,000 total per side job. Saudi pricing for S650 parts (Haraj, Tamimi, Al Jazirah Ford) was not findable online.

### Cited Findings
- Quarter panel replacement labor: full 8–12 h, partial 5–8 h; other sources 15–30 h; shop rates $75–$125/h or $100–$150+/h; labor $1,500–$4,500; total full replacement $3,000–$6,000 — [Jerry](https://jerry.ai/?p=25644), [JD Power](https://www.jdpower.com/cars/shopping-guides/how-much-does-it-cost-to-fix-the-quarter-panel-on-my-car), [EngineerFix](https://engineerfix.com/how-much-does-it-cost-to-replace-a-quarter-panel/) (aggregator-quality sources)
- Test-fit/alignment time often not included in book labor for quarter replacement — [BodyShop Business](https://www.bodyshopbusiness.com/charge-for-what-you-do-replacing-a-quarter-panel/)
- PPF removal: ~6–10 shop hours for a mid-size vehicle (low-quality generic source); removal can pull paint, hidden cost of edge refinish/repaint — [generic guide](https://new.risingsunartscentre.org/news/cost-of-removing-ppf-what-to-expect-2026.html), [Mustang7G forum](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3503571)
- Saudi: 2024 Mustang new price SAR 240,000–297,000 — [Syarah](https://syarah.com/en/prices/ford/mustang/2024), [DriveArabia](https://www.drivearabia.com/ar/carprices/ksa/ford/ford-mustang/2024/); desertcart.com.sa lists older-generation Mustang aftermarket tail lights SAR 977–1,432 but nothing for S650 — [desertcart](https://www.desertcart.com.sa/products/805906777-led-sequential-tail-lights-for-ford-mustang-2010-2011-2012.md)

### Inferences
- Both quarters at US rates: ~16–24 h labor (more with structural rails/trunk floor) → ~$1,600–$3,600 labor plus refinish; ~6,000–13,500 SAR. Saudi independent shops are commonly cheaper per hour than US but dealer body shops are not — unverified.
- PPF removal + full repaint: no reliable figure; budget separately and get written quotes.

- Dark Horse interior trim pieces (part numbers/prices): not researched successfully within call budget.
