# Electrical, Key, No-Start, Flood Risk and Market Value: Salvage 2024-2026 Mustang Dark Horse (S650) in Saudi Arabia

Research date: 2026-11-06. FX: 1 USD = 3.75 SAR. Note on method: several hosts could not be fetched from this research session (yallamotor.com, edmunds.com, ezkyz.com, redlineautoparts.com), so some findings below rely on search-result snippets of those pages rather than full page reads. Most price data are from forums, aggregators and parts resellers, not official Ford price lists.

## 1. New Intelligent Access key fob + PATS programming when all keys are lost (dealer vs locksmith)

### Takeaway
Expect roughly USD 450-900 (SAR ~1,700-3,400) for an all-keys-lost (AKL) S650 job in the US: an OEM fob is ~USD 300-350 at a dealer plus ~USD 185-230 programming, with AKL often costing more; mobile locksmiths quote somewhat less. Aftermarket/refurbished fobs are USD 45-110, but S650 PATS may reject generic fobs. In Saudi Arabia, the US-spec fob frequency (434 vs 902 MHz variants) and dealer access to the VIN must be checked.

### Cited Findings
- S650 smart fob part number PR3T-15K601-BB (FCC ID M3N-A3C108397 / 7812-A3C108397), 5-button, 434 MHz; refurbished OEM sold at USD 45 (regular USD 59) — [Best Key Supply](https://www.bestkeysupply.com/products/ford-mustang-5button-smart-key-m3na3c108397-pr3t15k601bb-oem1257)
- Another seller lists an original PR3T-15K601-BB at USD 61; a different 2024 part MR3T-15K601-BB (902 MHz) at USD 110 — frequency/part number must match the car — [TLKeys](https://www.tlkeys.com/products/original-ford-smart-key-5-buttons-434mhz-pr3t-15k601-bb-sedan); [TLKeys MR3T](https://www.tlkeys.com/de/products/genuine-ford-mustang-2024-smart-remote-key-5buttons-mr3t-15k601-bb)
- Forum owner: Ford dealers asking USD 300 for fob PR3T-15K601-BB plus USD 185 for programming — [Mustang7G forum](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3329871)
- 2024 Dark Horse owner (New Jersey): factory key ~USD 350 plus USD 230 programming (unclear if all keys lost) — [Mustang7G forum](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3302815)
- Locksmith vendor (EZ Car Keyz) claims dealer Intelligent Access fob AKL for Mustang typically USD 350-600; locksmith spare-key add mid-USD 200s and AKL USD 300-400+; says S650 uses Ford's newest PATS (pages inconsistently call it "PATS Type H" and "PATS6 with encrypted UHF") and won't accept generic aftermarket fobs; advertises on-site AKL for 2024 Mustang without towing — [EZ Car Keyz 2024 Mustang](https://ezkyz.com/vc/car-key/ford/mustang/2024/); [EZ Car Keyz 2023 Mustang](https://ezkyz.com/vc/car-key/ford/mustang/2023/) (vendor marketing; snippet only, page not fetchable)
- Adding a fob via onboard procedure only works when you already have working keys; AKL requires a diagnostic tool — [Mustang7G forum](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3302816); [StangNet](https://stangnet.com/mustang-forums/threads/what-happens-if-the-pats-key-is-lost.641935/?amp=1)

### Inferences
- SAR conversion: dealer route ~USD 485-580 (fob + programming) = SAR ~1,820-2,175; with AKL premium up to USD 600-900 = SAR ~2,250-3,375. Locksmith AKL USD 300-450 = SAR ~1,125-1,690.
- On a salvage car with cut wiring and possibly flood-damaged modules, key programming cannot succeed until the network (BCM, PCM, RF receiver/keyless antennas, cluster) is powered and communicating; key cost is small relative to the electrical diagnosis it depends on.
- Saudi Ford dealers may be reluctant to service a US-market salvage-titled VIN; independent locksmiths with Autel/Xhorse-type tools are the likely route in KSA.

### Gaps
- No official Ford list price or Saudi dealer quote (SAR) for PR3T-15K601-BB or AKL programming was found.
- Could not confirm whether Saudi Ford dealers (Al Jazirah Vehicles Agencies) will program a US-spec salvage S650.

## 2. S650 wiring harness prices (OEM vs repair kits) and labor

### Takeaway
Individual S650 OEM harnesses run roughly USD 600-1,250 each at discount Ford parts sites (front/engine harnesses); the main body/dash harness price was not found. Full interior harness replacement is labor-heavy (dash and interior removal). Splice-repair of cut wires is far cheaper if the cuts are localized.

### Cited Findings
- 2024 Mustang front wire harness PR3Z-14290-AA (2.3L, w/o adaptive cruise): list USD 923.33, sale USD 620.48; PR3Z-14290-NA (2.3L w/ adaptive cruise): list USD 926.67, sale USD 622.72 — [FordPartsGiant](https://fordpartsgiant.com/parts-list/2024-ford-mustang/wiring-harness.html)
- PR3Z-14290-DA (5.0L, without Dark Horse, automatic, no adaptive cruise): sale USD 1,230.16 (shows Dark Horse uses a different number) — [Wholesale Ford Parts Fast](https://www.wholesalefordpartsfast.com/oem-parts/ford-wire-harness-pr3z14290da)
- Used Mustang body/interior harnesses on eBay are cheap (e.g., USD 124.99) but mostly S550-era and of unknown completeness — [eBay Ford Mustang harnesses](https://sv.ebay.com/b/Genuine-OEM-Wiring-Harnesses-Cables-Connectors-for-Ford-Mustang/179847/bn_30101752)
- European dismantler lists a 5.0 V8 "complete wire network" for 2015+ Mustang at EUR 296.87 ex-VAT plus EUR 214.50 shipping (likely not S650-specific) — [Autoparts-24](https://www.autoparts-24.com/item/D_0133_685712/?pos=20)

### Inferences
- In SAR: one front harness ~SAR 2,330-4,610; if the main body (14A005-type) and rear/trunk harnesses also need replacement, parts could plausibly total USD 2,500-5,000+ (SAR 9,400-18,750+) before shipping to KSA and labor. This is an extrapolation from the front-harness prices, not a quoted figure.
- Cut harnesses on salvage cars are often where thieves or yards cut to remove modules, the PCM, or the cluster/infotainment; a "cut harness" plus missing key and dead cluster may mean modules were removed or swapped. Inspect what is missing.
- Repair with OEM-style connector pigtails/solder-and-heat-shrink is feasible for a few clean cuts; flood exposure makes splicing risky because corrosion wicks inside insulation.

### Gaps

- No S650-specific aftermarket "repair kit" found (generic Painless-type kits are for classic Mustangs only).

## 3. Digital instrument cluster (12.4" cluster + 13.2" SYNC 4 screen) replacement, mileage programming, and mileage verification

### Takeaway
A used S650 12.4" cluster sells around USD 900 (SAR ~3,375); cluster control module ~USD 225 used. A replacement cluster must be programmed to the VIN/mileage. Mileage is not stored only in the cluster: forum evidence says Ford stores odometer data in multiple modules (cluster, and often PCM/BCM), so a scan tool can read it even if the screen is dead. Dealer OASIS, Carfax/AutoCheck, and the Copart/IAAI lot record (which lists odometer) are the main verification routes.

### Cited Findings
- Used 2024-2025 S650 OEM instrument cluster display PR3T-18B955-BH: USD 899.99, "verify part number" — [Redline Auto Parts](https://www.redlineautoparts.com/ford/2024-current-s650-mustang/2024-2025-ford-mustang-s650-oem-instrument-gauge-cluster-display-pr3t18b955bh-fm202/) (snippet only)
- Used 2024 S650 speedometer cluster control module PR3T-10849-AJ: USD 225 — [eBay](https://www.ebay.com/itm/156940151635) (via search snippet)
- Forum posts disagree on storage: one says PCM no longer tracks mileage on newer Fords (cluster only); another says both PCM and cluster store it separately; another names BCM — [Ranger6G](https://www.ranger6g.com/forum/goto/post?id=199152); [Ranger6G](https://www.ranger6g.com/forum/threads/instrument-cluster-failed.11574/page-4); [Ford-Trucks](https://www.ford-trucks.com/forums/1763472-question-about-possible-pcm-replacement.html)
- Ford dealers often refuse odometer corrections; tampering is a US federal matter; Ford dealer OASIS report can show service-visit mileages but only if the car visited dealers; Carfax only reports what is reported to it — [Ford-Trucks odometer rollback](https://www.ford-trucks.com/forums/722841-odometer-rollback-verification.html?nojs=1); [Mustang6G](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/odometer-is-now-incorrect-what-would-you-do.106073/post-2286844)
- Copart/aggregator lot records publish odometer readings at sale (e.g., a 2024 Dark Horse lot recorded 14,956 km) — [CarsBat](https://carsbat.com/en/detail/1fa6p8r00r5506716-jlsffb/)

### Inferences
- Verification order: (1) run the VIN on Copart/IAAI history aggregators (CarsBat, stat.vin, dreambid) to see the auction odometer, damage type and photos; (2) Carfax/AutoCheck for title brand (salvage/flood) and reported mileage; (3) Ford dealer OASIS for warranty/recall visits; (4) once powered, read odometer from PCM/BCM/cluster with FORScan or dealer IDS/FDRS.
- Auction odometer is often marked "not actual" or "exempt" on damaged cars when the cluster cannot be read; that should be treated as unknown mileage.
- The 13.2" center SYNC 4 screen (APIM) is a separate module; its replacement price was not found.

### Gaps
- No new-part dealer price for the S650 cluster or the 13.2" SYNC screen/APIM was found.
- No official Ford service documentation confirming which S650 modules store odometer.

## 4. Common no-start causes after flood/collision; PCM/BCM module cost

### Takeaway
After flood/collision, typical S650 no-start causes are: no programmed key (PATS immobilizer), dead/shorted 12V battery and corroded battery/junction terminals, cut or corroded harnesses/connectors, flood-damaged PCM/BCM/RF receiver, inertia/crash fuel shutoff triggered by the restraints module, and missing modules. Replacement PCMs must be VIN-programmed and PATS-synced. Older Mustang PCM pricing (USD 460-700 new + programming USD 150-300) is a floor; no current S650 PCM/BCM price was found.

### Cited Findings
- Replacement modules must be VIN-programmed and synced to PATS; PATS prevents starting until the module is introduced; engine cranks but won't fire with immobilizer active — [Go-Parts guide](https://www.go-parts.com/garage/engine-control-module-ecm-ford-mustang-2013-2014)
- Ford service message: no-start with code P161A after PCM replacement if parameter reset not done — [Go-Parts P0602 guide](https://www.go-parts.com/garage/obd-p0602-ford-mustang-2004-2016)
- 2013-2014 Mustang ECM: new OEM USD 460-700 + core; reman USD 250-450; programming labor USD 150-300 (older model years) — [Go-Parts](https://www.go-parts.com/garage/engine-control-module-ecm-ford-mustang-2013-2014); [Go-Parts 2007-2008](https://www.go-parts.com/garage/engine-control-module-ecm-ford-mustang-2007-2008)
- Ford dealers have the proprietary software for programming and key relearn; some locksmiths have PATS tools — [Go-Parts](https://www.go-parts.com/garage/engine-control-module-ecm-ford-mustang-2006)
- Flood corrosion makes modules (BCM, airbag sensors) fail and these are expensive; connector corrosion can take weeks or months to show symptoms — [EngineerFix](https://engineerfix.com/can-a-flooded-car-be-fixed-the-real-costs/); [Motor.com](https://www.motor.com/2021/10/more-than-a-half-million-flood-damaged-cars-on-the-road-do-you-know-what-you-are-working-on/)

### Inferences
- For a 2024 S650 the PCM/BCM are likely USD 600-1,500 each new at a dealer plus programming; this is extrapolation from older-model pricing and should be verified. In SAR roughly 2,250-5,600 per module.
- A sensible diagnostic sequence: fresh battery and terminal check, inspect fuses/junction boxes for corrosion, repair cut harness, scan the CAN network (which modules respond), then program keys. Budget USD 300-1,000 (SAR 1,125-3,750) just for diagnosis on a car this damaged (inference, no source).

### Gaps
- No sourced price for S650 (2024+) PCM, BCM, RF transceiver or restraint module.
- No sourced data on S650-specific flood failure patterns.

## 5. Flood damage signs, flood restoration cost, and why flood cars are risky

### Takeaway
Mud/silt inside the cabin is a classic flood indicator. Flood restoration of a modern module-heavy car often costs several thousand to tens of thousands of USD, and the main risk is delayed electrical failure from corrosion in connectors and modules, including safety systems (airbags). Saudi Arabia bans import of flood vehicles, which also creates legal/registration risk.

### Cited Findings
- Signs: moist carpets, fogged headlights/taillights, silt/sand under the car and in crevices, rust on underbody and seat rails, corrosion under paint, intermittent windows/locks, warning lights, malfunctioning infotainment — [EngineerFix](https://engineerfix.com/can-a-flooded-car-be-fixed-the-real-costs/); [carVertical](https://studio.carvertical.com/blog/how-to-detect-signs-of-flood-damage-in-cars); [Edmunds](https://edmunds.com/car-buying/how-to-avoid-buying-a-flood-damaged-car.html) (snippet only)
- Waterline height largely determines feasibility; once main harnesses and electronics are involved it often becomes a total loss — [EngineerFix](https://engineerfix.com/can-a-flooded-car-be-fixed-the-real-costs/)
- Repair-guide advice: budget major parts and labor, then roughly double for unexpected electrical and corrosion issues — [EngineerFix](https://engineerfix.com/can-a-flooded-car-be-fixed-the-real-costs/)
- Technician guidance: visual inspection of harnesses/connectors for corrosion first, then pre-repair scans for module codes — [AutomotiveTechInfo (PDF)](https://automotivetechinfo.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Spotting-and-Repairing-Flood-Damage.pdf)
- Saltwater causes accelerated, near-unrecoverable corrosion; muddy water contaminates mechanical systems — [dailydriven.ro](https://dailydriven.ro/en/buying-or-repairing-flood-damaged-cars-costs-and-risks-1596915)
- Flood-damaged vehicles "can cost thousands to repair" (June 2026 local news) — [KSLA](https://www.ksla.com/2026/06/15/flood-damaged-vehicles-can-cost-thousands-repair/)
- Unverified secondary claim: NHTSA ~482,000 flood vehicles (2024) and Consumer Reports USD 3,000-8,000 electrical failure costs — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/robertatkinson3570/how-to-spot-a-flood-car-before-you-buy-it-482000-are-for-sale-4ggh) (not confirmed from primary sources; do not rely on)
- Resale value drops after flood even if repaired — [EngineerFix](https://engineerfix.com/can-a-flooded-car-be-fixed-the-real-costs/)

### Inferences
- Restoration scope for a flooded S650 to waterline above floor: carpet/padding, seats (incl. heated/power seat motors and occupant-classification sensor), under-seat/floor modules, harness connectors, airbag/restraint module and sensors, SYNC/cluster if water reached dash. A rough total of USD 5,000-20,000+ (SAR 18,750-75,000+) is plausible for a thorough job; this is an inference, not a sourced estimate.
- Mud inside the car, plus no key, unreadable cluster and cut harness, together strongly suggests flood or a car that was stripped; treat as worst case until the Copart/IAAI primary damage code is confirmed.

- Could not fetch Edmunds or NHTSA primary pages.

## 6. Market values (clean USA and Saudi) and salvage auction comparables; Saudi import rules

### Takeaway
Clean 2024 Dark Horse: KBB resale ~USD 46,200 (SAR ~173,250), dealer asking USD high-50k to 60k+. Saudi used asking prices ~SAR 285,000-290,000 for low-km cars; new SAR 315,900 (auto). Salvage comparables: a front-damaged 2024 Dark Horse sold on Copart for USD 29,750 (April 2026); flood 2025 base Mustangs sell around USD 11,200. Saudi rules (per commercial guides and 2011 customs announcement) ban import of flood/accident-damaged/salvage cars; duty 5% + VAT 15%.

### Inferences
- A clean 2024 Dark Horse is worth roughly SAR 285,000-290,000 asking in KSA vs ~SAR 173,000 (KBB) to ~SAR 225,000 (US asking) in the US, so Saudi prices carry a large premium; that premium is what drives salvage imports.
- Landed cost of a US salvage Dark Horse: e.g., USD 29,750 hammer + auction/fees + shipping, then duty 5% and VAT 15% on CIF value (VAT applies on value plus duty). Example: USD 32,000 CIF -> duty USD 1,600 -> VAT 15% of 33,600 = USD 5,040 -> ~USD 38,640 (SAR ~144,900) before repairs. Illustrative only.
- Because flood/salvage-history cars are banned for import, a car with a US salvage/flood brand that is now in Saudi Arabia may have entered via another GCC country, been documented as non-damaged, or be unregistrable; the buyer should verify it has valid Saudi registration (Istimara) and passes Fahas inspection. A flood brand on Carfax may block registration or resale.
- A flood-branded, non-running, keyless Dark Horse with unknown mileage should be valued well below the USD 29,750 front-damage comp; flood lots of base Mustangs trade at ~USD 11k, and buyers typically discount flood Dark Horses heavily (inference).
