# Saudi Arabia retail and second-hand prices vs US (research date: 2026-11-06)

**How this research was done:** All searches ran on 2026-11-06. This cloud session's network blocked direct page fetches (apple.com, themacindex.com, sa.pricena.com and shbabbek.com all failed DNS), so every figure below comes from search-engine snippets of the cited pages, not from pages I opened and verified. Treat the figures as indicative.

**Units and tax:** USD is converted at the 3.75 SAR peg. Saudi shelf prices include 15% VAT. US list prices exclude sales tax.

**US reference prices:** Figures marked "US ref" are the analyst's background knowledge of US MSRPs as of mid-2026. They were not sourced in this research and must be verified against the US-side research notes.

## Typical SAR prices on each platform, and Haraj / used resale prices

### Takeaway
The gap between Saudi and US prices is smallest for Apple, perfumes and Dyson. It is largest for products with no official Saudi channel: Steam Deck, Meta Quest and high-end NVIDIA GPUs.
- **Apple (official Saudi pricing):** about 12-27% above US pre-tax MSRP. Most of that gap is the 15% VAT. Amazon.sa already discounts iPhone 17 Pro Max close to US parity.
- **Perfumes (Creed, Dior):** roughly at or below US prices.
- **Dyson:** close to US prices at authorised retailers.
- **Grey-import gaming hardware and GPUs:** listings run at 2x or more the US price.
- **Sneakers and supplements:** moderately above US, by about 20-50%.

- **Biggest gaps:** niche or grey-import tech with no official Saudi distribution (Steam Deck, Quest, RTX 5090), then sneakers and supplements.
- **Smallest gaps:** Apple, Dyson and perfume. These leave little margin once 15% VAT and any customs duty on imports are added.
- **Apple arbitrage is thin:** Amazon.sa discounting already brings Apple flagships close to US parity.
- **Customs:** personal shipments under SAR 1,000, including shipping, are exempt from duty. 15% VAT may still apply. Above SAR 1,000, duty is generally 5%, and 0-15% by HS code. — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/611390/SAUDI-ARABIA/Customs-No-duty-for-shipments-valued-below-SR1000); [Stackry](https://www.stackry.com/duties-and-taxes/saudi-arabia). ### Gaps

## Do Saudi buyers discount US-version items (no local warranty, US plug, region)?

### Takeaway
I found no direct evidence quantifying a US-version discount. What I did find:
- Saudi retailers market "Middle East version" units explicitly.
- Jarir's warranty policy ties service to the manufacturer or its legal Saudi agent.
- Grey-market listings carry authenticity complaints.

### Cited Findings
- Jarir's warranty is provided by the manufacturer, or by its official agents if the manufacturer is not legally present in the Kingdom, under the manufacturer's policy. — [Jarir warranty policy](https://www.jarir.com/sa-en/warranty_policy)
- Carrefour KSA labels its Apple Watch Ultra 3 as "Middle East version". — [Carrefour KSA](https://www.carrefourksa.com/mafsau/ar/smart-watches-wearables/apple-watch-ultra-3-gps-cellular-49mm-middle-east-version-black-titanium-case-with-black-ocean-band/p/2817000000026)
- Desertcart lists "UAE version" PS5 units separately, so region labelling matters to buyers. — [Desertcart SA](https://www.desertcart.com.sa/products/593389476-playstation-5-disc-console-new-2023-slim-model-uae-version.md)
- A buyer review on a cheap Creed Aventus listing calls it fake. — [Desertcart SA](https://www.desertcart.com.sa/products/253698152-aventus-100ml-men-edp.md)

### Inferences
- Region labelling and warranty matter most for these items:
  - US iPhone 17 models are eSIM-only, which is likely a meaningful discount factor for Saudi buyers. This is background knowledge, not sourced here.
  - Dyson (US 120V plug and voltage).
  - DeWalt and Milwaukee (US voltage chargers).
- Region matters little for perfume, sneakers, clothing, bags, LEGO and supplements. Authenticity proof (receipts) matters more there.

### Gaps
- I found no Haraj or forum data quantifying how far US-version listings sell below local versions. Arabic forums and r/saudiarabia were not reachable.

## Saudi White Friday 2026 dates and expected discounts

### Takeaway
White Friday 2026 centres on Friday 27 November 2026. Amazon.sa's 2025 event ran 20-30 November with "up to 70%" off. Noon's Yellow Friday 2026 had not been officially announced as of 20 October 2026, and 20-30 November is the expected window.

### Cited Findings
- Salla's seasons calendar lists White Friday on 27 November 2026. — [Salla](https://salla.com/en/tools/seasons-calendar/seasons/white-friday-2026)
- A countdown site uses Thursday 26 November instead. — [Khaleej Calculators](https://khaleejcalculators.com/countdown/white-friday)
- An Arabic article says Noon's Yellow Friday date was not announced as of 20 October 2026, and estimates 20-30 November based on past years. — [Shbabbek](https://shbabbek.com/show/285682)
- Amazon.sa White Friday 2025:
  - Ran 20-30 November.
  - Prime early access started at midnight on 20 November.
  - Discounts were advertised at up to 70%, with brands including Apple, Sony, Bose and Samsung.
  - Prime costs SAR 16 per month or SAR 140 per year.
  - Source: [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2174404)
- Belkin deals on Amazon.sa ran 20-30 November 2025. — [M283 Arabia](https://m283arabia.com/en/gadgets-tech-and-gaming/belkin-unveils-white-friday-line-up-of-must-have-tech-gifts)

### Inferences
- White Friday discounts on Apple, Sony and Bose, plus existing everyday Amazon.sa markdowns such as the iPhone 17 Pro Max about 15% below official, would erode the small margin on imported Apple and audio goods.
- Grey-import-only items (Steam Deck, Quest) are less likely to see deep local discounts.

### Gaps
- No announced 2026 discount levels from Amazon.sa, Noon, Jarir or Extra.
- No category-level 2025 discount data, for example actual AirPods or Dyson sale prices.
