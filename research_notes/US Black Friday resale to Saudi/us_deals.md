## Q6. Sales tax: tax-free states and forwarder warehouses

### Takeaway
The five US states with no state sales tax include Delaware, Oregon and New Hampshire (plus Montana and Alaska), and forwarders with warehouses there avoid US sales tax. Forwardme (Delaware) explicitly ships to Saudi Arabia via DHL/FedEx/Aramex. Planet Express (Tualatin, Oregon), Shipito (Oregon) and Stackry (New Hampshire, serves KSA) are alternatives.

### Cited Findings
- Forwardme: US street address in tax-free Delaware, ships to Saudi Arabia via DHL, FedEx, Aramex — [Forwardme](https://www.forwardme.com/shipping/us-to-saudi-arabia)
- Forward2me: US warehouse in Delaware (Saudi coverage not confirmed in the result) — [Forward2me](https://www.forward2me.com/warehouses/united-states/)
- Planet Express: Tualatin, Oregon, no-sales-tax warehouse — [Planet Express](https://planetexpress.com/)
- Shipito: tax-free Oregon address (Saudi shipping not confirmed in the result) — [ShipToBox comparison](https://www.shiptobox.com/blog/best-package-forwarding-companies/)
- Stackry: New Hampshire (no sales tax), serves Saudi Arabia — [Stackry](https://www.stackry.com/); [Forwarders Compare KSA](https://forwarderscompare.com/saudiarabia.php)
- ParcelGoose forwards to Saudi Arabia — [ParcelGoose](https://shipforwarding.com/)

### Inferences
- Shipping to a DE/OR/NH address avoids typical sales tax of about 6–10%. That saving is on the same scale as many early BF discounts (e.g., AirPods Pro 3's 20%).
- Saudi customs duty (generally about 5–15% depending on HS code) and 15% VAT apply on import. These rates are general knowledge, not sourced here, and the report writer should verify them in a separate thread.

- Restrictions on hazmat (perfume, lithium batteries) by forwarder: not researched.
