# Landed Cost and Legal Requirements: Importing US Consumer Goods into Saudi Arabia for Resale (2026)

Research method note (2026-11-06): WebFetch could not reach outside hosts in this session (stackry.com, mydhl.express.dhl and globaltaxnews.ey.com all failed DNS resolution). Every finding below comes from search-result snippets, not full-page reads. Treat figures as indicative and check them on the primary page before using them in a business case. ## 1. Package forwarders US to KSA: rates, volumetric rules, fees, transit

### Takeaway
Forwarder pricing to KSA is roughly USD 20-42 for a 1 lb parcel (MyUS sample table dated Oct 2026). Expect to pay small per-package processing and consolidation fees as well. Billing uses the greater of actual or volumetric weight, with a divisor of 139 in imperial or 5000 in metric. I found no official, current per-0.5 kg Aramex Shop & Ship tariff for KSA. Naqel and SMSA forwarding products did not surface at all.

### Cited Findings
- **MyUS membership:** a free "Single Package" tier and a Premium tier at USD 9.99/month or USD 60/year. Premium includes 30 days' free storage, unlimited packages and free consolidation. The FAQ page appears to be several years old, so the price may be outdated. A free 30-day Premium trial is also referenced. — [MyUS Membership FAQ](https://www.myus.com/faq/myus-membership/); [MyUS eBay to Saudi](https://www.myus.com/stores/how-to-shop-ebay-and-ship/saudi-arabia/)
- **MyUS sample rates to Saudi Arabia, 1 lb package:** Budget Economy about USD 19.51, Aramex about USD 21.87, FedEx Economy about USD 25.61, FedEx Priority about USD 31.73, DHL Express about USD 42.25. The page is dated about 2026-10-05, and MyUS says prices can change without notice. — [MyUS Apple to Saudi](https://www.myus.com/stores/how-to-shop-apple-and-ship/saudi-arabia/)
- **MyUS transit times:** economy 4-10 business days, express 1-4 business days. MyUS pricing does not include Saudi duty or VAT, which is usually collected on delivery. — [MyUS Saudi Arabia](https://www.myus.com/where-we-ship/saudi-arabia/)
- **MyUS Family (DHL-based example):** 1 lb shipment USD 27.84; 4 lb split among four people USD 30.77 total. A USD 4.99 fee applies for Single Package members. — [MyUS Family](https://www.myus.com/benefits/shipping/myus-family/)
- **Shipito:** bills on the greater of actual or dimensional weight, using divisor 139 for most carriers and 166 for USPS Priority. Processing fee is USD 3.25 per package (free account) or USD 2.25 (Premium). Consolidation fee is USD 5.50 or USD 3.25. This comes from a third-party calculator site. — [DutyGlobal Shipito](https://dutyglobal.com/forwarders/shipito); [Atvido Shipito review 2026](https://atvido.com/shipito-review/)
- **Planet Express:** 5-30 business days to KSA depending on method. Sample: 0.5 kg, 25x18x10 cm parcel via Planet Mail Express USD 13.59. The page is about two years old. — [Planet Express US to Saudi guide](https://planetexpress.com/how-to/shipping-from-the-usa-to-saudi-arabia-guide/)
- **Stackry:** cost is calculated from package size and weight after you choose a carrier. No per-kg rate is published in the snippet. A review says Stackry is competitive on light parcels but gets expensive quickly on bulky ones. — [Stackry US to Saudi](https://www.stackry.com/parcel-forwarding/parcel-forwarding-from-the-usa-to-saudi-arabia); [ShipToBox 2026 comparison](https://www.shiptobox.com/blog/best-package-forwarding-companies/)
- **Aramex Shop & Ship (forum figures only, undated, unverified):**
  - One post says rates are charged per half-kg starting from about SAR 35.
  - Another estimates about SAR 90 per kg before VAT (about SAR 100 with VAT).
  - One commenter says the US side bills by the pound.
  - [Expat.com thread 365135](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/middle-east/saudi-arabia/riyadh/365135-shopandship-aramex-vs-url.html); [Expat.com thread 806210](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/middle-east/saudi-arabia/806210-shop-ship-aramex.html)
- **Volumetric divisor:**
  - 5000 cm3/kg (metric) or 139 (imperial) is the courier standard, used for example by DHL and FedEx.
  - Some China-to-KSA air services use 6000.
  - Confirm the divisor for each specific service. — [Boxit4me US to Saudi cost examples](https://www.boxit4me.com/blog/us-to-saudi-shipping-cost-examples/); [Winsail Logistics](https://winsaillogistics.com/shipping-cost-per-kg-from-china-to-saudi-arabia/); [Wikipedia: Dimensional weight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimensional_weight)

### Inferences
- For landed-cost modelling, a reasonable working range is USD 20-40 for the first lb plus roughly USD 6-12 per additional lb via the economy or Aramex options. This range is my extrapolation, not a sourced figure. Bulky items such as Dyson boxes and consoles will be billed at volumetric weight, so repacking or consolidating at the forwarder matters.
- Consolidating several Black Friday orders into one shipment cuts per-package fees. However, it can push the declared value above SAR 1,000 (see section 3), which triggers duty.

### Gaps
- No official current Aramex Shop & Ship KSA rate card was found. Its membership fee, which I recall as an annual fee, could not be verified. Its US warehouse location also could not be verified (I believe it is Delaware, but this is unverified).
- No 2026 data was found for Naqel, SMSA or Borderlinx alternatives offering US-to-KSA forwarding.
- Sales-tax-free warehouse locations were not sourced in this session. It is general knowledge that Delaware and Oregon have no state sales tax, and that MyUS uses Florida (which charges sales tax) while Shipito uses Oregon/California. All of this needs verification.

## 2. Commercial courier / air freight rates (20-100 kg)

### Takeaway
I found no reliable published DHL, FedEx or UPS per-kg rates for 20-100 kg US-to-KSA shipments. One undated third-party estimate puts DHL Express at about USD 575 for 20 kg, about USD 29/kg. DDP pricing has to be quoted case by case.

### Cited Findings
- **DHL Express Worldwide estimate (undated, third party):** about USD 89 for a 4.5 kg box and about USD 575 for a 20 kg box. — [ShipBob US to Saudi](https://www.shipbob.com/shipping-from-us/to-saudi-arabia/)
- **Duties:** the recipient normally pays duties unless the sender prepays (DDP). DDP options are listed on Easyship's US-to-KSA calculator. — [Easyship US to Saudi](https://www.easyship.com/shipping-rate-calculator/usa-to-saudiarabia)
- **Restricted items:** some carriers list radio transceivers and smartwatches among restricted electronics for KSA. This comes from a third-party summary, and the smartwatch restriction looks questionable. — [ShipBob](https://www.shipbob.com/shipping-from-us/to-saudi-arabia/)

### Inferences
- At about USD 29/kg, retail express courier is usually worse than forwarder economy rates for the same weight. Forwarders resell discounted courier rates. For 50-100 kg, general air cargo through a freight forwarder plus a Saudi customs broker is likely cheaper per kg, but it requires a CR and importer documentation.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 per-kg courier or air-cargo rates were found for 20, 50 or 100 kg. A live quote from DHL, FedEx or UPS, or from an air-cargo forwarder, is needed.

## 3. Saudi customs: duty rates, de minimis, VAT, clearance fees, personal vs commercial

### Takeaway
VAT of 15% applies to all imports regardless of value. Customs duty is waived for consignments of SAR 1,000 or less. Above SAR 1,000, duty is generally 5% of CIF, with some lines at 10-15%+, and 15% VAT is then charged on CIF + duty. Couriers add clearance and disbursement fees. Category-specific rates for perfume, cosmetics and electronics could not be confirmed from the official ZATCA tariff.

### Cited Findings
- **Duty threshold:** shipments valued below SAR 1,000 are exempt from customs duty. — [Saudi Gazette: "No duty for shipments valued below SR1000"](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/611390/SAUDI-ARABIA/Customs-No-duty-for-shipments-valued-below-SR1000)
- **VAT below the threshold:**
  - DHL's regulatory update says 15% VAT applies regardless of value.
  - B2C and C2C shipments under SAR 1,000 are cleared on a consolidated entry; larger ones get an individual entry. The update is effective 2024-01-01. — [DHL customs regulatory updates, Middle East](https://mydhl.express.dhl/at/en/help-and-support/customs-clearance-advice/customs-regulatory-updates/africa-middle-east.html) (search snippet only)
  - Another source says 15% VAT has applied to all shipments regardless of value since 2020-07-01. — [Fuuffy Saudi tax & duty](https://www.fuuffy.com/en/tax-and-duty/SAUDI%20ARABIA)
  - Conflicting sources: Planet Express says SAR 1,000 is the de minimis for both duty and tax, and Easyship cites a SAR 266 tax/duty threshold. Both are likely outdated. — [Planet Express](https://planetexpress.com/how-to/shipping-from-the-usa-to-saudi-arabia-guide/); [Easyship](https://www.easyship.com/shipping-rate-calculator/usa-to-saudiarabia)
- **VAT base:** VAT is charged on goods value plus shipping, insurance and duty (CIF + duty). — [Fuuffy](https://www.fuuffy.com/en/tax-and-duty/SAUDI%20ARABIA); [Commenda: Saudi import VAT](https://www.commenda.io/saudi-arabia/import-vat)
- **General duty level:** duty is 0-15% depending on item type. The trade.gov country guide covers the import tariff structure. — [Fuuffy](https://www.fuuffy.com/en/tax-and-duty/SAUDI%20ARABIA); [trade.gov Saudi import tariffs](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/saudi-arabia-import-tariffs)
- **2024 tariff changes:**
  - Resolution No. 1-88-1446 (15 July 2024) raised duty on HS 85352100, 85352900 and 85364900 from 5% to 15%. These are electrical switches and fuses, not consumer electronics. — [EY Tax News 2024-1439](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2024-1439-saudi-arabia-issues-resolution-amending-customs-duties-on-certain-goods)
  - A 2020 round of increases covered dairy, food, chemicals, plastic, rubber, leather, paper, clothes, shoes, fabrics, ceramics, glass, metals, furniture and machinery. — [KPMG KSA customs alert June 2020](https://kpmg.com/sa/en/home/insights/2020/06/customs-alert.html)
- **Category rates (third-party calculators, conflicting):**
  - Electronics: "5%" on one site, 8.5% under HS 8528.72 (TVs) on another, and 0% for smartphones on another. — [DutyDecoder US to Saudi electronics](https://dutydecoder.com/calculate/us-to-saudi-arabia-electronics-import/); [CalcMyTariff consumer electronics](https://www.calcmytariff.com/country/saudi-arabia/consumer-electronics)
  - Cosmetics and perfumes: 6.5% on one calculator. — [Hijri-calendars customs calculator](https://hijri-calendars.com/customs-duty-calculator.php?lang=en)
  - I found no official confirmation of any of these.
- **Courier clearance fees:**
  - Aramex: customs service fee of 0.15% of (declared value + freight + insurance), minimum SAR 15, plus a USD 10 clearance charge on shipments over SAR 1,000. This is per Stackry's Saudi duties page, via search snippet. — [Stackry duties & taxes Saudi Arabia](https://www.stackry.com/duties-and-taxes/saudi-arabia)
  - Carriers generally charge SAR 20-100 as a disbursement fee for paying ZATCA on the importer's behalf. — [Fuuffy](https://www.fuuffy.com/en/tax-and-duty/SAUDI%20ARABIA)
- **Who collects:** ZATCA assesses the duty and the carrier collects it at delivery or before release. With Aramex, Stackry collects payment before forwarding. — [Stackry](https://www.stackry.com/duties-and-taxes/saudi-arabia)
- **Personal vs commercial:**
  - Commercial-quantity requirements (for example SFDA or conformity documents) apply once the quantity exceeds personal-use limits. The limit is not stated in the source. — [Chamber International: cosmetic clearance requirements](https://www.chamber-international.com/uploads/files/requirements-and-documents-for-clearing-cosmetic-products-from-customs-ports.pdf)
  - DHL notes there is no general duty-free threshold for business shipments; duty depends on the HS code. — [MyDHL+ Saudi customs regulations](https://mydhl.express.dhl/yt/en/help-center/international-shipping/customs-regulations-saudi-arabia.html)

### Inferences
- **Working formula:**
  - Landed = item price (USD to SAR at 3.75) + US sales tax (if any) + forwarder fees + international shipping.
  - Duty = 5% x CIF (if the consignment is over SAR 1,000; check the HS rate).
  - VAT = 15% x (CIF + duty).
  - Then add courier clearance/disbursement (about SAR 15-100).
  - Example: a SAR 3,000 item with SAR 150 shipping, so CIF = 3,150. Duty at 5% = 157.5. VAT at 15% of 3,307.5 = 496.1. Total taxes about SAR 654, or about 21.8% of item price, plus fees.
- Splitting shipments to stay under SAR 1,000 saves duty but not VAT. Systematic splitting for resale may be treated as evasion or commercial importing (see the risks in section 6).

### Gaps
- The official ZATCA integrated tariff (HS chapters 33, 84, 85, 95) was not accessible. Duty rates for perfume (3303), cosmetics (3304), smartphones (8517), consoles (9504) and hair dryers (8516) remain unconfirmed.
- I found no ZATCA primary text confirming the 2026 status of the SAR 1,000 threshold.

## 4. Personal baggage allowance (carrying goods when flying US to KSA)

### Takeaway
Personal effects and gifts are duty-exempt only if they are personal in nature, not commercial in quantity, and (per secondary sources) do not exceed SAR 3,000 in value. Goods brought for resale are not covered by this exemption and should be declared.

### Cited Findings
- **Legal basis:** ZATCA's airport guide cites Article 103 of the GCC Common Customs Law, which exempts passengers' personal effects and gifts that are "of a personal nature and not in commercial quantities." — [ZATCA customs guide: airports](https://eservices.zatca.gov.sa/sites/sc/en/CustomsGuideNew/CustomsGuideAirPorts/Pages/Page02.aspx)
- **SAR 3,000 cap:** secondary sources report it for luggage and gifts that are personal, non-commercial and not restricted. The World Travel Guide says items over SAR 3,000 must be declared. — [Wego blog](https://blog.wego.com/saudi-arabia-travellers-must-declare-cash-and-gold/); [World Travel Guide](https://www.worldtravelguide.net/guides/middle-east/saudi-arabia/money-duty-free/) (about 5 years old); [House of Saud](https://houseofsaud.com/travel/saudi-customs-rules/) (says it could not verify the figure on ZATCA)
- **Arrival duty-free shops (separate rule):** purchases are capped at SAR 3,000 per passenger for personal use, with 200 cigarettes. This applies to arrival duty-free shops, not general baggage (2024). — [ZATCA news 1238](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news_1238.aspx); [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/642856/SAUDI-ARABIA/ZATCA-sets-SR3000-maximum-duty-free-purchase-limit-for-incoming-passenger)
- **Cash and goods declaration:** declaration is mandatory via ZATCA's e-Declaration (website or app). The threshold for cash, jewellery and goods was reported as SAR 60,000. A later Wego piece reports SAR 40,000 for cash and gold; these reports conflict. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-passengers-required-to-declare-money-goods-worth-more-than-sr60000-1.90831519); [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/saudi-arabia-travellers-must-declare-cash-and-gold/)

### Inferences
- Several sealed units of the same product (for example 5 identical iPhones) would very likely be judged "commercial quantities." Duty and VAT would then be charged, and SFDA, SASO or CST conformity could be required, with possible seizure.

### Gaps
- There is no official ZATCA definition of "commercial quantity" for travellers, and no 2026 confirmation of the SAR 3,000 baggage limit.

## 5. Product compliance: SASO/SABER, CST, SFDA, GACA, batteries, perfumes

### Takeaway
Commercial imports of electronics need SABER certificates, and wireless approval has moved from CST into SABER. Cosmetics and perfumes need SFDA eCosma notification plus a CoC, and foreign manufacturers need a local representative. Drones cannot clear customs without GACA registration. Alcohol-based perfumes are flammable and widely restricted by couriers and forwarders. I found no clear exemption threshold for small resale parcels.

### Cited Findings
- **SABER:** certain items, including electronics, cosmetics and toys, may require a Certificate of Conformity or registration on the SABER platform. — [DutyDecoder](https://dutydecoder.com/calculate/us-to-saudi-arabia-electronics-import/)
- **Wireless approval:** it has moved under SASO. CST no longer issues wireless type approval certificates; SASO-approved certification bodies issue a SABER Product Certificate covering safety and wireless, and CST still sets the technical specifications. — [UL: Saudi Arabia wireless regulatory updates](https://www.ul.com/insights/saudi-arabia-wireless-regulatory-updates); [Eleos Compliance: CST to SABER](https://www.eleoscompliance.com/en/article/saudi-arabia-transitions-from-cst-platform-to-saber)
  - Some 2025-2026 freight guides still describe CST approval as a separate track. — [Carraglobe](https://carraglobe.com/import-it-equipment-saudi-arabia-without-local-entity/)
- **USB-C:** smartphones, tablets and laptops listed in Appendix 2 must have USB Type-C. — [UL](https://www.ul.com/insights/saudi-arabia-wireless-regulatory-updates)
- **SFDA cosmetics and perfumes:**
  - Perfumes are low-risk products notified via the SFDA eCosma portal.
  - Foreign manufacturers must work through a resident representative.
  - eCosma does not replace the CoC requirement for shipments. — [SGS SFDA datasheet](https://www.sgs.com/-/media/sgscorp/documents/corporate/brochures/sgs-gis-pca-saudi-arabia-sfda-datasheet-a4-en-20-v13.cdn.en.pdf); [DDReg Pharma](https://www.ddregpharma.com/global-reach/regulatory-services-in-saudi-arabia/cosmetic-products); [Bureau Veritas SFDA cosmetics datasheet 2024](https://cdn2-verigates.bureauveritas.com/sites/verigates/files/2024-09/SAUDI%20ARABIA%20-%20%28SFDA-COSMETICS%29%20Datasheet%20Rev%201.9.pdf)
  - SFDA launched the FASEH portal for cosmetics and perfumes clearance in October 2021. — [Intertek webinar](https://www.intertek.com/resources/webinars/2021/webinars-sfda-faseh-platform-for-cosmetics-perfumes/)
- **Drones:**
  - GACA's FAQ says customs clearance cannot be requested without an approved drone registration.
  - Drones of 250 g to 25 kg must be registered.
  - Confiscations have happened at entry points, and one DJI Mini was held in Dammam awaiting a GACA permit.
  - Foreigners may need a national ID or iqama to register. — [GACA UAS FAQ](https://uas.gaca.gov.sa/uas/faq); [Drone-traveller](https://drone-traveller.com/drone-laws-saudi-arabia); [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/1482511); [DJI forum](https://forum.dji.com/thread-192577-1-1.html)
- **Perfume shipping:** forwarders and couriers list flammable liquids (perfume, alcohol, nail polish) as restricted. — [Fuuffy](https://www.fuuffy.com/en/article/Popular%20Shipping%20Routes/182/hong-kong-to-saudi-arabia)

### Inferences
- Small DDP parcels for personal use routinely clear without SABER or SFDA paperwork in practice. Larger or repeated commercial-looking shipments of cosmetics, perfumes or electronics risk being held for conformity documents a reseller cannot get without a CR and SABER account.
- Lithium batteries: forwarders generally accept batteries installed in devices (UN3481) but restrict loose batteries. This was not sourced in this session.

### Gaps
- I found no official SFDA personal-use quantity limit, and no current SABER exemption for low-value e-commerce parcels.
- Lithium battery and alcohol-perfume air rules (IATA DG, and specific MyUS or Aramex policies) were not confirmed.

## 6. Legal requirements for reselling online in KSA

### Takeaway
Selling online requires a commercial registration or a freelance certificate. Stores register and verify via the Saudi Business Center's Business Platform, with Maroof as a trust layer. VAT registration is mandatory above SAR 375,000 in annual taxable supplies and voluntary above SAR 187,500.

### Cited Findings
- **Licensing:** online sellers must obtain a CR or, where legally applicable, a freelance certificate. Individuals can register with their national ID in certain activity categories. — [Watily Maroof 2026 guide](https://watily.com/blog/en/saudi-maroof-online-store-registration-2026/); [Expandway 2026](https://expandway.sa/saudi-e-commerce-registration/)
- **Store registration:** the Business Platform (Saudi Business Center) is now the body responsible for registering and verifying e-commerce stores. Maroof is described as an optional, free trust badge. These sources conflict on details. — [Middle East Commerce: Maroof verification 2026](https://middleeastcommerce.net/blog/maroof-store-verification); [Incorpmena](https://incorpmena.com/saudi-ecommerce-license)
- **CR fees:** quoted from SAR 200-500/yr to SAR 2,000-4,000; unverified. — [Aztech 2026](https://aztechtraining.com/articles/how-to-obtain-an-e-commerce-license-in-saudi-arabia)
- **VAT registration:** mandatory at SAR 375,000 in annual taxable turnover, voluntary at SAR 187,500. — [Skylight VAT e-commerce guide](https://skylightchat.com/en/resources/saudi-vat-ecommerce-guide)
- **E-commerce law penalties:** reported to start at SAR 10,000 and reach up to SAR 1 million; unverified against an official source. — [Businesslink](https://www.businesslink.sa/en/complete-guide-to-starting-online-business-in-saudi-arabia/)

### Inferences
- A reseller below SAR 375k turnover does not charge VAT but cannot reclaim the 15% import VAT, so import VAT is a full cost.
- Selling on Instagram or WhatsApp without a CR or freelance certificate breaches the E-Commerce Law.

### Gaps
- Whether the freelance certificate (freelance.sa) covers trading or reselling physical goods was not confirmed. My understanding is that it mainly covers services, so resale likely needs a CR; this needs verification.

## 7. Warranty, region and compatibility issues

### Takeaway
US iPhone 17 models are eSIM-only, but so are Saudi models, and Saudi carriers support eSIM, so this is low risk. The bigger risks are no local Apple warranty for US units (per community sources) and US Dyson 120V units, which Dyson says should not be used on 230V and which lose warranty abroad.

### Cited Findings
- **iPhone 17 eSIM:** the iPhone 17, 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max are eSIM-only in about 12 countries including the US and Saudi Arabia. — [MacRumors](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/iphone-17-and-iphone-17-pro-models-are-esim-only-in-these-countries.2464993); [Gulf News eSIM setup KSA](https://gulfnews.com/business/retail/how-to-set-up-esim-on-iphone-17-pro-air-and-pro-max-in-uae-saudi-arabia-1.500275509)
- **Carrier lock:** a US phone must be carrier-unlocked to work in KSA. — [Apple Community 256133222](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256133222)
- **Apple warranty:** community replies say US iPhone warranty is not honoured in Saudi Arabia. Apple's official policy was not checked. — [Apple Community 256133222](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256133222)
- **Local alternative:** Jarir's replacement warranty applies only to units bought at Jarir in KSA. — [Jarir](https://www.jarir.com/sa-en/jarir-iphone-offers)
- **Dyson voltage and warranty:**
  - Dyson representatives say US machines are designed solely for 110-120V and do not recommend converters, which shorten lifespan and void warranty.
  - Use outside the purchase country may void warranty. — [Best Buy Q&A, Dyson V11](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/questions/dyson-v11-torque-drive-cordless-vacuum-blue-nickel/6331929/question/606701cc-006e-339f-9c3e-bdb6a59ed253); [Best Buy Q&A, Dyson TP04](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/questions/dyson-pure-cool-purifying-fan-tp04-nickel-nickel/6192408/question/5e765bc4-3ceb-334a-962a-1162f9013af2); [Dyson Community](https://community.dyson.com/general-discussion-126/dyson-airwrap-how-to-use-120volt-in-euraisia-962)

### Inferences
- **Corded mains appliances** (Airwrap, Supersonic, Corrale, purifiers, kitchen appliances): avoid US 120V units for resale.
- **Cordless vacuums:** these are probably usable because their chargers are often universal 100-240V, but this needs checking per charger label, and they need a Type G plug adapter.
- **Laptops and phone chargers:** these are generally 100-240V. This is general knowledge, not sourced here.

### Gaps
- PS5 and Switch 2 region-locking, and US 120V-only consoles versus universal PSUs, were not researched in this session.
- I found no official Apple statement on iPhone warranty portability to KSA in 2026.
- Saudi's Type G 230V standard was not cited from a primary source in this session.
