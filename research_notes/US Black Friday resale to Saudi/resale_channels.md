## Selling channels: fees, requirements, buyer trust

### Takeaway
For a small, occasional US-to-Saudi reseller, the cheapest and lowest-barrier channels are classifieds (Haraj at about 1% commission paid on the honour system, OpenSooq with no sales commission) plus social and WhatsApp selling. Noon and Amazon.sa need a formal business identity: Noon KSA no longer accepts freelance certificates for new sellers. Amazon.sa charges referral fees of about 7–10.5%. TikTok Shop has not officially launched in Saudi Arabia. Anyone selling commercially online, social accounts included, is expected to hold a commercial registration (CR) or be listed on Maroof, and fines can reach SAR 1M.

### Cited Findings
**Haraj**
- Haraj's standard fee is 1% of every transaction made through the platform. Subscriptions change this, and when a subscription expires, fees go back to "regular membership (1%)". — [Haraj FAQ](https://haraj.com.sa/en/faq)
- The seller owes the fee when Haraj was instrumental in the sale. Fees under SAR 20 are to be donated to an official charity on Haraj's behalf rather than transferred. The only way to reduce fees is Haraj's "fee reduction system". After paying, sellers notify Haraj through the fee account, and payment shows within 24 hours. — [Haraj FAQ](https://haraj.com.sa/en/faq)

### Inferences
- For a one-off Black Friday batch, the practical stack is: Haraj and OpenSooq for reach, Instagram, Snapchat and WhatsApp for repeat buyers and pre-orders, and TikTok as an advertising channel only (no checkout). Noon and Amazon.sa are only worth it if the seller already has a CR or Data Certificate and VAT setup. Their referral fees (about 7–10.5%, before fulfilment costs) also eat into thin arbitrage margins.
- Haraj's 1% honour-system fee is a religious and ethical obligation many users take seriously. Budget for it.
- Buyer trust on classifieds and social channels rests on Maroof or CR listing, a visible US receipt or invoice, sealed packaging, and cash or transfer on hand-over. This is an inference; no direct source was found.

## Demand: fastest-selling categories, seasonality, 2026 trends

### Takeaway
Electronics, fashion, beauty and perfume, and home goods dominate Saudi online sales. White Friday (late November) is the biggest sales event and drives heavy local discounting. The next gifting peaks are Ramadan, expected to start around 8 February 2027, and Eid al-Fitr, around 9–10 March 2027. Perfume is a large premium-skewed market but carries a high counterfeit risk. No reliable 2026 data was found ranking specific items such as Switch 2, Stanley/Owala, Lululemon or supplements.

### Cited Findings
- White Friday discounts cover electronics, fashion and home furniture. Black Friday leans on tech. — [ClicFlyer](https://blog.clicflyer.com/en/black-friday-vs-white-friday-best-deals-and-coupon-in-ksa)
- The best-selling online categories in Saudi Arabia are electronics, clothing and fashion, and cosmetics and perfumes. — [Oto (tryoto) blog](https://www.tryoto.com/otopedia/blog/المنتجات-الأكثر-مبيع-ا-في-السعودية)
- One platform's leaderboard shows Fashion and Home Goods overtaking electronics, with furniture and household goods jumping to the top. The period and source were not confirmed. — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/?p=236665)
- MENA White Friday sales generated 40% more revenue year on year (per headline). — [Khaleej Times](https://khaleejtimes.com/business/mena-white-friday-sales-generate-40-more-revenue-this-year)
- Mobile commerce is forecast to exceed 70% of White Friday e-commerce sales in KSA and the UAE. This is a vendor forecast. — [Vinculum](https://www.vinculumgroup.com/?p=27618)
- 2025 timing: Amazon.sa White Friday ran 20–30 November, and Noon "Yellow Friday" ran roughly 23–29 November. US Black Friday 2026 falls on 27 November 2026. — [ClicFlyer](https://blog.clicflyer.com/?p=2897); [Egypt Telegraph](https://egypttelegraph.com/article/293652/)
- Ramadan 2027 is expected to start Monday 8 February 2027 (Umm al-Qura), with Eid al-Fitr on about 9 March, or 10 March if Ramadan runs 30 days. SAMA's Eid holiday is 7–11 March 2027. — [Saudi Life Guide](https://saudilifeguide.com/ramadan-2027-saudi-arabia-dates-eid-holidays/); [Jordan News](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-20/Middle-East/Date-of-the-First-Day-of-Ramadan-2027-According-to-Astronomical-Calculations-52316); [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/ramadan-2027/)
- Perfume: premium perfume holds 61% of the Saudi perfume market (2025). In the luxury segment, products above $200 hold 54%. Oriental and oud scents are favoured. Counterfeits are a "significant concern". — [IMARC perfume](https://www.imarcgroup.com/saudi-arabia-perfume-market); [IMARC luxury perfume](https://www.imarcgroup.com/saudi-arabia-luxury-perfume-market)
- Perfume market size estimates differ: about $1.95B (2025) versus $2.43B (2025) rising to $3.62B (2034). — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/saudi-arabia-perfume-market); [Renub via MarketResearch](https://www.marketresearch.com/Renub-Research-v3619/Saudi-Arabia-Perfume-Type-Value-44215997)
- Price gaps: the US has the lowest list price for the iPhone 18 Pro and Duo, ahead of the UAE and then Saudi Arabia. US list prices exclude sales tax. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/retail/us-uae-saudi-uk-or-india-where-iphone-duo-and-iphone-18-pro-are-cheapest-to-buy-1.500669754); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/tech/iphone-18-pro-duo-price-comparison-uae-us-saudi-arabia-india)
- AirPods Pro 3 lowest Saudi price was about SAR 781, and AirPods Max 2 about SAR 2,049, both including 15% VAT (snapshot). — [SaudiPCPrice](https://saudipcprice.com/en/apple/airpods)
- Apple sells directly in Saudi Arabia (Apple Store online), which narrows the grey-market gap on Apple products. — [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/apple_starts_selling_its_products_directly_in_saudi_arabia-news-68724.php)
- Forwarder MyUS publishes a list of what Saudi shoppers buy from the US (categories). — [MyUS blog](https://www.myus.com/blog/what-saudi-arabians-buy-from-the-usa/)

### Inferences
- White Friday overlaps US Black Friday, so local Saudi retailers (Noon, Amazon.sa, Extra, Jarir) cut prices on the same mainstream electronics in the same week. Items that ship from the US also land in December, after the local sale is over. The window to sell US-bought mainstream electronics is therefore weakest exactly when they arrive. Better targets are items not sold locally, sizes and colours not available locally, or limited releases.
- The best resale window for Black Friday stock is mid-December to early February: pre-Ramadan gifting, plus winter and camping season. Stock should be cleared before Eid (about 9 March 2027).
- Apple sells officially in KSA and the US–KSA price gap is small, so AirPods and iPhones offer thin arbitrage. They also carry warranty and region concerns (for example, US iPhones are eSIM-only).

### Gaps
- No 2026 Saudi data was found on Switch 2 pricing or availability, Stanley/Owala demand, Lululemon, Dyson price gaps, or supplements. No MCIT or Monsha'at category report was found.
- No measured Ramadan or Eid category-uplift data was found.

## Testimonials and case studies: margins, mistakes

### Takeaway
No credible, detailed first-hand US-to-Saudi resale testimonial with margins was found in Arabic or English: no Reddit, Haraj, YouTube or X posts with numbers. What exists is anecdotal (carry-on "suitcase trade" stories, shopper savings write-ups) and forwarder marketing. The documented pitfalls are structural: customs and VAT on the full landed value, the commercial-quantity and SABER trigger, counterfeit enforcement, retailer cancellations, and local White Friday price drops.

### Cited Findings
- "Suitcase trade" (تجارة الشنطة) is a known Saudi practice: goods mainly from Turkey, Lebanon and the US. Some practitioners started as hobbyists bringing Canadian and American goods on each trip and grew into store owners. — [Laha Magazine](https://www.lahamag.com/article/98101-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D9%86%D8%B7%D8%A9-%D9%8A%D8%AC%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B2%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA)
- Distinguish this from the illegal "black suitcase" trade (unlicensed products such as stimulants), which Okaz warns about. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/na/1569413)
- One Black Friday buyer found that forwarding from the US was "not as straightforward as we initially thought". They saved only about $200 in total, and only by buying a dozen heavily discounted items. — [Medium](https://medium.com/@wiradikusuma/buying-from-the-united-states-and-shipping-it-to-your-country-3091dc8d5f0) (not Saudi-specific)
- Customs: the duty exemption applies only when total value is under SAR 1,000 including shipping and insurance, and no clearance permission is needed. VAT (15%) still applies, charged on the full customs value including duty. — [ZATCA e-stores customers](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs_individual/e-stores-customers.aspx); [ZATCA FAQ 325](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/FAQs/Pages/FAQ_325.aspx); [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/611390/SAUDI-ARABIA/Customs-No-duty-for-shipments-valued-below-SR1000)
- A Saudi guide says a SAR 15 fee is charged per e-store customs declaration at or under SAR 1,000. The SAR 3,000 limit applies to travellers' luggage and gifts, not to parcels. — [Saudi Export Pro](https://saudiexportpro.com/رسوم-الجمارك-على-الشحنات-الشخصية/); [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/thehour/are-personal-shipments-subject-to-customs-duties-customs-clarifies); [Siasat (SR 3k travellers)](https://www.siasat.com/saudi-arabia-travelers-need-not-pay-tax-worth-less-than-sr-3k-2112091/)
- Conflict: a US forwarder says the SAR 1,000 threshold excludes shipping. ZATCA says it includes shipping, so ZATCA governs. — [Comgateway](https://www.comgateway.com/sv/blogs/smart-ways-to-lower-your-customs-bill-when-shipping-from-the-usa-to-saudi-arabia/)
- Commercial-quantity risk: large quantities of the same item may be flagged as a commercial import needing a Certificate of Conformity. Saudi customs also requires a tax ID or national ID on shipments, otherwise they may be held or returned. — [Planet Express](https://planetexpress.com/?p=4618); [Stackry](https://www.stackry.com/duties-and-taxes/saudi-arabia)
- SABER: customs classifies imports by quantity and by whether the importer is a company with a trading activity number. Personal imports reportedly need only a shipment certificate. Commercial imports need a Product CoC (1 year) and a Shipment CoC per batch, obtained before arrival. Each electronics model must be listed on the PCoC, and mismatched model numbers are a common reason for rejection. — [Al Tamimi](https://turtl.tamimi.com/story/law-update-issue-376-transport-and-insurance-focus/page/11); [HQTS](https://www.hqts.com/saber-certificate-guide/); [Crane Worldwide](https://www.craneww.com/knowledge-center/latest-news-and-info/saber-saudi-arabia)
- Counterfeit enforcement: the Ministry of Commerce closed more than 75 social accounts for fake-goods ads. — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/1067061/amp)
- Car-shipping example: shipping a car from the US to KSA costs about SAR 10,500–22,000. Bulky goods quickly erase margin. — [Saudi Export Pro (cars)](https://saudiexportpro.com/%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%83%D8%A7-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9/)
- Shop and Ship (Aramex) shipping was about SAR 90/kg excluding VAT, per an older forum post (unofficial). — [Expat.com forum](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/middle-east/saudi-arabia/riyadh/365135-shopandship-aramex-vs-url.html)

### Inferences
- Rough landed-cost formula: US price + US sales tax (0% if shipped to a tax-free forwarder state such as Delaware or Oregon) + forwarder fee (often weight or volumetric based) + 15% VAT on goods plus shipping + 5–15% duty if the total is at or above SAR 1,000 + SAR 15 declaration fee + selling-channel fee (1% on Haraj, about 7–10.5% on Amazon.sa) + local delivery. The 15% VAT alone wipes out most Black Friday discounts of under 25–30% on items also sold locally.
- Splitting orders to stay under SAR 1,000 avoids duty but not VAT. Repeated identical items can trigger commercial classification and SABER requirements. Volume resellers should formalise with a CR and import properly.
- The common mistakes are inferred from the structural evidence above, not from testimonials: buying items Saudi retailers discount in the same week, under-budgeting VAT and volumetric shipping, selling perfume or sneakers without proof of authenticity, and stock arriving after the gifting peak.

### Gaps
- No Reddit (r/saudiarabia, r/Flipping), Haraj, X or YouTube testimonials with real margins surfaced in search. The Arabic queries ("الشراء من امريكا وبيعه في السعودية", "تجارة الشنطة") returned forwarder and marketing pages only. This could not be closed in the tool budget. A manual Reddit, X and YouTube search is recommended.

## Pre-order / presell strategy

### Takeaway
No source documented a Saudi pre-order or deposit practice for US goods. The legal frame applies: e-commerce and consumer-protection rules apply to anyone selling online, and taking deposits for goods not yet bought is commercial activity that falls under the CR/Maroof expectation.

### Cited Findings
- E-commerce sellers, social media sellers included, are expected to hold a CR or Maroof registration. Sanctions reach SAR 1M fines and store blocking. — [Ministry of Commerce](https://mc.gov.sa/en/mediacenter/News/Pages/12-06-17-01.aspx); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/retail/saudi-arabia-tightens-up-ecommerce-law-with-stiff-penalties-1.73327592)
- Travellers' allowance: luggage and gifts worth under SAR 3,000 are duty-free. This is relevant to the "friend travelling from the US" route, but commercial quantities in luggage are a separate matter. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/saudi-arabia-travelers-need-not-pay-tax-worth-less-than-sr-3k-2112091/); [Saudi Export Pro](https://saudiexportpro.com/رسوم-الجمارك-على-الشحنات-الشخصية/)

### Inferences
- A pre-sell can remove inventory risk: post US Black Friday prices with a fixed SAR landed price and an ETA, take a 20–50% deposit by bank transfer, and buy only what is reserved. Lead time should be stated honestly (about 2–4 weeks via forwarder). Order cancellations (see below) mean a refund policy must be stated up front.
- Using a friend's luggage keeps value under the SAR 3,000 traveller allowance and avoids forwarder fees. Several identical sealed units in one bag may be treated as commercial at customs.

### Gaps
- No sourced testimonials on deposit practices, default rates or customer disputes. No source was found on the e-commerce law's specific rules on advance payment and delivery time limits (the origami.sa compliance guide was blocked).

## Payment issues buying from US retailers

### Takeaway
Major US retailers, Best Buy and Apple included, often reject or cancel orders paid with foreign cards or shipped to known freight-forwarder addresses, according to user reports and forwarder blogs. The usual workarounds are forwarder "buy for me" or assisted-purchase services that pay with a US card, a US gift card or PayPal balance, or retailers that accept international cards. Per-customer purchase limits on doorbusters cap volume either way.

### Cited Findings
- Reddit users report Best Buy declining international billing addresses or cards, and rejecting freight-forwarder addresses. This is anecdotal, and no official Best Buy policy was found. — [Big Apple Buddy](https://bigapplebuddy.com/blog/best-buy-international-shipping)
- Many US stores automatically decline international credit cards or orders that use a known forwarder address. Forwarders offer assisted purchasing with a domestic US card. — [Comgateway](https://www.comgateway.com/blogs/i-nearly-lost-thousands-on-a-google-pixel-9-pro-fold-because-i-didn-t-use-a-us-freight-forwarder); [ForwardMe help: cancelled by retailer](https://help.forwardme.com/en/article/5-canceled-order-by-retailer)
- An Apple cancellation notice (user-reported) says Apple does not ship to freight-forwarder addresses. Refunds post in about 5 business days. — [Apple Community](https://discussions-cn-prz.apple.com/thread/254991858)
- Aramex Shop and Ship FAQ: merchants may not ship if card authorisation fails, and some do not accept international cards or non-US billing addresses. The customer is responsible for all duties. — [Aramex](https://www.aramex.com/sa/en); [Expat.com forum](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/middle-east/saudi-arabia/806210-shop-ship-aramex.html)
- eBay sellers notice Saudi buyers using US forwarder addresses and US PayPal to get around country blocks, which causes friction with sellers who don't ship internationally. — [eBay Community](https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/How-did-someone-from-Saudi-Arabia-get-around-my-country-blocks/m-p/27016875/highlight/true)
- Shipito, Global Shopaholics and Ubuy offer US addresses or buy-and-ship to KSA. Global Shopaholics advises checking Saudi customs restrictions before buying. — [Shipito](https://www.shipito.com/ar/buy-from-US-ship-to-Saudi-Arabia); [Global Shopaholics](https://globalshopaholics.com/us-forwarding-service/saudi-arabia/); [Ubuy](https://www.ubuy.com.sa/ar/shop-import-products-from-usa)

### Inferences
- Budget for cancellations on Best Buy, Walmart and Apple Black Friday doorbusters. Prefer Amazon.com (ships internationally), retailers known to accept foreign cards, assisted-purchase services (fees usually a few percent), or US gift cards bought in advance. Using assisted purchase to get around a retailer's terms is a risk.
- Doorbuster quantity limits per customer, plus fraud flags on bulk orders to forwarder addresses, push resellers towards several small orders. That increases forwarder fees and the risk of commercial classification at customs.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 sources were found on Walmart's foreign-card policy, specific Black Friday per-customer limits, or Saudi bank cards (mada vs. Visa/Mastercard credit) being declined on US sites. User reports on Reddit are needed.
