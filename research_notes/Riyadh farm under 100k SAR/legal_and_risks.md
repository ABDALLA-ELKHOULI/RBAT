# Legal, Regulatory and Due-Diligence Requirements for Buying Agricultural Land / a Farm near Riyadh (budget ~100,000 SAR)

Research date: 2026-11-17. Method: web searches (Arabic and English). Two official hosts (mewa.gov.sa, argaam.com) could not be fetched from this session (network allowlist), so some official texts were seen only as search-result summaries; those items are flagged. Items not backed by a URL are in Inferences or Gaps, not in Cited Findings.

## 1. Deed types (electronic sakk, paper deeds, hujjat istihkam, wad' yad, agricultural grants, shbook), verification and transfer (Najiz / Real Estate Exchange), Real Estate Registry status in Riyadh

### Takeaway
Buy only land that has a current electronic title deed (صك إلكتروني) in the seller's name. Check it on MoJ/Najiz, and transfer it electronically with the price paid through the escrow ("intermediary") bank account. Avoid land held only by possession (وضع يد), fencing (شبوك), an unfinished حجة استحكام, or a "share" (شريحة/حصة مشاعة) in a larger farm. Ehkam only recognises possession-based claims where the land was revived before 9/11/1387H. In Riyadh-region districts announced for the Real Estate Registry (السجل العقاري), the deed is converted into a registry title (رقم عقار + صك تسجيل ملكية).

### Cited Findings
- **Electronic transfer steps (Najiz, as reported in 2021).** The seller picks "electronic property transfer", adds a request, selects the property, previews the deed, enters the deal data, checks that Real Estate Transaction Tax was paid, then approves. The buyer accepts, receives notice that an intermediary bank account has been issued, and transfers the price into it. — [Slaati 2021](https://slaati.com/2021/06/07/p1864631.html) (older source; the flow may have changed)
- **The service also checks bank-account ownership and RETT payment** before the transfer goes through. — [Slaati 2021](https://slaati.com/2021/06/07/p1864631.html)
- **Transfers now start on the Real Estate Exchange.** A law-firm blog says that, per the current MoJ service page, ownership transfer now begins on the Real Estate Exchange platform (البورصة العقارية) rather than the old Najiz transfer path. — [HD Law Firm blog](https://hd-lawfirm.com.sa/blog/محامي-إفراغ-عقاري-إلكتروني/). MoJ has published guidelines for the Real Estate Exchange platform — [MoJ PDF](https://www.moj.gov.sa/Documents/Sharing/GuidelinesForRealEstateStockExchangePlatform.pdf). (This is an unofficial blog; check on moj.gov.sa / srem.moj.gov.sa.)
- **Agricultural-land transfer requirements.** An MoJ document lists, for agricultural property, the approval of the Agricultural Development Fund, plus the original electronic deed. If only part of the property is transferred, a subdivision document (مستند التجزئة) is also required. — MoJ documents surfaced in search: [moj.gov.sa PDF 70](https://www.moj.gov.sa/Documents/Regulations/pdf/70.pdf) / [Real Estate Exchange guidelines](https://www.moj.gov.sa/Documents/Sharing/GuidelinesForRealEstateStockExchangePlatform.pdf). I saw this only as a search summary and could not open the PDF to confirm which document it is or the exact wording. The ADF approval is most likely about land that carries an ADF loan or lien.
- **Deed check on Najiz.** Najiz offers the services "اطلاع على عقاراتي" (view my properties) and "التحقق من بيانات الصك العقاري" (verify title-deed data). — reported in search summary of [amlak.net.sa](https://amlak.net.sa/?p=29629) / [afifnp.com](https://afifnp.com/?p=421171)
- **Ehkam (State Properties General Authority) and possession claims.** Ehkam's committees only consider ownership claims based on revival (إحياء) that took place **before the prohibition order of 9/11/1387H**, by the applicant, their predecessor or a successor in title. They do not consider revival after that date. — [Qanoniah: rules of the land-ownership committees](https://qanoniah.com/File/wkxV01WJv87ER1dn9r2jqQ3lp-قواعد-وآلية-عمل-لجان-النظر-في-طلبات-تملك-العقارات); [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2052155)
- **Ehkam uses neighbours' deeds as evidence** and requires revival before the prohibition order. — [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1188761)
- **Converting an agricultural deed to residential** is outside Ehkam's jurisdiction. — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/FL-6fCB)
- **Ehkam filing windows.** The window for filing ownership claims was extended several times (e.g., a one-year extension in 2022). The Shura Council also discussed reopening it for people who missed it. — [Slaati 2022](https://slaati.com/2022/11/13/p2158199.html); [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/Fo9_uTX)
- **Undivided-share risk.** Buying a "farm plot" (شريحة زراعية) may mean buying only an undivided share, e.g. 1% of a 100,000 m² parcel, with no independent title to a defined piece. If transfer is never completed, the land can become a source of disputes, especially after a party dies and there are multiple heirs. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2118192); [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/32475)
- **Real Estate Registry in Riyadh region, 2025–2026.** REGA (which now runs the registry) announced rolling phases of first-time in-rem registration:
  - 166 Riyadh-region districts, 7 Sep – 11 Dec 2025 — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/slmTo_-)
  - 10,052 plots in 56 districts from 12 Oct 2025 — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2213268)
  - 111 districts in Riyadh and the Eastern Province — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/gBhwXfV)
  - 5,637 plots in 18 districts of Al-Rain governorate, 23 Nov 2025 – 26 Feb 2026 — [Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/aswaq/realestate/2025/11/12/العقار-السعودية-تعلن-بدء-اعمال-السجل-العقاري-لـ-18-حيا-بمنطقة-الرياض)
  - Districts in Dhurma governorate from 1 Feb 2026 — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/saudi-arabia/2227956)
  - A 90-day grace period for districts whose window had closed — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/bZLwc2O)
- **Registry rules.** Registration requires a title deed that meets the legal requirements. Each registered unit gets a property number (رقم عقار) and a registration title (صك تسجيل ملكية). Owners who do not register within the announced period face fines. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2221856); [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1170959)

### Inferences
- **Deed safety ranking for a small buyer:**
  1. Real Estate Registry title (صك تسجيل ملكية), or an electronic MoJ deed that verifies on Najiz in the seller's name, with no restrictions noted: safe.
  2. An old paper or handwritten deed: usable only after the seller updates it to an electronic deed (تحديث/إلكترونية الصك) at MoJ before the sale. Do not pay until that is done.
  3. حجة استحكام still in progress, or a claim before Ehkam: not a title. The outcome is uncertain, and post-1387H revival is not recognised.
  4. وضع يد / شبوك / "منح" letters with no final deed: no legal title. The buyer pays for nothing transferable, and the land may be removed as an encroachment on state land (see section 5).
- **Agricultural grants (منح زراعية / أراضي البور).** These were historically distributed with conditions to cultivate before a final deed was issued. A grant without a final deed is not freely transferable. *(I found no 2025–26 source on current grant rules; see Gaps.)*
- **Shbook.** Fenced desert plots (شبوك) offered "cheap" around Riyadh are typically unregistered state land. At a ~100k SAR budget, many listings on Haraj will be of this kind or undivided shares.
- **Real Estate Exchange.** The Exchange has replaced the old Najiz path as the transfer channel, so the buyer should insist the transfer happens there, with payment through the system's escrow. Never pay cash or a deposit (عربون) before the transfer.

### Gaps
- I could not open the official MoJ/Najiz service pages to confirm the 2026 step-by-step flow for agricultural land on the Real Estate Exchange, or whether ADF approval is needed only for land with an ADF lien.
- I found no current (2025–26) official text on transferring agricultural grant land (منح زراعي) before a final deed, or on the minimum cultivation period.
- I could not confirm whether the Real Estate Registry phases cover outlying agricultural areas around Riyadh (e.g., Al-Kharj, Muzahmiyah, Huraymila, Thadiq), beyond Al-Rain and Dhurma.
- I could not confirm whether Ehkam's filing window is open as of late 2026.

## 2. Taxes and fees: 5% RETT on agricultural land; brokerage cap (2.5%); REGA broker licence (FAL) and ad licence on Aqar/Haraj

### Takeaway
A 100,000 SAR farm purchase normally carries 5% RETT (5,000 SAR). RETT is legally due from the seller, but deals often shift it to the buyer by contract. Brokerage is 2.5% of the deal value (2,500 SAR) unless agreed otherwise in writing. Every online listing must carry a REGA ad licence, issued through the FAL platform on the basis of a brokerage contract the owner approves by SMS. A listing with no ad licence number is a red flag.

### Cited Findings
- **RETT rate.** ZATCA's simplified RETT guide includes a table showing the sale of residential, commercial or **agricultural** land at 5% RETT. ZATCA's VAT real-estate guideline also lists agricultural land among real estate subject to RETT. — [ZATCA simplified RETT guide](https://zatca.gov.sa:443/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Simplified%20guide%20to%20the%20real%20estate%20transaction%20tax.pdf); [ZATCA VAT real estate guideline](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-For-the-Real-Estate-Sector-under-VAT-Provision.pdf)
- **RETT exemptions.** ZATCA's detailed RETT guideline (v5, May 2024) has an exemptions section. — [ZATCA detailed RETT guideline](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Detailed-Guideline-for-RETT-In-accordance-with-provision-of-RETT-Law-and-its-Implementing-Regulations.pdf)
- **New 2025 implementing regulations.** New RETT Implementing Regulations were approved in 2025 and kept most prior exemptions. — [PwC 2025](https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/services/tax/me-tax-legal-news/2025/saudi-arabia-new-rett-implementing-regulations.html); [KPMG draft consultation, Feb 2025](https://kpmg.com/sa/en/insights/tax-insights/tax-alert-draft-rett-implementing-regulations-for-public-consultation.html)
- **RETT checked at transfer.** The electronic transfer flow includes a step that verifies RETT payment. — [Slaati](https://slaati.com/2021/06/07/p1864631.html); [ZATCA real estate transaction platform guide](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/eServices/Documents/Real%20estate%20transaction%20platform%20guide%20EN.pdf)
- **Brokerage commission.** On a sale, commission is 2.5% of the transaction value unless otherwise agreed in writing. On a lease, it is 2.5% of the first year's rent. — [REGA brokerage report PDF](https://rega.gov.sa/media/wkfdtv3y/تقرير-الوساطة-العقارية.pdf); [Raghdan blog (commercial)](https://raghdan.sa/ar/news/real-estate-brokerage-contract-licensing-guide-2025/); [Sabq (rental)](https://sabq.org/saudia/7ljg9ftux2). Another source says total commission may not exceed 2.5% of contract value. — [amlak.net.sa](https://amlak.net.sa/en/19246/). *Sources differ on whether 2.5% is a hard cap or a default.*
- **Ad licence.** A real-estate ad requires a licence, issued on the FAL platform (REGA) through an electronically documented brokerage contract between owner and broker. The owner approves it by SMS. — [Raghdan](https://raghdan.sa/ar/news/real-estate-brokerage-contract-licensing-guide-2025/)
- **Licensing of individuals.** Individuals need a licence to practise brokerage and must pass a qualifying programme for each activity. — [REGA brokerage report](https://rega.gov.sa/media/wkfdtv3y/تقرير-الوساطة-العقارية.pdf)
- **Enforcement.** REGA started compliance monitoring of the Brokerage Law, including the legality of real-estate ad signs. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/economy/saudi/2206531)
- **Fee for non-Saudis.** Non-Saudis pay a separate 2% fee on the value of their dispositions in real-estate rights in Riyadh, Makkah, Madinah and Jeddah under the 2026 foreign-ownership regulations. — [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://aawsat.com/الاقتصاد/5287566-السعودية-تقر-اللائحة-التنفيذية-لتملك-غير-السعوديين-للعقار)

### Inferences
- **Cost budget for a 100,000 SAR deal:**
  - RETT: 5,000 SAR
  - Brokerage: up to 2,500 SAR, plus 15% VAT on the broker's fee if the broker is VAT-registered (from general VAT rules; not specifically sourced here)
  - Survey and other fees: a few hundred SAR
  - Plan for roughly 7,500–8,500 SAR on top of the price, or a price of about 92k if the total must stay within 100k.
- **Who pays RETT.** RETT is legally the seller's liability. A buyer should agree in the written contract who pays it, and check on the platform that it has been paid before transfer.
- **First-home exemption.** The first-home RETT exemption (Ministry of Housing–funded, up to 1M SAR) applies to residential homes. It does not appear to apply to agricultural land (from general knowledge; not verified in this session).

### Gaps
- I could not open the current ZATCA exemptions list to confirm that no agricultural-specific exemption exists (e.g., transfers between relatives or inheritance are exempt, but those do not help an arm's-length buyer).
- I found no exact fine amounts for an unlicensed broker, an ad without a licence, or charging more than 2.5%.
- I could not verify how Haraj/Aqar enforce the ad-licence number in 2026.

## 3. Wells, water and power: MEWA well licences, restrictions on new wells near Riyadh, meters, groundwater depletion, the fodder ban, and farm electricity

### Takeaway
Do not assume a farm's well is licensed or that a new one can be drilled. Since 2025, MEWA allows new wells on non-renewable aquifers (the sedimentary shelf, which covers Riyadh) only for limited-consumption specialised projects, with at most 2 wells per project. Heavily depleted aquifers are excluded, including the Saq aquifer near Riyadh and the Minjur/Wajid/Tawil aquifers. Green fodder has been banned since Nov 2018. A halt on perennial fodder (e.g. alfalfa) is reported from 16 Nov 2026. A cheap farm whose value rests on growing alfalfa or drilling a new well is high-risk.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 well rules.** In July 2025, MEWA published a draft and then a decision setting conditions for new well licences on non-renewable groundwater (sedimentary shelf), for specialised agricultural and livestock projects with limited consumption (poultry, greenhouses, hydroponic/vertical farming). The conditions are:
  - The project must already be at least 50% built (of its main components).
  - No more than 2 licensed wells per project.
  - Aquifers with sharp water-level decline are excluded, including Minjur, Wajid, Tawil and the **Saq aquifer near Riyadh**, Qassim, Hail and Tabuk.
  - The land must be owned under a valid deed or held on a long-term lease.
  - Sources: [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2198855); [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/9Rmp6c/); [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/YZ7ypq/). Seen via search summary; the full decision text was not opened.
- **Well-licence application.** MEWA's e-service lets individuals, companies and government bodies apply for a licence to drill a tube or hand-dug well. Applicants specify depth and the water quantity requested, upload the land-ownership document and pin the location. For agricultural use the required documents are: national ID (individuals) or commercial registration (companies), a copy of the ownership document, and a location sketch. — [MEWA well-licence renewal service PDF](https://mewa.gov.sa/ar/eServices/SiteAssets/Pages/Service024/12%20-%20تجديد%20رخصة%20حفر%20بئر-converted.pdf); [MEWA well-licensing conditions PDF](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/InformationCenter/DocsCenter/RulesLibrary/Documents/WelLicnCndtions.pdf); [Qanoniah: conditions for drilling and using groundwater wells](https://qanoniah.com/File/jw5DbJMQVKLzb46LvdkYq14Wy-شروط-وضوابط-حفر-واستخدام-آبار-المياه-الجوفية)
- **Unlicensed wells are removed.** Government encroachment-removal actions have taken out wells, e.g. in Qassim, two artesian wells and a fence on ~600,000 m² of government land. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/1501971)
- **Green fodder ban.** Cabinet Decision No. 66 (Oct 2016) set a 3-year phase-out of green fodder. The ban took effect in Nov 2018 (sources say 3 or 5 Nov). It was expected to save about 9 bn m³ of water a year out of ~17 bn m³ used by green fodder. — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/568056); [Netherlands agro-attaché](https://www.agroberichtenbuitenland.nl/actueel/nieuws/2018/12/03/ksa-fodder-ban); [MEWA news 17-9-2018](https://mewa.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News-17-9-2018.aspx)
- **Fodder fines.** MEWA announced a fine of 4,000 SAR per hectare for farms violating the green-fodder ban (2019). — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/612910)
- **Implementing rules.** Implementing mechanisms exist for halting green fodder and for wheat/seasonal forage cultivation. These tie the rules to aquifer water-level reports by area. — [Qanoniah: mechanism for halting green fodder](https://qanoniah.com/en/File/Jy4DGw9da7vj1vg78Qv65RrKX-Mechanism-for-Implementing-Regulations-on-Halting-the-Cultivation-of-Green-Fodder); [Qanoniah: wheat and seasonal forage](https://qanoniah.com/en/File/dbQ9aO8q3oy950yoX521MvYpe-Regulations-for-the-Cultivation-of-Wheat-and-Seasonal-Forage)
- **Perennial fodder halt (single source).** Saudi Arabia will halt cultivation of perennial fodder crops and shift to seasonal varieties from **16 Nov 2026**. — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1944977) (seen only via search result; page could not be fetched). Related: [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/na/2232883); [Arab News: research backs shift to seasonal forage](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2630925/saudi-arabia)
- **New agricultural power tariff from 1 Jan 2027.** Announced Oct 2026: 19 halalas/kWh up to 6,000 kWh per month and 23 halalas above. Monthly fixed charges are 140–200 SAR depending on breaker capacity, excluding VAT. — [Al-Eqtisadiah](https://www.aleqt.com/الأخبار/21-هللة-للكهرباء-السكنية-و9-ريالات-للمياه-في-السعودية-من-أول-يناير-15729). An undated comparison site lists 16/20 halalas as the earlier agricultural rate (unverified). — [hijri-calendars.com](https://hijri-calendars.com/electricity-tariff-comparison.php)
- **Grid-connection e-service.** MEWA launched an electronic service for the agricultural sector to connect to the electricity grid. — [Asharq Al-Awsat English](https://english.aawsat.com/business/5056149-saudi-environment-ministry-launches-electronic-service-agricultural-sector-connect?page=3)

### Inferences
- **Well licence goes with the land.** The buyer should get the well licence (رخصة البئر) and confirm it covers the farm on the deed. A well with no licence can be capped or removed, and a farm without water around Riyadh has low agricultural value.
- **Electricity.** Getting a farm grid connection appears to need MEWA confirmation of agricultural status, i.e. a valid deed and agricultural classification. A plot without an existing meter may face long and costly connection. Check whether an SEC (Saudi Electricity / Saudi Energy Company) meter exists in the seller's name, and whether the bills are paid.
- **Small-farm use.** At ~100k SAR, realistic legal uses are a small family farm (palms, vegetables, greenhouse) or a rest area (استراحة) on land zoned for it. Alfalfa or large fodder is not a realistic use.

### Gaps

- I could not open the July 2025 MEWA decision's full text or confirm whether it changes rules for existing licensed wells on transfer of ownership.
- I could not get the official SEC/SERA page for the 2026 agricultural tariff or the connection cost and timeline for remote farms.
- I found no specific groundwater-depletion figures for Riyadh-area aquifers.

## 4. Use restrictions: non-agricultural use, building on farmland, subdividing farmland, foreigners/expats

### Takeaway
Agricultural land cannot be legally subdivided and sold as "plots" (شرائح / مخططات) without MEWA approval and a municipal (MOMRAH) subdivision approval under Cabinet Decision 119 (1437H). Many sub-100k "farm plots" are illegal subdivisions or undivided shares. Converting a farm to residential is a separate municipal process; Ehkam does not handle it. The 2026 Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law took effect in January 2026. I found no evidence that it opens agricultural land around Riyadh to expatriates, so a non-Saudi buyer should assume agricultural land is not available to them.

### Cited Findings
- **Subdivision warning.** MEWA warned companies, real-estate offices and individuals against selling subdivision plans on agricultural land without MEWA approval, or without subdivision approval from the Ministry of Municipal, Rural Affairs and Housing. MEWA said this violates the subdivision controls approved by Cabinet Decision No. 119. — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/2ssp8t); [MEWA news (Arabic)](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News10872020.aspx); [amlak.net.sa English](https://amlak.net.sa/en/31798/)
- **Date of Decision 119.** Sources give 19/4/1437H — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/2ssp8t) — or 15/4/1437H — [Al-Madina](https://www.al-madina.com/ampArticle/484901).
- **Heirs' exception.** Special controls exist for subdividing agricultural land among heirs' shares, linked to the sedimentary-shelf (non-renewable water) area. — [Al-Madina](https://www.al-madina.com/ampArticle/484901)
- **Further MEWA notices** on agricultural land use and subdivision: [MEWA news 18/12/1442](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News2922020.aspx); [MEWA news 14/04/1443](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News3352020.aspx)
- **Agricultural to residential.** Converting an agricultural deed to residential is outside Ehkam's jurisdiction. — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/FL-6fCB)
- **Foreign ownership law in force.** REGA announced the Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law in force from 3 Sha'ban 1447H / 22 Jan 2026. — [REGA](https://rega.gov.sa/media-center/الأخبار-والإعلانات/هيئة-العقار-تعلن-بدء-نفاذ-نظام-تمل-ك-غير-السعوديين-للعقار-اعتبار-ا-من-اليوم/). Another outlet cites 1 Jan 2026. — [Eye of Riyadh](https://www.eyeofriyadh.com/ar/news/details/updated-law-on-non-saudis-property-ownership-comes-into-effect-today)
- **Foreign ownership zones.** Ownership is limited to designated geographic zones. In Makkah and Madinah it is limited to Saudi companies and Muslim individuals. The geographic scope and framework were approved in 2026, and applications go through the "Saudi Real Estate" (عقارات السعودية) portal. — [JLL](https://www.jll.com/ar-sa/insights/saudi-arabia-foreign-ownership-legislation-goes-live); [Al Arabiya, Riyadh and Jeddah zones](https://www.alarabiya.net/saudi-today/2025/07/25/%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%B1%D8%B2%D9%87%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B6-%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B7%D9%82-%D8%AA%D9%85%D9%84%D9%83-%D8%BA%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1-); [Al Arabiya, conditions from 2026](https://www.alarabiya.net/aswaq/realestate/2025/12/06/شروط-تملك-الاجانب-للعقار-في-السعودية-اعتبارا-من-مطلع-2026)
- **2% fee for non-Saudis** in Riyadh, Makkah, Madinah and Jeddah. — [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://aawsat.com/الاقتصاد/5287566-السعودية-تقر-اللائحة-التنفيذية-لتملك-غير-السعوديين-للعقار)

### Inferences
- **No legal "farm plot" market under ~100k.** A 100k budget near Riyadh rarely buys a full, independently titled farm. Offers of "شريحة زراعية" at this price are most likely undivided shares or unapproved subdivisions. These cannot get their own deed, may not get a well licence (which needs a deed or long lease), and cannot legally be built on as residences.
- **Building.** Building houses or rest areas (استراحات) on agricultural land without a municipal building permit risks removal. Agricultural buildings (stores, staff housing) generally need municipal and MEWA permits. *(Not confirmed with a 2025–26 source.)*
- **Expatriates.** Agricultural land is generally treated as reserved for Saudis and outside the urban designated zones, so expatriates should not buy it through a Saudi nominee (تستر). That carries legal risk for both parties. *(Inference; see Gaps.)*

### Gaps
- I found no source stating the minimum farm area after subdivision under Decision 119 (figures circulate informally but were not found in a citable source).
- I could not confirm from the law's text or the 2026 zone map whether agricultural land is explicitly excluded from non-Saudi ownership.
- I found no current MOMRAH rules on permitted structures on farmland (building-coverage ratio, rest areas).

## 5. Common scams and disputes: fake deeds, encroachment removals, planning/road reservations, unpaid dues; 2024–2026 news on encroachments and shbook around Riyadh

### Takeaway
The biggest risk near Riyadh is paying for fenced or "possessed" state land, or an illegal subdivision, that the Riyadh Emirate's encroachment committees later clear. They report clearing tens of millions of m² per quarter. Nazaha has exposed land falsely marked as "private property". Also check whether the land lies in an RCRC planning-suspended area or a road reservation. Land and planning rules in Riyadh changed sharply in 2025.

### Inferences
- **Typical scam patterns at the ~100k price point near Riyadh:**
  1. Fenced desert land (شبوك) or "وضع يد" sold with a handwritten sale paper (ورقة مبايعة) or a "منحة" letter. It is not a deed, and the land is liable to removal.
  2. "Farm plots" in an unapproved subdivision, or an undivided share, marketed as a farm.
  3. Old or paper deeds that are cancelled, superseded, disputed, or that overlap other deeds. Check via Najiz and by updating to an electronic deed.
  4. A seller who is not the deed-holder (agent with an expired or fake power of attorney). Verify the وكالة on Najiz.
  5. Requests for a cash deposit (عربون) before electronic transfer.
  6. Land in RCRC suspension zones, road or utility reservations, or valleys (wadis / flood channels), where building or permits are refused.
  7. Farms with unlicensed wells, unpaid electricity bills, or ADF loans/liens.
  8. Listings with no REGA ad licence number.
- **Checks before paying:**
  - Ask the municipality (Balady, or the Riyadh Municipality / governorate municipality) for the plot's planning status.
  - Ask MEWA's branch about the land's agricultural status and well licence.
  - Ask the Emirate or the municipality whether any removal decision exists.
