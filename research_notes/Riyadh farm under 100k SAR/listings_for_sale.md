## 1. Which areas within ~150 km of Riyadh have farms or agricultural plots listed around 100k SAR?

### Takeaway
None of the ~18 searches turned up a confirmed, live listing near Riyadh for a farm or agricultural plot priced at 50k–150k SAR. The cheapest farm-classified listings near Riyadh that I saw start at about 400k–700k SAR for 2,000–10,000 m² (Durma/Al-Aridhiyah, Al-Muzahmiyah, Al-Hayathem in Al-Kharj). ### Inferences
- In late 2026, about 100k SAR does not buy a titled farm with a well within ~100 km of Riyadh, judging by the index snippets. Close-in farmland (Al-Muzahmiyah, Durma, Al-Hair, Uraidh, Al-Kharj/Al-Hayathem) runs about 60–325 SAR/m². At those rates 100k SAR equals roughly 300–1,700 m², smaller than any agricultural subdivision plot I saw listed (the smallest were ~2,000 m²).
- Low per-m² prices (1–3 SAR/m²) exist only for very large remote parcels (Shaqra, ~190 km), and none were being split into 100k lots.
- Realistic routes to ~100k SAR are (a) a share or partition of a large remote parcel, (b) an untitled plot (وضع يد / حجة استحكام pending), or (c) a farm outside the Riyadh region (Hail, Qassim). None of these was verified as an actual listing.
- The Haraj "50,000 m², limit 100k" ad is the only farm ad near budget. It is old or deleted, and at ~2 SAR/m² it would fit a remote, unserviced parcel, but its location is unconfirmed.

### Inferences

### Gaps
