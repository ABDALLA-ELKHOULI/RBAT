## 1. Annual farm rent near Riyadh, by area. Can 100k SAR cover several years? Typical lease terms?

### Takeaway
Advertised annual farm rents near Riyadh range from about 18,000 SAR to more than 200,000 SAR a year. The most common band for small or modest farms is about 25,000–90,000 SAR a year. 100k SAR could cover roughly 1–5 years of rent on a modest farm, for example about 4 years on a large plain plot in Al-Kharj at 25k a year, or 1.5 years on a 2,700 m² Huraymila farm at 65k a year. It cannot cover a well-equipped "leisure" farm in Diriyah, Dirab or Dhurma for long, since those ask 90k–200k a year. I found no data on typical lease lengths in years.

## 3. Do Saudi banks or the Agricultural Development Fund (ADF) finance agricultural land purchase for individuals?

### Takeaway
No evidence was found that Saudi retail banks offer a mortgage product for agricultural or farm land. Al Rajhi's published real-estate finance covers residential units and residential land. ADF loans require the applicant to **already own the land (deed) or hold a long registered lease**, plus an agricultural register or licence. ADF finances farm projects (development, operating, agritourism), not land purchase as such. One unofficial guide claims otherwise, but it is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- Al Rajhi Bank's official real-estate finance page describes murabaha financing to buy a ready residential unit. Document requirements include a title deed and building permit. No agricultural-land product is mentioned — [Al Rajhi Home Finance](https://www.alrajhibank.com.sa/en/Personal/Finance/Real-Estate-Finance/Home-Finance)
- Unofficial sites on Al Rajhi land finance cover residential land only: minimum about 200 m², within an approved plan, financing ratio about 60% for residential land, tenors of 15–25 years (sources conflict) — [almaal.org](https://almaal.org/mortgage-bank-al-rajhi-bank); [mhtwyat.com](https://mhtwyat.com/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%85%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%83-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%AD%D9%8A/)
- ADF development loans require, among other things, a **deed of possession or ownership of agricultural land acceptable to the Fund**, an agricultural register, and the farm's location coordinates — [ADF development loans service](https://adf.gov.sa/en/ServicesCatalog/Pages/Services100.aspx)
- ADF loans covering fruit farms, greenhouses and agritourism have repayment of up to 10 years with a 2-year grace period. Agritourism financing requires sound farm ownership, a farm that is agriculturally productive, and a Ministry of Tourism licence — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/economy/35klqv); [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/a/1155908)
- Under the Sustainable Agricultural Rural Development Program (Reef), ADF lends against **temporary ownership documents** with a cap of **500,000 SAR**. The application is certified by the MEWA branch and the governorate (emara) — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/zwlmpb)
- Requirements summarised from ADF and secondary sources: applicant aged 21 or over; national ID; title deed (two copies) or deed-backed lease; survey by an approved engineering office; MEWA licence for the activity — [Mashroo3k ADF guide](https://sa.mashroo3k.com/en/funding/agricultural-development-fund/); [value-in.com regular-loan terms](https://value-in.com/public/en/blog/31); [ADF projects page](https://adf.gov.sa/en/CreditServices/Products/Pages/ProjectsRelatedtoAgriculture-environment-sector.aspx)
- Conflicting, unofficial claim: one feasibility-study consultancy says ADF finances purchase of agricultural land with a feasibility study, covering up to 70% of project cost. ## 4. Government agricultural land allocation (توزيع الأراضي البور / منح زراعية): status 2025–2026

### Takeaway
The user's belief is correct. Free allocation of agricultural land under the Fallow Land Distribution Law has been **suspended since 1431H (2010)**. The suspension was **extended by Council of Ministers Decision No. 130 (28/2/1440H) for a further 10 years starting 2/12/1441H (about July 2020)**, so it runs to about 2/12/1451H (around 2030). The only government access route now is **leasing** state fallow land or plots in approved agricultural schemes, through MEWA's "Aradi" (أراضي) platform and the "Naama" (نما) investment platform. These are aimed at investment projects.

### Cited Findings
- Council of Ministers Decision No. 372 dated 2/12/1431H suspended distribution of agricultural land for 10 years and asked the Ministry of Agriculture to report 6 months before expiry — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/ar/article/articledetail/id/796743); [Qistas: telegram on suspending distribution and rules for leasing](https://qistas.com/legislations/ksa/view/MjEzMjA3MzU=)
- Council of Ministers Decision No. 130 dated 28/2/1440H extended the suspension **10 more years from 2/12/1441H**. MEWA must report 6 months before the end — [Tashree: Decision 130](https://tashree.app/ncar/regulations/2778)
- Ministry of Justice circular 13/T/7705 dated 26/6/1440H confirms the halt on agricultural land grants under the Fallow Land Distribution Law. It applies even to people who had grant decisions but could not use them because of objections — [manielaw-sa.com](https://manielaw-sa.com/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-13-%D8%AA-7705-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AE-26-06-1440%D9%87%D9%80/); [elmokhtarlaw.com](https://elmokhtarlaw.com/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-13-%D8%AA-7705-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AE-26-06-1440%D9%87%D9%80/)
- Okaz (2019) reported the 10-year extension from 1441H and discussion of replacing land grants with financial compensation for unused allocated land — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/na/1710232)
- Under the original law, allocations ran from a minimum of 5 ha, about 10 ha for individuals and up to 400 ha for companies, with neighbouring owners given priority — [Qanoniah: Fallow Land Distribution Law](https://qanoniah.com/File/NzVa9Qpew87akdVoA0lYDr5yG-%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B6%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%B1)
- MEWA's **"Aradi" platform** lets registered individuals, companies and government bodies apply to **lease fallow land** (for investment, within an approved plan, or to expand a project). Individuals are verified by national ID — [Ajel: MEWA explains Aradi registration](https://ajel.sa/local/ms6rtv)
- **Naama platform** (MEWA): investors, including individuals, can apply for agricultural plots offered **for lease** in approved agricultural schemes. The service is free. Example: 27 poultry-project opportunities offered in 2024 — [Naama service page](https://naama.sa/services/details/3a6dd09b-f49a-4ee6-be6f-e26117a53633); [SPA](https://www.spa.gov.sa/N2161870)
- A small-palm-farmer support programme exists, but it is a subsidy rather than land allocation — [NCPD service: small farmer subsidy](https://ncpd.gov.sa/ar/services/12f73dae-66e1-4125-a1d5-e9a7a837a603)

### Inferences
- No free agricultural land grant route is open in 2025–2026, and none is expected before about 1451H (around 2030) unless the suspension is lifted.
- Leasing state land through Aradi or Naama is a real alternative, but it targets commercial investment projects with feasibility requirements. It is not suited to a 100k SAR weekend or hobby farm.

### Gaps
- No public information was found on MEWA's report on the suspension or any change after 2020. Current Aradi/Naama lease rates per hectare in the Riyadh region were not found.

## 5. Shared or partnership ownership (مشاع / شراكة)

### Takeaway
Buying an undivided share (حصة مشاعة) in a larger farm is a common low-budget route, but it is legally risky. A share does not give a defined physical piece until a formal subdivision (فرز). A partner cannot separate their share without everyone's consent or a court order. The Ministry of Justice launched a Najiz-based procedure for trading shared agricultural deeds in December 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Ministry of Justice controls: owning a percentage of a property does not mean owning a specific part of it unless the formal subdivision requirements are completed. Incomplete transfer of title can turn the land into a source of dispute, especially when an owner dies and there are multiple heirs — [Okaz: MoJ tackles shared agricultural deeds](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/na/2225490)
- **December 2025:** the MoJ launched a mechanism to process and transfer shared agricultural deeds electronically through **Najiz** (ناجز) — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/na/2225490)
- A lawyer is quoted saying no partner can separate their share from the other partners' shares except by agreement of all or through the courts — [Al-Watan: shared ownership threatens owners of agricultural slices](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1188761)
- Religious ruling: selling an undivided share is permissible if the land's boundaries, area and share percentage are known — [IslamQA](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/524009)
- Forum anecdotes say notaries record only the percentage, not dimensions, and buyers agree the plot location with the seller in front of witnesses. This is anecdotal and not a legal reference — [Hawamer forum](https://hawamer.com/vb/hawamer2566029)

### Inferences
- A partnership with friends or family, pooling 100k each to buy or lease a farm jointly, is feasible. It needs a written partnership agreement covering exit, inheritance and usage schedule, and ideally a formal subdivision or a company structure.

### Gaps
- No prices for shares (حصص) in specific Riyadh-area farms were found.
- No organised fractional-ownership or farm-sharing platforms in Saudi Arabia were found.

## 6. Daily and weekend farm rental (reference prices)

### Takeaway
Daily farm or istiraha rentals around Riyadh run roughly 900–2,500 SAR per night, with simple units lower. 100k SAR would pay for about 40–110 nights, i.e. roughly one night every weekend for 1–2 years. That is comparable to or worse than an annual lease of a modest farm or istiraha for a regular weekend user.
