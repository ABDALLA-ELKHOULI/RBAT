## 1. Labor: hiring a farm worker (عامل زراعي)

### Takeaway
A worker for a farm can be recruited through **Musaned** under the "farmers, fishermen and herders" activity. The employer needs a valid agricultural register (سجل زراعي), and the cap is **6 workers per individual**. Since Dec 2025 a dedicated regulation makes the employer pay all recruitment, visa and iqama costs, plus housing, food, transport and medical cover. A realistic first-year cash outlay for one recruited worker is roughly **12–15k SAR in one-off costs plus salary and upkeep**. However, I could not find an official, agriculture-specific fee schedule, and I found no reliable wage figure.

### Cited Findings
**Recruitment route and eligibility**
- Agricultural labor visas are requested online through **Musaned**. The official activity is "المزارعين والصيادين والرعاة" (farmers, fishermen and herders). The cap is **six workers per individual**, and three things are required: a **valid agricultural register (سجل زراعي)**, an existing agricultural file, and an available agricultural-worker visa balance. The source is a recruitment-office page seen only as a snippet, since the fetch was blocked. — [Rukn Services](https://rukn-services.com/استقدام-مزارع/)
- HRSD caps the prices of Musaned support-labor services and bans charging beneficiaries extra fees. The page covers support labor in general and gives no agricultural figures. — [HRSD news](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/media-center/news/تحقق-أرقام-جديدة-لتسهيل-وتبسيط-رحلة-الاستقدام)

**Government and office fees (proxies; not agriculture-specific)**
- Government recruitment visa fee: **2,000 SAR**, paid through SADAD before Musaned accepts the request. The source is commercial and the figure concerns domestic workers. — [Capsulaa](https://www.capsulaa.sa/saudi-arabia-services/شروط-استقدام-عاملة-منزلية-من-بنجلاديش/)
- Recruitment-office fee for a worker from Bangladesh: **7,000–10,000 SAR**, excluding VAT and the government visa fee. This is a domestic-worker figure used here as a proxy. — [Capsulaa](https://www.capsulaa.sa/saudi-arabia-services/شروط-استقدام-عاملة-منزلية-من-بنجلاديش/)
- An older figure (2023) for **domestic** labor contracts put the average at 31.6k SAR, within a range of 17.25k–43.5k SAR. It is dated and covers a different category. — [Al-Eqtisadiah 2023](https://www.aleqt.com/2023/05/23/article_2553086.html)
- **Iqama renewal for an agricultural worker: 650 SAR/year.** — [almaal.org](https://almaal.org/agricultural-worker-residency-renewal-fees). Sources disagree on 600 vs. 650 SAR for a first issuance by an individual sponsor. — [Capsulaa iqama fees](https://www.capsulaa.sa/saudi-arabia-services/كم-تبلغ-رسوم-تجديد-الإقامة-في-السعودية-2/)

**Expat levy (المقابل المالي / work-permit fee)**
- For establishments, the general levy is **700 SAR/month** (8,400 SAR/yr) for each expat up to the number of Saudis, and **800 SAR/month** (9,600 SAR/yr) for each expat above it. — [holool2030](https://holool2030.com/?p=6957)
- Small establishments with **9 or fewer workers, including the owner**, receive a levy exemption for up to **2 expats if the owner works full-time in the business**, or up to 4 if a Saudi employee is registered with GOSI. The exemption was extended for 3 years in 2024, and the owner must be a single person with no other business. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2100362); [al-madina](https://www.al-madina.com/ampArticle/554581)
- Through Qiwa, small establishments get a **100 SAR work permit**. — [Capsulaa](https://www.capsulaa.sa/saudi-arabia-services/طريقة-إصدار-رخصة-عمل-100-ريال-شروط-الحصول/). One site says this small-establishment exemption ends on **21 Jan 2027**, while others say it continues. No official HRSD confirmation was found. — [holool2030](https://holool2030.com/?p=5115)
- Licensed **industrial** facilities had their levy scrapped by Cabinet in Dec 2025. Agriculture was **not** reported as part of that decision. — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1866738)

**New rules for agricultural workers (Dec 2025 / 2026)**
- HRSD's "Regulation for agricultural workers, private shepherds and similar" covers workers employed by **individuals** on private farms or herding the employer's livestock, and excludes establishments. **The worker must not pay any recruitment, visa, iqama or re-entry fees.** — [HRSD regulation page](https://prod.hrsd.gov.sa/en/knowledge-centre/decisions-and-regulations/regulation-and-procedures/regulation); [Saudi Expatriates, Dec 2025](https://www.saudi-expatriates.com/2025/12/30-days-paid-leave-every-year-weekly-rest-for-agricultural-herding-similar-workers.html?m=1)
- Employers must provide **suitable housing, food allowance, transport, and medical/repatriation coverage**. The rules also set an **8-hour day**, **50% overtime pay**, **30 days of paid leave a year** and weekly rest, and they ban withholding documents. Penalties range from **300 to 20,000 SAR**. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-updates-labour-laws-for-agricultural-workers-1.500379272); [ARY News](https://arynews.tv/saudi-arabia-announces-perks-for-agricultural-and-home-workers)

**Wages**
- No reliable source was found for the monthly wage of a male expat farm worker. Paylab survey data for "animal care worker" shows 2,054–5,438 SAR gross, but that is a different and more skilled job. — [Paylab](https://saudiarabia.paylab.com/salaryinfo/agriculture-food-industry/animal-care-worker)
- The average wage for non-Saudi **women** in agriculture/animal husbandry was 1,433 SAR/month in 2017 (GASTAT, via Al Jazeera). This is old data. — [Al Jazeera 2018](https://www.aljazeera.net/ebusiness/2018/4/16/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d8%b9%d9%88%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%8a%d8%aa%d9%82%d8%a7%d8%b6%d9%8a%d9%86-%d9%86%d8%b5%d9%81-%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%aa%d8%a8)

**Alternatives: labor rental and loan (Ajeer)**
- **Ajeer** (HRSD) lets one establishment use expat workers already in KSA who are sponsored by another establishment. Each worker needs a permit. Limits: **20% of the workforce** and **12 months within a 2-year window**. — [Qoyod](https://www.qoyod.com/learn/عقد-أجير/)
- A labor-lending service exists for small establishments with **1–5 workers**. Loans last up to 6 months, are capped at 50% of the workforce, and are not allowed in a worker's first 12 months in KSA. — [Qoyod](https://www.qoyod.com/learn/عقد-أجير/)
- No published monthly price was found for renting a farm worker from a manpower company. SMASCO and similar providers quote on request. — [SMASCO](https://www.smasco.com/en/best-manpower-companies-in-saudi-arabia/)

### Inferences
- Ajeer requires both sides to be *establishments*, so a farmer working only on an individual agricultural register may not be able to use it. To use Ajeer, the user would probably need a CR or a Qiwa-registered establishment.
- **Rough one-off cost to recruit one worker, built from the proxies above:** visa 2,000 + office 7,000–10,000 + iqama 650 + medical exam and ticket (no figure found) ≈ **10–13k SAR, and likely 12–15k with travel**. That is about **10–15% of a 100k SAR total budget**, before any wages. These are proxy figures, not agriculture-specific quotes.
- **Running cost:** wage (unverified; the user's assumption is 1,200–2,000 SAR) + food allowance + housing on the farm + medical cover. Note that the 2025/26 regulation makes housing, food and medical mandatory.
- **Levy risk:** if the worker is sponsored through an *establishment* (CR) rather than as an individual farmer, the 700–800 SAR/month levy may apply once the small-establishment exemption ends. That would add 8.4–9.6k SAR/yr per worker.
- **Saudization:** individual-employer farm workers fall under the agricultural-worker regulation, which excludes establishments, so Nitaqat quotas plausibly do not bite at this scale. This is not confirmed by an official source.

### Gaps
- No official Musaned fee schedule for agricultural visas, and no confirmation of whether the levy applies to farm workers sponsored by individuals. The Rukn page with costs could not be fetched.
- No reliable 2025–26 monthly wage for expat farm workers. No priced quotes for monthly labor rental.
- The end date of the small-establishment levy exemption (21 Jan 2027 vs. continuing) is unconfirmed.

## 2. Electricity: agricultural tariff, the 2027 change and diesel

### Takeaway
The agricultural tariff is **18 halalas/kWh up to 6,000 kWh/month and 22 halalas above that**, in force through Dec 2026. **From 1 Jan 2027** it becomes **19 / 23 halalas**, plus a **new fixed charge of 140–200 SAR/month** set by breaker size, with VAT on top. Diesel rose to **1.79 SAR/L on 1 Jan 2026**.

### Cited Findings
- From 1 Jan 2027, agricultural rates move **from 18 to 19 halalas/kWh** for monthly use up to 6,000 kWh, and **from 22 to 23 halalas** above that. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-to-raise-electricity-water-and-sewage-tariffs-from-january-2027-1.500723516); [ARY News](https://arynews.tv/saudi-arabia-announces-new-electricity-water-and-wastewater-charges); [Maaal](https://maaal.com/en/news/details/saudi-energy-announces-el/)
- Fixed monthly charge for the **agricultural sector and charities: 140, 160, 180 or 200 SAR**, depending on which of four breaker-capacity bands applies. It replaces the old meter-reading, maintenance and billing fee, is pro-rated by billing days, and excludes VAT. Consumption before 1 Jan 2027 is billed at the old tariff. — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/economy/akkj28rot); [Al-Eqtisadiah](https://www.aleqt.com/الأخبار/21-هللة-للكهرباء-السكنية-و9-ريالات-للمياه-في-السعودية-من-أول-يناير-15729)
- The tariffs were announced by **Saudi Energy** (formerly SEC). The official tariff page is at se.com.sa. — [SE consumption tariffs](https://www.se.com.sa/en/Ourservices/ColumnC/Bills-and-Consumption/ConsumptionTariffs)
- Conflict: one site lists 16/20 halalas for agriculture. This disagrees with the news consensus of 18/22 now and 19/23 from 2027, and is likely outdated or wrong. — [hijri-calendars.com](https://hijri-calendars.com/electricity-tariff-comparison.php)
- A three-tier "intensive consumption" agricultural tariff exists, under Cabinet decision 111. A 2020 MEWA notice invited farms to apply. — [MEWA 2020](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News9492020.aspx). Its current details were not found.
- **Diesel: 1.79 SAR/L from 1 Jan 2026**, up 7.8% from about 1.66 SAR. It was unchanged as of the Sept 2026 pricing, and Aramco reviews diesel prices annually. — [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://aawsat.com/node/5096937); [Attaqa](https://attaqa.net/2026/01/01/%d8%a3%d8%b3%d8%b9%d8%a7%d8%b1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d9%82%d9%88%d8%af-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d8%b9%d9%88%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d8%a8%d8%b9%d8%af-%d8%b2%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af-2/). One outlet reported 1.97 SAR, which conflicts with the other sources and is probably a typo. — [Erem Business](https://www.erembusiness.com/companies/ppg7xh3)

### Inferences
These are my own calculations from assumed pump sizes. None of them comes from a source.
- **Assumption:** a 5.5 kW (7.5 hp) submersible pump running 7 h/day uses about **1,155 kWh/month**.
  - At the 2026 tariff: 1,155 × 0.18 ≈ **208 SAR**, or about **240 SAR with 15% VAT**.
  - At the 2027 tariff: 1,155 × 0.19 + 140 fixed ≈ **359 SAR**, or about **413 SAR with VAT**.
  - In summer, if run time doubles: about 2,310 kWh → roughly **505 SAR/month incl. VAT in 2027**.
- For a small farm (under 6,000 kWh/month), the **fixed charge adds more to the bill than the 1-halala rate rise**. For a 2,000–10,000 m² farm, a realistic 2027 bill is roughly **300–600 SAR/month**. Electricity is still likely to be cheaper than diesel.
- **Diesel comparison (assumption):** a small diesel pump or genset burning about 2 L/h for 7 h/day uses about 420 L/month, which costs about **750 SAR/month at 1.79 SAR/L**, before maintenance.

### Gaps
- The kVA/amp ranges for each of the four agricultural breaker bands were not found.
- Whether the lease includes the electricity meter account, and any arrears on it, depends on the contract (see section 6).

## 3. Water: well, pump, water testing and drip irrigation

### Takeaway
No reliable published SAR figures were found for drip-irrigation installation per 1,000 m², for replacing a pump, or for testing water salinity. The only hard data point is the retail price of a 3 hp submersible pump, about 1,200 SAR. Well cleaning or deepening without a permit can be fined 20–50k SAR.

### Cited Findings

- MEWA's well rules: cleaning or deepening a well in the sedimentary shelf without authorization carries a fine starting at **20,000–50,000 SAR**, so check whether a permit is needed before any well work. — [Qanoniah, MEWA well conditions](https://qanoniah.com/en/File/jw5DbJMQVKLzb46LvdkYq14Wy-Conditions%2Band%2BRegulations%2Bfor%2BDrilling%2Band%2BUsing%2BGroundwater%2BWells); [MEWA](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News6422020.aspx)
- New water-conservation regulations carry fines of up to 200,000 SAR. The reported urban rules take effect on 21 Mar 2027. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-sets-water-conservation-fines-of-up-to-sr200000-1.500642580); [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/xw2yzww)
- Drip irrigation costs more to install than other systems but cuts labor and water use. No SAR figure per dunam was given. — [ronahi.net](https://ronahi.net/?p=65244)

### Inferences
- In a deep Riyadh-region well, a submersible usually needs to be larger than 3 hp. Replacing one (pump, cable, pipe and labor) plausibly costs several thousand SAR. This is unverified, so get local quotes.
- Ask the landlord for the well's license and the latest water analysis. Any well work may need MEWA approval.

### Gaps
- No sourced cost for drip installation per 1,000 m², water-salinity (EC/TDS) lab tests, or well maintenance. Local supplier and lab quotes are needed.

## 5. Licenses and registrations

### Takeaway
The core is the **free agricultural register (سجل زراعي) on MEWA's Naama platform**, which is mandatory under Cabinet decision 39. It is followed by an **agricultural activity license** (رخصة ممارسة النشاط الزراعي), also on Naama, and **tenants can be registered on the holding**. To sell, a **freelance document** (free, annual) or a CR is needed. VAT registration is **mandatory only above 375k SAR in revenue**, and optional from 187.5k SAR.

### Cited Findings
- Cabinet decision **39** requires anyone practicing agriculture to hold an **agricultural register**. The register is a prerequisite for agricultural licenses, which define the activity and the area. — [MEWA FarmReg](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/Ads/Pages/FarmReg.aspx); [MEWA news 2020](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News12072020.aspx)
- MEWA launched issuance of the **agricultural activity license (رخصة ممارسة النشاط الزراعي) through Naama**, based on updated activity data for the holding. — [MEWA news 2020](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News13232020.aspx)
- The Naama user guide for the agricultural register says the **service is free**. A **"tenant" page lets the farmer register all tenants of the holding, verified via Absher**. — [MEWA Naama user guide (PDF)](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/eServices/Documents/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%20%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84%20%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%20%D8%A5%D8%B5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%20%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84%20%D8%B2%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A.pdf)
- MEWA also publishes an agricultural licensing guide (PDF). It was not readable because the fetch was blocked. — [MEWA Agricultural Licensing Guide](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/InformationCenter/DocsCenter/RulesLibrary/Documents/AgriculturalLicensingGuide.pdf)
- An unofficial site says applications are approved within about 30 days. — [Capsulaa](https://www.capsulaa.sa/saudi-arabia-services/منصة-زراعي-وزارة-البيئة-والمياه-والزر/)
- **Freelance document (وثيقة العمل الحر)**: issued by HRSD through freelance.sa. It is electronic, valid for one year, renewable and **free**. — [Wdeftk guide](https://wdeftksa.com/sa/guides/freelance)
- ZATCA: a freelance-document holder registers by adding the document as an activity license in their ZATCA account. **VAT registration is mandatory at ≥375,000 SAR annual revenue, optional at 187,500–375,000, and not allowed below 187,500.** — [ZATCA e-commerce guideline](https://zatca.gov.sa:443/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/E-commerce%20Guideline.pdf); [Ajel/ZATCA reply](https://ajel.sa/thehour/7hsian089j)
- **SFDA**: food establishments must be licensed before operating, and some agricultural processing units are licensed by MEWA rather than SFDA. — [SFDA guide](https://sfda.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2024-08/FF5456Fw.pdf); [SFDA Local Food Guide](https://www.sfda.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2020-10/Local_Food_Guide.pdf)

### Inferences
- Selling **raw, unpacked produce** under an agricultural register is likely covered by MEWA licensing. **Packaged or processed goods** (packed eggs, jarred honey, processed food) likely trigger SFDA establishment licensing and labeling rules. This is not confirmed.
- A tenant should get the landlord to add them as a tenant on the holding in Naama, or register a holding in their own name. This step is what unlocks visas (see section 1) and support programs.
- At this scale, revenue will be well under 375k SAR, so VAT registration is not required.

### Gaps
- No specific SFDA rules were found for selling farm eggs or local honey retail. Baladi licensing for a farm stall or selling point was not researched because of the tool-call budget. Zakat obligations for a freelancer or individual farmer were not confirmed.
- The "farmer card / agricultural number" (بطاقة مزارع / الرقم الزراعي) was not found as a distinct product. It may be the Naama agricultural register number. Unconfirmed.

## 6. Support programs (Reef, ADF, MEWA) and the lease

### Takeaway
**Reef** (the Sustainable Agricultural Rural Development Program) gives direct support capped at **54,000 SAR/yr per beneficiary**. It targets **8 sectors**: coffee, honey and beekeeping, small livestock, fisheries, fruit, rain-fed crops, roses, and rural productive families. It is **not aimed at irrigated vegetable farming near Riyadh**, but beekeeping or a rural productive-family project could qualify. **ADF** loans for small livestock farmers go up to **100k SAR (75% of project cost)**. No tenant-specific rules or agricultural-insurance program were found.

### Cited Findings
**Reef**
- Reef supports small farmers and fishermen. It covers 8 sectors, including Arabica coffee, beekeeping and honey, small livestock, fisheries, fruit, rain-fed crops and roses. — [UNCTAD](https://unctad.org/node/40289); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/business/saudi-environment-ministry-launches-reef-programme-to-support-small-producers-i0bck9sz); [Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/en/reef-portal)
- There is **no fixed monthly amount**. Entitlement depends on tree counts, area, hive counts or output, under an **overall cap of 54,000 SAR/year across all programs**. Sources conflict on the income cap: **9,000 SAR/month** per Ajel versus **6,000 SAR** per Capsulaa 2026. — [Ajel Reef guide](https://ajel.sa/guide/reef); [Capsulaa](https://www.capsulaa.sa/saudi-arabia-services/شروط-الدعم-الريفي-للأسر-المنتجة-والعا/)
- For **rural productive families**, the monthly support is 10% of registered project capital, capped at the capital amount and at 54k SAR/yr. — [Capsulaa](https://www.capsulaa.sa/saudi-arabia-services/شروط-الدعم-الريفي-للأسر-المنتجة-والعا/)
- Scale: SAR 2.4bn disbursed through end-2024. Earlier, SAR 1.9bn had gone to 71k farmers. — [Riyadh Daily](https://alriyadhdaily.com/article/9fb1c78840e0433c974e53422452dfd3); [Al Arabiya 2024](https://www.alarabiya.net/saudi-today/2024/01/30/ريف-لـ-العربية-نت-مليونا-ريال-لـ-٧١-ألف-مزارع-منذ-اطلاق-البرنامج-)
- MEWA has a project targeting **1,000 small beekeepers**. Reef runs honey projects (May 2026). — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%85%D9%84%D9%83%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%AC-7786-%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B3%D9%84-%D8%B3%D9%86%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D8%A3%D9%83%D8%AB%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A-%D8%AE%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%86%D8%AD%D9%84-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%BA%D8%B0%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A)

**Agricultural Development Fund (ADF)**
- **Small livestock farmers** can borrow up to **100,000 SAR, at no more than 75% of project cost**. Conditions: hold 50–500 head, be registered with MEWA, be aged 21+, not be a government or private-sector employee, attend a workshop, and repay monthly within 4 years. The article date is unclear. — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/vfdmbm)

### Inferences
- For an irrigated vegetable farm in Al-Kharj, Durma or Al-Muzahmiyah, Reef is relevant mainly if the user adds **beehives** or qualifies as a **rural productive family**. ADF's small-livestock line fits only if the farm holds 50 or more head.
- Bank-style guarantees and MEWA registration make ADF hard to use for a first-year tenant on a 100k budget. Treat ADF as an option for year 2 and later.
- **Lease must-haves (my inference):**
  - written, notarized term of 3 years or more
  - the well license and the electricity account sit with the landlord
  - clear allocation of electricity bills, pump repairs and well permits
  - the landlord consents to registering the tenant on Naama, which is needed for visas and support
