# Company setup costs and steps in Saudi Arabia (2025–2026) for a small farm-produce business near Riyadh

Method note: page fetches were blocked by the session proxy (noblecoreksa.com and qoyod.com failed DNS resolution). All findings below come from search-result snippets, so check each figure on the official portal (business.sa, mc.gov.sa, balady.gov.sa, naama.sa, sfda.gov.sa, zatca.gov.sa) before budgeting. Nine searches were run.

## Q1. Which legal form fits a Saudi owner with about SAR 100k

### Takeaway
For one Saudi owner, the choice is between a sole establishment (مؤسسة فردية), which is cheapest and fastest but carries unlimited personal liability, and a one-person LLC, which gives limited liability at a somewhat higher cost and more paperwork. A freelance document is free, but no source found lists agriculture among its activities, so it does not appear to suit a farm plus wholesale business.

### Cited Findings
- Under the Companies Law, a one-person company is an LLC formed by a single individual or a single legal entity — [vjmglobal](https://www.vjmglobal.com/feeds/blog/one-person-company-saudi-arabia-india); [AstroLabs](https://insight.astrolabs.com/business-setup-costs-in-saudi-arabia-2025-comprehensive-overview)
- Sources disagree on minimum capital. One firm says SAR 100,000 is required, but that applies to foreign investors. Another says the Companies Law sets no minimum for an LLC, though the declared capital must be paid in — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/saudiarabia/inward-foreign-investment/1661066/how-to-set-up-an-llc-business-in-saudi-arabia-key-requirements-costs-and-advantages-for-foreign-investors); [Healy](https://www.healyconsultants.com/saudi-arabia-company-registration/setup-llc/)
- A one-person company can reportedly be set up electronically within one working day — [Etqan law firm](https://etqanlawfirm-sa.com/%d8%aa%d8%a3%d8%b3%d9%8a%d8%b3-%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%83%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%ae%d8%b5-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%ad%d8%af-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d8%b9%d9%88%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%a9/)
- Freelance document (وثيقة العمل الحر):
  - It is issued by the Ministry of Human Resources (HRSD) through freelance.sa. It is free, valid for one year and renewable — [HRSD](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/ar/ministry-services/services/%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%88%D8%AB%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%B1); [Sabbar](https://sabbar.com/blog/freelance-work-document-terms)
  - Applicants must be Saudi, aged 18–60, with an active Absher account and an active bank account — [Sabbar](https://sabbar.com/blog/freelance-work-document-terms)
  - The listed categories (IT, design, consulting, crafts, logistics and so on) do not include agriculture — [wdeftksa](https://www.wdeftksa.com/sa/guides/freelance)

### Inferences
- With a SAR 100k budget, either form is affordable, since government fees are a small share (see Q4). A one-person LLC protects personal assets from farm-lease, supplier and food-liability claims. Quick-commerce buyers may also prefer a company, though this is an assumption that no source confirms.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed whether the freelance document has an income cap. The often-quoted "1.5 million" figure refers to the number of documents issued, not an income limit.
- No official confirmation that agriculture is excluded from the freelance document.

## Q2. Government fees, item by item

### Takeaway
The new Commercial Register Law and Trade Names Law came into force on 3 April 2025. One Qoyod report gives 2026 instead, so the date conflicts. The law replaced CR expiry and renewal with an annual confirmation (تأكيد سنوي). Most agricultural and municipal registrations are free or low cost. New establishments are exempt from Chamber of Commerce fees for their first three years. Many exact amounts could not be confirmed from official sources.

### Cited Findings
- **Commercial Register, new law:**
  - Royal Decree M/83, in force 3 April 2025. It abolishes CR expiry in favour of an annual confirmation of data and abolishes sub-registers, so one CR covers all activities in the Kingdom — [Etqan](https://etqanlawfirm-sa.com/%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3%D8%A9-%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9/)
  - The Qoyod operating-cost report dates the change to 3 April 2026, which conflicts with Etqan — [Qoyod PDF](https://www.qoyod.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/qoyod-operating-costs-ar.pdf)
  - If the annual confirmation is not made within 90 days of its due date, the registrar warns that the CR will be suspended after 14 days — [Etqan](https://etqanlawfirm-sa.com/%d9%81%d8%aa%d8%ad-%d8%b3%d8%ac%d9%84-%d8%aa%d8%ac%d8%a7%d8%b1%d9%8a-%d8%ac%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%af/)
  - Fee figures conflict:
    - SAR 500 to issue an electronic CR for a sole establishment — [Etqan](https://etqanlawfirm-sa.com/%d9%81%d8%aa%d8%ad-%d8%b3%d8%ac%d9%84-%d8%aa%d8%ac%d8%a7%d8%b1%d9%8a-%d8%ac%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%af/)
    - SAR 200 per year for the main CR, SAR 100 per sub-CR, and a SAR 1,000 penalty for late renewal. These appear to reflect the pre-2025 regime — [thelawsa](https://thelawsa.com/%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3%D8%A9/); [Qoyod PDF](https://www.qoyod.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/qoyod-operating-costs-ar.pdf)
- **Trade name:**
  - Reserved on the Saudi Business Center for up to 60 days, with up to 5 proposed names per application. The invoice is issued after Ministry approval — [mc.gov.sa FAQ](https://mc.gov.sa/ar/FAQ); [mc.gov.sa service](https://mc.gov.sa/ar/eservices/Pages/ServiceDetails.aspx?sID=1)
  - The fee is described only as "nominal" and non-refundable; no amount was found — [projectsegy](https://projectsegy.com/conditions-choosing-trade-name/)
- **Articles of association (company only):** The process is partner approval, then a payment invoice, then the articles are issued, the CR is issued and the contract is published electronically — [mc.gov.sa FAQ](https://mc.gov.sa/ar/FAQ). No fee amount was found.
- **Chamber of Commerce:**
  - New establishments are exempt for their first 3 years after CR registration. In years 4–5, the reduced fee is SAR 500 for sole establishments and SAR 200 for companies with capital under SAR 375k and no more than 5 workers. Only one subscription is needed regardless of branches — [mc.gov.sa news 2021](https://mc.gov.sa/ar/mediacenter/News/Pages/21-08-21-01.aspx); [Aleqt](https://www.aleqt.com/2021/08/22/article_2156586.html)
  - An unofficial source puts class 3 at SAR 800 — [aleqtsad](https://www.aleqtsad.org/التجارة-بدء-سريان-إعفاء-المنشآت-من-رس/)
  - Etqan gives a range of SAR 300–1,500 per year — [Etqan](https://etqanlawfirm-sa.com/%d8%aa%d9%83%d9%84%d9%81%d8%a9-%d8%aa%d8%a3%d8%b3%d9%8a%d8%b3-%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%83%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%ae%d8%b5-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%ad%d8%af/)
  - None of these sources confirms any change in 2025.
- **Baladi municipal licence and Salamah:**
  - The annual fee is charged per m², plus a per-m² waste-collection fee. Riyadh is in the first fee category. Licences can now run up to 5 years — [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1024394)
  - The Baladi commercial activity licence is issued together with the Civil Defense safety permit, and fees apply — [balady.gov.sa](https://balady.gov.sa/ar/all-products/10920)
  - The official fee schedule is the municipal services fee regulation — [momah.gov.sa PDF 2025-11](https://momah.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2025-11/layht%20rswm%20alkhdmat%20albldyt.pdf). The PDF was not readable here.
- **MEWA agricultural register and licence (Naama):**
  - The MEWA user guide says issuing the agricultural register is free, done electronically through Naama — [MEWA guide PDF](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/eServices/Documents/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%20%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84%20%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%20%D8%A5%D8%B5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%20%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84%20%D8%B2%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A.pdf)
  - The agricultural activity licence (plant, animal and fish) is issued through naama.sa — [MEWA news](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News13232020.aspx)
  - The Agriculture Law implementing regulations define the register (سجل) and the licence (ترخيص) — [qanoniah](https://qanoniah.com/File/gdbQ9aO8q3oykW27X521MvYpe)
- **SFDA:**
  - Local establishments register on the Ghad platform — [SFDA eservice](https://www.sfda.gov.sa/en/eservices/19513)
  - A consultancy gives the warehouse licence fee as a formula of area and term. For 500 m², it quotes SAR 1,300 for 1 year, 1,600 for 2 years and 1,900 for 3 years. Registering ordinary food products is free — [Motaded](https://motaded.com.sa/blog/food-establishment-licensing-sfda-factories-warehouses-and-special-foods-complete-guide-2026)
  - Official guide — [SFDA guide PDF](https://sfda.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2025-02/GuideObtainingLicenseOperateFoodFeedManufacturersWarehouses.pdf)
- **VAT and e-invoicing:**
  - Wave 24 covers taxpayers whose VAT-subject revenue exceeded SAR 375k in 2022, 2023 or 2024. They must integrate with Fatoora by 30 June 2026 — [Lookuptax](https://lookuptax.com/tax-changes/saudi-arabia/zatca-wave-24-criteria-2026); [ZATCA news](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-1063.aspx)
  - Wave 25 reportedly uses a SAR 187.5k threshold from 1 February 2027. This is unverified — [Lookuptax](https://lookuptax.com/tax-changes/saudi-arabia/einvoicing-wave-25)
- **Overall setup cost:** One consultancy says government fees for a foreign service company are usually under SAR 10,000 of a SAR 45k–120k first-year total — [AstroLabs](https://insight.astrolabs.com/business-setup-costs-in-saudi-arabia-2025-comprehensive-overview). That total is not representative of a Saudi-owned business.

### Inferences
- These points come from general knowledge, not this research, and need checking:
  - The VAT registration thresholds of SAR 375k (mandatory) and SAR 187.5k (voluntary) are well established.
  - Phase 1 of e-invoicing (generating electronic invoices) applies to every VAT-registered business. Phase 2 (integration with Fatoora) applies by wave.
  - Fresh produce is believed to be standard-rated at 15% in KSA, not zero-rated.

### Gaps
- Exact current fees under the new CR law, including issuance and annual confirmation, for a sole establishment versus an LLC. Conflicting figures are given above.
- Fee for the articles of association or incorporation. The SAR 1,200 figure could not be found anywhere.
- Trade name fee amount.
- Riyadh Baladi rate per m² for a warehouse or farm, the signboard fee, and the Salamah fee. These are in the momah PDF, which could not be read.
- Whether a farm needs a Baladi licence at all, or only the MEWA licence. Not confirmed.
- GOSI, Qiwa and Muqeem costs; national address; bank account; accountant fees; cold-transport vehicle permits (SFDA / Transport General Authority). None were researched because of the tool-call budget.
- Fee for the MEWA agricultural activity licence. Only the register is confirmed free.

## Q3. ISIC activity codes

### Takeaway
GASTAT's ISIC4 list uses 6-digit codes. Two have been checked: 011301 is potatoes, and 011306 is vegetables in greenhouses. The code the user mentioned, 463011, was not confirmed. The nearest code found was 4620, wholesale of agricultural raw materials.

### Cited Findings
- 011301 is cultivation of potatoes and sweet potatoes. 011306 is growing vegetables in greenhouses — [GASTAT ISIC4](https://stats.gov.sa/en/isic4)
- Group 0113 covers vegetables, melons, roots and tubers — [WorldOfTaxonomy](https://worldoftaxonomy.com/codes/isic_sa/0111)
- 4620 is described as wholesale of agricultural raw materials, including fruits and vegetables. This is a third-party crosswalk — [WorldOfTaxonomy](https://www.worldoftaxonomy.com/crosswalks/isic_sa/4620/isic_rev4)
- SFDA keeps its own list of accepted economic activities — [SFDA](https://www.sfda.gov.sa/en/economic-activities?page=8)

### Inferences
- In international ISIC, wholesale of fruits and vegetables falls under class 4630, so a Saudi 4630xx code is likely. Confirm it on the GASTAT search page.

### Gaps
- The exact Saudi codes for open-field vegetables and for fruit and vegetable wholesale were not confirmed.

## Q4. Total first-year cost range

### Takeaway
No source gives a total for a Saudi-owned agricultural micro-business. Combining the partial figures above, mandatory government fees appear to be in the low thousands of SAR, roughly SAR 1k–5k excluding labour, because of the following:
- Chamber fee exempt for 3 years
- MEWA register free
- CR in the SAR 200–500 range per the conflicting sources
- SFDA warehouse licence about SAR 1.3k, only if a store is licensed
- Baladi/Salamah unknown, charged per m²

Accountant fees, Qiwa/GOSI and vehicle permits would come on top. This estimate is an inference and should be verified.

### Cited Findings
- See the line items in Q2. The only explicit range found is SAR 30k–50k for "opening a company", which likely includes legal and consultancy fees — [Etqan](https://etqanlawfirm-sa.com/%d8%aa%d9%83%d9%84%d9%81%d8%a9-%d8%aa%d8%a3%d8%b3%d9%8a%d8%b3-%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%83%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%ae%d8%b5-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%ad%d8%af/)

### Inferences
- A realistic budget would add an outsourced accountant and bookkeeping, a ZATCA-compliant e-invoicing tool, and permits for a refrigerated vehicle. Amounts for these are unsourced.

### Gaps
- Sourced numbers for accountant fees, GOSI/Qiwa/work permits, cold-vehicle permits and bank charges.
