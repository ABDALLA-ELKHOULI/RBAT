# Wholesale Produce Prices, Wholesale Platforms, and Riyadh Central Market (Aziziyah) Rules

Method note: Done 2026-11-17. The sandbox could not reach sabq.org, aleqt.com or mewa.gov.sa (DNS blocked), so most findings below come from search-engine snippets and summaries, not full articles. Many dates in the snippets are page-render dates, not publication dates. Verify before relying on them.

## 1. Current wholesale (جملة) prices in Riyadh and official price bulletins

### Takeaway
I found no reliable, dated 2025–2026 wholesale price per carton for Riyadh's Aziziyah market, and no official daily price bulletin from MEWA, Amanat Al-Riyadh or GASTAT. Arabic "today's wholesale prices" searches return mostly Egyptian (Obour market) reports.

### Cited Findings
- Al-Watan (date not visible in snippet) reported a Riyadh tomato carton jumping to 40 SAR from about 15 SAR a few weeks earlier. Shop staff blamed higher prices at the Aziziyah central market, which their shops buy from — [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1043104)
- A 14 Sep 2026 headline gives a tomato crate at 18 riyals and a cucumber crate at 23 riyals at "the central market". raya.com is the Qatari daily Al-Raya, so these are almost certainly Doha (QAR) prices, NOT Riyadh — [Al-Raya](https://www.raya.com/2026/09/14/استقرار-أسعار-الخضراوات-بالسوق-المركزي)
- MEWA and Riyadh Development Co. launched a "digital portal" for the Al-Ta'mir (Aziziyah) vegetable and fruit market. It tracks produce from farm to market to consumer and pre-registers farmers and marketers through the "Nama" portal (agricultural register data). Snippets show no price publication — [Sayidaty](https://www.sayidaty.net/node/1792767); [MEWA partnership page](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/Ministry/AboutMinistry/Partnerships/PrivateSector/Pages/riyadhdev.aspx) (fetch blocked)

### Inferences
- In practice, wholesale prices are discovered daily at the Aziziyah auction or through traders and dalals. Treat any figure as highly volatile: the 15 to 40 SAR swing for tomatoes shows this.
- The farm should collect its own price data, for example by visiting Aziziyah at dawn or calling dalals for a few weeks before planting decisions.

### Gaps
- No sourced wholesale prices for cucumbers, peppers, lettuce, herbs (جرجير، بقدونس، كزبرة، نعناع), zucchini, eggplant, eggs or strawberries.
- I could not confirm whether the MEWA/Ta'mir portal publishes daily prices, or whether GASTAT's monthly average-price releases cover wholesale (they generally cover retail).

## 2. Platforms and sources for wholesale produce trading in KSA

### Takeaway
Aziziyah is the dominant channel: the owner, Riyadh Development Co., says about 60% of the Kingdom's produce passes through it. B2B apps exist that specifically trade fresh produce (Nabt, Farmi, Qawafel). Sary is a general FMCG wholesale app, not a produce channel.

### Cited Findings
- Aziziyah Wholesale Fruit & Vegetable Market is owned and run by Riyadh Development Co. It was built in 1998 on about 274,000 m² in Al-Aziziyah. The company says about 60% of the Kingdom's fruit and vegetables flow through it (this is the company's own claim) — [Riyadh Development](https://new-design.riyadh.dev/en/?p=26879); [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/bf/arriyadh/article/id/1860121/multi-business-riyadh-development-ink-deal-to-build-riyadh-wholesale-market)
- Nabt (نبت) app: a B2B vegetable and fruit marketplace linking wholesale and retail buyers with farmers and suppliers across KSA. It claims farm delivery within 12 hours (company claim) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nabt-nbt/id1617317572?l=en)
- Farmi: a B2B platform connecting Saudi farmers and SMEs to sell local farm products. Qawafel: a B2B platform for perishable and refrigerated food. Sary: a general B2B wholesale marketplace, not produce-specific — [Mubasher](https://www.sharikatmubasher.com/media-hub/experts-thoughts/16468?lang=en); [Qawafel](https://www.builtinaustin.com/company/qawafel)
- A Saudi founder reports raising $3.4M "to build the physical and digital backbone of Saudi Arabia's fresh-produce sector" (company unnamed in snippet) — [LinkedIn](https://sa.linkedin.com/in/ahmedalbasi)
- MEWA runs a "Farmers Market" (سوق المزارعين) program page — [MEWA](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/Ministry/Agencies/AgencyofAgriculture/FarmersMarket/Pages/default.aspx) (fetch blocked, contents unverified)
- A weekly "Farmer's Day" (يوم المزارع) market has run for 10 years at Muruj Riyadh. It is a direct-to-consumer outlet — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/EkhwjQ1)
- MEWA's "موسم حصادها 2024" campaign promotes local seasonal produce in public markets. It is a campaign, not a trading platform — [search summary of MEWA news](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/MediaCenter/News/Pages/News10112020.aspx)

### Inferences
- For a small farm, the realistic wholesale options are: (a) consign to a dalal at Aziziyah; (b) sell to Aziziyah traders or shops directly; (c) list on Nabt or Farmi-type B2B apps. Onboarding terms and fees are unknown.
- WhatsApp groups and Haraj wholesale listings exist, but I found no citable source on them.

### Gaps
- Fees, commissions and the current operating status of Nabt, Farmi and Qawafel. Whether Zood or Retailo handle fresh produce (no evidence found).
- The role of agricultural cooperatives in Riyadh produce marketing (not researched; no results).

## 3. Riyadh central market regulations (Aziziyah)

### Takeaway
The market is being redeveloped. A design-and-build contract was signed in Nov 2025, and the North (Muruj) market was closed on 30 Oct 2025. MEWA approved mandatory packaging and labeling rules for public-benefit markets around Sep 2025: a product card with the agricultural-register number, and corrugated, ventilated cartons. Registration via the agricultural register (Nama) is linked to the market portal. I could not source the dalal commission rate for Riyadh, booth costs, or a vegetable-market-specific Saudization decision.

### Cited Findings
**Redevelopment and relocation**
- Oct 2025: Multi Business Group won a contract from Riyadh Development Co. to develop the Aziziyah wholesale market, worth more than 85% of Multi Business's 2024 revenue — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1854169)
- 18 Nov 2025: the design-and-build contract for the "Riyadh wholesale market for vegetables and fruits" was signed. It was later assigned to subsidiary Hulul Salasil — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/bf/arriyadh/article/id/1860121/multi-business-riyadh-development-ink-deal-to-build-riyadh-wholesale-market); [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1879523)
- Jan 2026: Riyadh Development transferred the Aziziyah market assets to its subsidiary "Hulul Salasil Imdad Almontajat Altazija" (Fresh Produce Supply Chain Solutions) — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/bf/arriyadh/article/id/1870543)
- North central market (Al-Muruj) was closed at Amanat Al-Riyadh's request, with an evacuation deadline of 30 Oct 2025. National Agricultural Services Co. said this was part of a development plan — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/bf/arriyadh/article/id/1843157/riyadh-s-north-market-evacuation-deadline-set-for-oct-30)
- An earlier 2013 report described a ~$240m upgrade plan for the Riyadh fruit and vegetable market — [Construction Index](https://theconstructionindex.co.uk/news/view/riyadh-fruit-and-veg-market-to-get-240m-upgrade)

**Registration and traceability**
- The MEWA–Riyadh Development digital portal pre-registers farmers and marketers through the Nama portal, using agricultural-register data, and traces produce from farm to market to consumer — [Sayidaty](https://www.sayidaty.net/node/1792767); [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2214211)

**Packaging and labeling**
- MEWA approved packaging rules for public-benefit markets (أسواق النفع العام), reported around Sep 2025. They cover the agricultural product card, plastic and carton containers, and packing specifications. Each package must carry a product-card label with product name, net weight, packing date, origin, and supplier or producer data such as the agricultural register number. Cartons must be corrugated board, food-grade, recyclable and ventilated, and pallet stacks must be covered — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2214211); [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/economy/saudi/2214175)
- The older SFDA guide for fresh produce packing (2020) says box materials must be free of harmful substances and gives a box volume limit of 20 dm³ — [SFDA PDF](https://SFDA.GOV.SA/sites/default/files/2020-01/Giud06.pdf)

**Pesticide residue control**
- 2018: SFDA and MEWA sampled produce from Aziziyah (Riyadh) and Al-Kharj central markets to detect banned or unregistered pesticides. MEWA's Riyadh branch tested 112 samples with none positive, and it also samples farm produce prepared for marketing — [SFDA](https://www.sfda.gov.sa/ar/news/1809); [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/na/2235108)
- MEWA (Eastern Province branch) says a grower whose produce exceeds the maximum residue limit is penalized under regulations. Testing is run with King Faisal Specialist Hospital on about 2,000 random samples nationwide (undated) — [Al-Yaum](https://www.alyaum.com/ampArticle/6054254)
- A Ramadan campaign reported 658 compliant and 5 non-compliant residue samples (year and city unclear) — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2207717)

**Labor and Saudization**
- Press reports describe long-standing control of Aziziyah trading by expatriate labor and anonymous stalls, with municipal council complaints (older reports) — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/Fa-Dd-K); [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1165174)
- Jeddah central market banned expat labor in 2014. HRSD's 2021 Saudization decisions covered grocery stores (≥300 m²) and central supermarkets (≥500 m²), which are retail, not produce wholesale halls — [Aleqt 2014](https://www.aleqt.com/2014/11/16/article_906394.html); [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/dpzwql); [HRSD](https://hrsd.gov.sa/ar/news/%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%8A%D8%B5%D8%AF%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D8%A8%D9%82%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D9%87%D9%86)

**Dalal commission**
- The only figure found is 7% charged to the seller by dalals at the Buraidah dates market. It covers dates, not Riyadh vegetables — [search summary; source page in results](https://sabq.org/article/Fa-Dd-K)
- A 2010 King Saud University study analyzes the structure of the Riyadh wholesale market based on dalal interviews (full text not read) — [KSU](https://direct.ksu.edu.sa/bitstreams/80d6b7ea-6dd1-4c59-9f82-03d63ba01f12/download)

**Market operating rules elsewhere**
- Regional vegetable markets in Riyadh province (Thadiq, Ramah, Dawadmi, Shaqra...) are tendered on Furas. The booklets cover operating hours, entry rules and rent/service price schedules, which may serve as a proxy for typical rules — [Furas Thadiq](https://furas.momah.gov.sa/opportunity/attachment/182781754/كراسة سوق الخضار والفواكه بمحافظة ثادق/pdf)

### Inferences
- A farm selling at Aziziyah should expect to need: an agricultural register or farm record linked to Nama, compliant labeled cartons, and readiness for residue sampling. It will likely sell via a licensed dalal on commission rather than renting a stall.
- The redevelopment (2025–2026 onward) may change stall allocation, fees and traceability rules. Check with Hulul Salasil / Riyadh Development directly.

### Gaps
- Dalal commission % at Aziziyah. Stall or booth rent. Market opening hours. Whether farmers can sell directly without a CR. A specific Saudization decision for produce wholesale markets. Residue-violation penalty amounts. The new market's completion date and location. All unconfirmed: the relevant pages were blocked or not found.
