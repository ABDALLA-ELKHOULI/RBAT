# Novel / Emerging Livestock Feed Technologies in Saudi Arabia & the Gulf (2020-2026)

Method note: built from 12 searches. The primary-source fetches failed with DNS errors (iofs.org, mdpi.com, doi.org), so many findings rest on search-result summaries of the cited pages. Treat any figure not marked as verified as a "check before use" figure.

## Which novel approaches have real Saudi deployments and data?

### Takeaway
Nearly all of the novel feed approaches in KSA are still at R&D, pilot or MoU stage. The ones with real Saudi field or experimental data are treated-wastewater fodder (decades of KSU/KACST trials), date by-product feeds (traditional use plus trials), and KAUST microalgae (a 3,000 L pilot with >40% protein). Insect protein (PHYLA) is a university-lab study plus MoUs. I found no commercial Saudi black soldier fly (BSF) plant. Market context: the 2018 ban on green fodder cultivation pushed large dairies onto imports and overseas farms.

### Cited Findings
**Insect protein (BSF)**
- 2017: AgriProtein licensed Saudi tech hub Sajt to build three fly farms, the "Middle East's first". Each was planned at 8.5 billion flies converting 250 t/day of organic waste. I found no evidence these were built (AgriProtein itself later collapsed; not verified here) — [Engineering News](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/saudi-arabia-to-build-three-fly-farms-says-agriprotein-2017-03-28); [WATTAgNet](https://wattagnet.com/articles/30298-saudi-arabia-fly-farm-to-produce-insect-based-animal-feed)
- PHYLA (Saudi biotech) leads what it calls the Middle East's first comprehensive study of BSF larvae protein for poultry feed. The study runs in King Saud University labs with ARASCO and Tanmiah. No results have been published — [MENA Startup Digest](https://menastartupdigest.com/?p=48315)
- Feb 2026: Tanmiah Food Company signed MoUs at IFAT Saudi Arabia with PHYLA (BSF R&D turning organic waste into feed) and RECYCLEE (a by-product monetisation platform). The deals are linked to the national target of 90% poultry self-sufficiency by 2030. Stage: R&D, with no funding disclosed — [PR Newswire](https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20823/release/20260205EN80655); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/tanmiah-partners-with-phyla-and-recyclee-to-bolster-circular-economy-and-food-security-dduxf5pe)

**Microalgae / single-cell protein**
- KAUST and MEWA run DABKSA (Development of Algal Biotechnology in KSA), which includes a proof-of-concept algae plant at KAUST aimed at animal and aquaculture feed — [KAUST Innovation](https://innovation.kaust.edu.sa/kaust-and-mewa-to-establish-the-algal-biotechnology-industry-in-saudi-arabia/); [Arab News](https://arabnews.pk/node/2053156)
- More than 20 native and commercial strains proved robust, with over 40% protein in dry biomass. *C. merolae* was grown on commercial fertiliser in 3,000 L pilot systems. Phase II at >100,000 L in seawater using flue-gas CO2 was "expected" in autumn 2023, and I found no later confirmation. The pilot was designed to produce a year of productivity data for an LCA and economics assessment, which I did not find published — [KAUST news 2023](https://kaust.edu.sa/news/algal-biotechnologists-at-kaust-are-generating-sustainable-animal-feeds-in-saudi-arabia); [Aquafeed](https://aquafeed.com/newsroom/news/saudi-arabia-to-establish-algal-industry-for-aquafeeds)
- Topian (NEOM's food company, launched late 2023) has pillars that include novel foods and aquaculture. Its disclosed deals are fish and salmon. I found no source tying Topian to algae feed or Salicornia — [AGBI](https://www.agbi.com/sustainability/2023/12/saudi-neom-company-food-security/); [Arab News](https://arab.news/49waz)

**Seawater / halophyte forage**
- ICBA's Salicornia work is UAE-based (Dubai). It reached a seed yield of 3 t/ha in 2018 using aquaculture effluent seawater, and the crop is positioned for food, forage and biofuel — [ICBA 2018](https://www.biosaline.org/news/2018-05-10-6466); [ICBA 2023](https://www.biosaline.org/news/2023-02-20-14364)
- Red Sea Farms, a KAUST spin-out near Jeddah, grows crops with saltwater/seawater. It is food-focused, not forage — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/1895431/amp)

**Treated wastewater (TWW) fodder**
- KACST found that TWW supplied sufficient N, P, K and micronutrients, with fertiliser cost savings of about 45% for wheat and about 94% for alfalfa — [Aljaloud, WCSS](https://old.iuss.org/19th%20WCSS/Symposium/pdf/2053.pdf)
- Riyadh (Dirab station, 2013-15): a trial compared alfalfa cultivars on TWW against well water — [Agronomy 2019](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4395/9/11/729)
- A MEWA-hosted KSU paper (Ghanem et al., Saudi J Biol Sci 2021) covers the economics of directing TWW to green fodder, including priority regions — [MEWA PDF](https://www.mewa.gov.sa/ar/InformationCenter/OpenData/SiteAssets/Pages/OpenDataExamples/The%20economic%20dimension%20of%20directing%20treated%20wastewater%20to%20theproduction%20of%20green%20fodder%20in%20Saudi%20Arabia.pdf)
- A Makkah study (KAU) found that higher TWW concentration raised alfalfa and Sudan grass yields, but also raised toxic elements and faecal bacteria — [KAU](https://citis.kau.edu.sa/Files/305/Researches/19093_30302.docx)

**Hydroponic fodder**
- A Saudi study (Sustainability 2023, 15(1):822) found hydroponic barley green fodder out-yielded field barley and saved water. Higher fixed and variable costs made conventional cultivation more profitable, and the authors judged hydroponics unsuitable for small-scale farms — [Sustainability](https://dx.doi.org/10.3390/su15010822)
- International benchmark: a sprouting system cost about $0.40/lb DM against good hay at about $0.107/lb DM, roughly 3.7x — [U Minnesota WCROC](https://wcroc.cfans.umn.edu/node/856)

**Date palm by-products**
- MEWA, the National Center for Palms and Dates and TAQNIA studied a regional Qassim factory to turn about 500,000 t of palm waste into about 300,000 t/yr of products. The date and status are unverified — [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/saudi-arabia/qassim-reuses-date-palm-by-products-3002496)
- Traditional feed uses include cull dates, fronds, shees, date-press residue and date-seed meal — [Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/en/uses-of-palm-in-saudi-arabia)
- KSA date production exceeded 1.9 Mt in 2025 (SPA via the same source) — [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/saudi-arabia/qassim-reuses-date-palm-by-products-3002496)

**Camel feeds**
- An FAO-linked Saudi trial fed date blocks (50% dates, 40% wheat bran, 5% urea, 5% cement) to 14 young camels. They were judged safe as a partial feed substitute (2015) — [CIRAD](https://agritrop.cirad.fr/577435)
- Fattening dromedaries on molassed-date finishing diets with urea has been studied — [Cambridge](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-british-society-of-animal-science/article/abs/fattening-performance-of-dromedary-camels-fed-a-finishing-diet-containing-molassed-dates-supplemented-with-0-05-or-10-urea/8FA58D7B681F0352B1D2143CC4E3631D)

**Food waste to feed**
- Carbon CPU, a KAUST-linked startup, converts food waste into fatty acids and oils for livestock feed. Its founder says more than 90% of KSA food waste goes to landfill — [Arab News](https://arabnews.pk/node/1615941)
- KAUST has a pilot converting expired dairy and juice into feed chemicals — [KAUST](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/news/turning-spoiled-food-waste-into-commercial-products)
- Savola recycled about 27,737 t of oil waste in 2023, partly into poultry feed. MWAN and Oklin have a slaughterhouse-waste unit (up to 2,500 t/yr) producing feed ingredients — search summary; I could not verify the primary pages

**Incumbents (Almarai / NADEC)**
- Almarai has sourced 100% of its alfalfa from outside KSA since early 2019, in line with the green fodder cultivation ban. It took a roughly SAR 200m feed-cost hit and raised prices — [EFG Hermes](https://www.efghermesone.com/News/Index/almarai-secures-100-of-alfalfa-needs-from-imports); [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/584254?amp)
- 2025: Almarai reportedly produced about 257,784 t of feed on 27 overseas farms covering about 27,486 ha, and had about 189,000 cows. These are secondary figures from Mordor Intelligence — [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/saudi-arabia-alfalfa-market)
- FAO says the government plans to fully phase out domestic alfalfa production by 2027/28 — [FAO GIEWS](https://www.fao.org/giews/countrybrief/country.jsp?code=SA)

### Inferences
- Scale in KSA does not yet exist for insects, algae or halophyte forage. Each is at least two to four years from commercial volume.
- The 2027/28 alfalfa phase-out and the 90% poultry self-sufficiency target create policy pull for local roughage substitutes (TWW fodder, dates) and protein substitutes (BSF, algae).

### Gaps
- I found no Saudi Salicornia or saltbush forage project with data, and nothing on NEOM or Red Sea Global algae for feed.
- I found no named Saudi hydroponic fodder company with published costs.
- I found nothing on precision fermentation for feed in KSA, and no Almarai or NADEC novel-feed R&D announcements.
- I found no status for the Qassim date-waste factory.

## What are unit economics vs imported alfalfa/barley?

### Takeaway
I found very little hard Saudi unit-cost data. The only rigorous Saudi comparison (hydroponic barley) concludes that hydroponics loses to conventional fodder on cost despite its water savings. TWW fodder has documented fertiliser savings of up to 94% for alfalfa. Algae and BSF have no published Saudi costs.

### Cited Findings
- Saudi barley imports run about 4.0-4.2 Mt/yr (MY2025/26), almost entirely for feed — [World Grain](https://www.world-grain.com/articles/22195-saudi-arabia-to-import-more-barley-corn); [USDA via APEDA](https://agriexchange.apeda.gov.in/MarketReport/11351060520251227154317183.pdf)
- Wheat procurement price is SAR 1,750/t per USDA, while FAO says SAR 1,450/t (conflict unresolved). It is intended to shift farmers away from alfalfa — same sources; [FAO GIEWS](https://www.fao.org/giews/countrybrief/country.jsp?code=SA)
- Hydroponic fodder costs about 3.7x hay per lb DM (US benchmark). The Saudi study's net profit favoured conventional fodder — [WCROC](https://wcroc.cfans.umn.edu/node/856); [Sustainability 2023](https://dx.doi.org/10.3390/su15010822)
- TWW saves about 94% of fertiliser cost on alfalfa — [KACST](https://old.iuss.org/19th%20WCSS/Symposium/pdf/2053.pdf)
- Feed can reach 70% of livestock production cost in arid regions — [Taylor & Francis](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/e/9781439849477/chapters/10.1201%2Fb11874-27)

### Inferences
- Date by-products are likely the cheapest novel energy feed because the input is low-cost waste. They are low in protein, so they need urea or a protein supplement.
- BSF meal competes with fishmeal and soymeal, not with alfalfa or barley, so its relevant benchmark is protein price.

### Gaps
- I found no current SAR/t prices for imported alfalfa or barley, and no per-tonne costs for Saudi BSF, algae or date-feed products.

## Which have the most realistic opportunity for a new entrant?

### Takeaway
The most realistic near-term plays are date by-product feed blocks and pellets (abundant waste, proven trials, camel and sheep demand, low capex) and BSF, if partnered with poultry integrators such as Tanmiah or ARASCO, which are actively seeking partners. TWW fodder is constrained by regulation and pathogen risk. Hydroponics has weak economics. Algae is still state and KAUST-led and capex-heavy.

### Cited Findings
- Integrators are already signing MoUs with BSF startups (Tanmiah with PHYLA) — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/tanmiah-partners-with-phyla-and-recyclee-to-bolster-circular-economy-and-food-security-dduxf5pe)
- KSA date output is about 1.9 Mt, and the government is pursuing palm-waste valorisation — [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/saudi-arabia/qassim-reuses-date-palm-by-products-3002496)
- Date blocks are safe for camels — [CIRAD](https://agritrop.cirad.fr/577435)
- National landfill-diversion target: 90% by 2040 (cited by Carbon CPU) — [Arab News](https://arabnews.pk/node/1615941)

### Inferences
- Waste-feedstock models (dates, food waste to BSF) benefit from MWAN landfill policy and could earn gate fees as well as feed revenue.

### Gaps
- I found no MAGNiTT funding data for Saudi feed-tech startups, and no regulatory pathway details from SFDA for insect feed.
