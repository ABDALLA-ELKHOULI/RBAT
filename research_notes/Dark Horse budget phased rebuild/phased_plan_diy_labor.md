# Phased Rebuild Plan, DIY vs Pro Split, Saudi Legality and Labor: 2024+ Mustang Dark Horse (S650), Heavy Rear Damage

Research date: 2026-11-06. FX: 1 USD = 3.75 SAR. Anything labeled **[ESTIMATE]** is the researcher's judgment, not a cited figure.
Access limits: rts.i-car.com and mstaml.com could not be fetched from this session (network allowlist), so I-CAR and Saudi-forum material comes from search-result summaries, not full pages. No S650-specific Ford body repair manual pages were reachable; Ford's procedures sit behind Motorcraft Service / FDRS.

## 1. Correct order of repair phases

### Takeaway
Use this order: diagnose and power up (no key, so PATS/BCM work is needed early) → measure and repair structure on a bench → hang the mechanicals → finish the electrical → body, paint and wrap → lights, sensors, ADAS calibration and alignment last. Ford's own procedures (Motorcraft Service / FDRS) govern what may be sectioned. Public sources show only limited Mustang sectioning (outer quarter through the sail panel). They give conflicting rear-rail guidance, and Ford explicitly does not approve "clip" repairs.

### Cited Findings
- Ford's Mustang workshop manual was updated (January 2017, for 2015-2017 S550) to show outer quarter panel sectioning through the sail panel. The full procedure is at motorcraftservice.com. — [I-CAR OEM Linking Pin: Ford Mustang Quarter Panel Sectioning](https://rts.i-car.com/crn-526.html) (search-summary only)
- A Ford Mustang sectioning matrix states "No sectioning repairs can be carried out on inner reinforcement panels." — [I-CAR prm-9581](https://rts.i-car.com/prm-9581.html) (search summary)
- Rear rail: I-CAR's partial-replacement listing for the 2023 Mustang says "Rear Rail | Sectioning: OEM Warning, Do Not Section". A separate I-CAR response says Ford has a rear rail sectioning procedure, but it names no model year. **The sources conflict, and no S650-specific answer was found.** — [I-CAR prm-9122](https://rts.i-car.com/prm-9122.html); [I-CAR prm-9052](https://rts.i-car.com/prm-9052.html)
- I-CAR warns that the general replacement language in the 2007-2014 Mustang manual "is NOT in any way an authorization for general sectioning on the Ford Mustang." Sectioning is only allowed at documented locations. — [I-CAR: Did You Think That Ford Was Allowing General Sectioning?](https://rts.i-car.com/crn-57.html)
- B-pillar reinforcements on the 2023 Mustang are boron steel and "can not be sectioned". Replacement must be at factory joints. — [I-CAR prm-9122](https://rts.i-car.com/prm-9122.html)
- Ford's 2024 On Target bulletin says welding within 50 mm of a door hinge or striker location may compromise structural integrity. — [Ford On Target OT20242 (I-CAR PDF)](https://rts.i-car.com/images/pdf/oem-info/ford/on-target/OT20242.pdf)
- Ford previously issued Mustang sectioning guidelines after VeriFacts sent it photos of improper Mustang rear-rail repairs. — [BodyShop Business](https://www.bodyshopbusiness.com/?p=36543); [FenderBender](https://fenderbender.com/running-a-shop/operations/article/33020767/ford-issues-mustang-sectioning-guidelines-following-verifacts-request)
- Ford's collision position statements were reissued on 1 Oct 2025. Recycled or salvaged structural parts "have not been validated by Ford for structural repairs". Ford "does not approve or validate so-called 'clip' repairs". Aluminum structural repairs should be done at an aluminum-certified collision center. — [Ford statements index (I-CAR)](https://rts.i-car.com/oem-information/ford/ford-statements.html); [CollisionWeek, Oct 2025](https://collisionweek.com/2025/10/23/ford-details-updated-collision-repair-position-statements-technical-data-latest-target-magazine/); [Repairer Driven News](https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2018/03/22/ford-tweaks-auto-body-position-statements-use-it-as-an-opportunity-to-review-them/)
- Ford's ADAS position statement (dated 1 May 2026, as summarized in search) says the Blind Spot Information System depends on repair to Ford specs. Calibration procedures are in the Workshop Manual and require FDRS/IDS. — [Ford statement 85186 (I-CAR PDF)](https://rts.i-car.com/images/pdf/oem-info/ford/statements/85186.pdf)
- Generic Ford-family BSI entries call for calibration "if body panel repairs are made near the BSI mounting location" or "if body panel that sensor mounts to is repaired or replaced". These are not confirmed specifically for the S650. — [I-CAR: Repairing Blind Spot Sensor Mounting Locations](https://rts.i-car.com/collision-repair-news/repairing-blind-spot-sensor-mounting-locations.html); [FenderBender on BSM radar calibration](https://www.fenderbender.com/running-a-shop/technology/blog/55050197/blind-spot-radar-calibration-post-collision)
- On owner forums, a rear-ended 2024 GT drew comments that "$11K does not even start to address the damage". Estimates hinged on whether the rail needed repair. — [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3284420); [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3284629)
- A Copart-type 2024 GT with rear damage had "both of the rear wheels pushed out of position", meaning costly suspension and subframe damage. — [Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/2023/11/this-2024-ford-mustang-gt-went-from-showroom-to-salvage-in-just-16-miles/)

### Inferences
- **Recommended phase order [ESTIMATE / synthesis]:**
  1. **Diagnose and power up.** Fit a battery or a regulated power supply. Isolate the cut rear harness so it cannot short. Scan all modules (FORScan or a pro tool) and save the DTC list and as-built data. Read the odometer from the IPC/BCM. Solve the key/PATS problem. Check the hood and front for hidden damage. Document everything before buying parts.
  2. **Structural.** Strip the rear. Get a bench/3D measurement against Ford dimensions. Pull, then replace or section **only at Ford-documented locations**. The trunk floor, rear rails, quarters and C-pillar on this car sit at the edge of what Ford allows. Expect full rail/floor replacement at factory seams rather than sectioning. If the damage reaches inner reinforcements, sectioning is not allowed.
  3. **Mechanical.** Rear subframe, IRS, differential, half-shafts, knuckles, springs/dampers (MagneRide on the Dark Horse), brakes and exhaust. Subframe mounting points must be confirmed against the bench measurements first.
  4. **Electrical.** Repair or replace the rear harness. Plug in the BSM radars, camera, lights and fuel pump module. Clear and recheck DTCs.
  5. **Body, paint and wrap.** Seam sealer, corrosion protection, primer and paint (or at least e-coat/primer under the wrap), then the matte wrap.
  6. **Trim, lights, glass, calibration, alignment.** Rear glass bonding, tail lights, bumper cover, then BSM radar, rear camera and parking-aid calibration, a four-wheel alignment, and a road test and final scan.
- Do structural work before mechanical so the subframe hard points are verified. Do the alignment last, because pulling the body moves the hard points.

### Gaps
- No S650 (2024+) Ford sectioning pages were found for the rear rail, trunk floor, quarter or C-pillar. The owner or shop must pull the current procedures from Motorcraft Service (paid subscription) for this VIN.
- I could not confirm the S650 body's material mix (which rear parts are boron, UHSS or aluminum).

## 2. DIY-friendly jobs vs pro-only jobs

### Takeaway
An amateur can safely do bolt-on removal and installation: bumper cover, tail lights, trim, interior cleaning and stripping, and the exhaust. Subframe and suspension bolt-up and basic harness splicing with a pin-out are feasible with supervision. Structural pulling and welding, SRS, key/PATS on the S650, glass bonding, ADAS calibration and alignment need pro tools, credentials or certification.

### Cited Findings
- FORScan's free Standard license covers diagnostics. The Extended license is needed for configuration, programming and PATS. Third-party guides put it at roughly $20 a year or about $50 lifetime, with a free 2-month trial. FORScan 2.4.x requires a paid license. — [OBDAdvisor FORScan review](https://obdadvisor.com/forscan-review/); [FORScan 2.4.x release thread](https://community.cyanlabs.net/t/forscan-software-version-2-4-x-releases-and-information-2-4-22-beta-2026-02-07/4043)
- 2024 S650 support in FORScan is mixed in owner reports. One says the vendor site lists 2024 Ford as unsupported. Another says it works but has issues programming the BCMB and LDCM modules. A third says they programmed three 2024 Mustangs. — [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3288540); [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3288557); [FORScan 2.3.64 notes](https://community.cyanlabs.net/t/forscan-2-3-64-released-2024-12-16/16325)
- FORScan's PATS programming procedure requires an existing working key (the "insert each key in Run" method) and the Extended license. — [FORScan forum: How to program PATS](https://forum.forscan.org/viewtopic.php?p=11174)
- For all-keys-lost on newer Fords, FDRS with J2534 hardware and NASTF credentials is the recommended route. One poster says a non-dealer FDRS login cannot program keys. — [diag.net thread](https://diag.net/discussion/snnl3h2a6rggi9eyz2ka4ivgp2); [The Group Training Academy: Key Programming Using FDRS](https://thegrouptrainingacademy.com/shop/lbt-375-key-programming-using-the-ford-fdrs/)
- A locksmith site claims Ford TSB 23-2017 covers 2023-2024 S650 BCMs dropping programmed keys. This is unverified against a Ford source. — [ezkyz 2024 Mustang](https://ezkyz.com/vc/car-key/ford/mustang/2024/)
- Autel aftermarket coverage of the 2024 Mustang for all-keys-lost is unconfirmed. One report says the KM100 and Topdon tools only cover a different Ford up to 2019. — [obdii365 blog](https://blog.obdii365.com/2024/04/23/autel-km100-ford-f-150-2019-security-authentication-failed-solution/)
- Ford's BSI calibration requires FDRS/IDS procedures. — [Ford statement 85186](https://rts.i-car.com/images/pdf/oem-info/ford/statements/85186.pdf)
- Ford recommends that aluminum structural repairs be done at an FCCN aluminum-certified center. — [CollisionWeek Oct 2025](https://collisionweek.com/2025/10/23/ford-details-updated-collision-repair-position-statements-technical-data-latest-target-magazine/)

### Inferences
- **DIY-friendly [ESTIMATE]:** teardown and labeling, parts inventory, interior removal, cleaning and drying (cut harness, possible glass and fluids), removing and fitting the bumper cover, tail lights, trunk trim, sill plates and rear seats, a cat-back/axle-back exhaust, wheel and tire swaps, battery and power-supply hookup, and running FORScan scans and saving logs.
- **DIY with mentoring or a torque spec sheet [ESTIMATE]:** bolting up the subframe, IRS and differential once the body is measured true (needs a transmission jack, torque-to-yield bolts replaced per Ford, alignment of the subframe locating pins); dampers and springs; brake lines and bleeding; and a harness splice repair **if** a Ford wiring diagram and pin-out is available. Use OEM-style crimp-and-heat-shrink splices, never twist-and-tape. Do not splice CAN, airbag or radar circuits casually; Ford typically specifies twisted-pair rules for CAN.
- **Pro only [ESTIMATE, consistent with Ford statements above]:** bench measuring, pulling, structural welding and resistance spot welding (STRSW), riveting and bonding; anything in the SRS/restraints (rear-impact sensors, belt pretensioners, ORC); all-keys-lost PATS/BCM on the S650 (FDRS or a locksmith with S650 coverage); rear glass urethane bonding; BSM radar, rear camera and parking-aid calibration (FDRS); four-wheel alignment; MagneRide module setup; and paint/e-coat corrosion protection.

### Gaps
- No confirmed list of S650 modules FORScan can program in 2026. Verify on forscan.org before relying on it.
- No Saudi locksmith price for S650 all-keys-lost was found.

## 3. Rear clip / half-cut grafting: method, safety, Saudi legality, insurance, resale

### Takeaway
Ford's current position is that it "does not approve or validate" clip repairs and has not validated salvaged structural parts. In Saudi Arabia, structural changes done without the official procedure are a Muroor violation. A car removed from the register as damaged cannot be re-registered, and SASO runs a separate modified-vehicle inspection. I found **no** Saudi rule that explicitly permits or forbids grafting a rear clip from another VIN. Treat it as high-risk until the Muroor confirms in writing.

### Cited Findings
- Ford does not approve or validate "clip" repairs (joining sections of vehicles). Recycled or salvaged unibody/structural parts are not validated for structural repair (statements reissued 1 Oct 2025). — [CollisionWeek](https://collisionweek.com/2025/10/23/ford-details-updated-collision-repair-position-statements-technical-data-latest-target-magazine/); [Ford statements (I-CAR)](https://rts.i-car.com/oem-information/ford/ford-statements.html)
- Saudi Traffic (Muroor) has stated that any modification to a vehicle's body/structure without the official procedures is a violation. "Modification" covers changes that substantially alter the vehicle, even ones that hide its previous features. — reported in search summaries of [mstaml.com](https://www.mstaml.com/f273081) and [rukn-services: فحص السيارات المعدلة](https://rukn-services.com/فحص-السيارات-المعدلة/) (secondary sources, could not be fetched to verify)
- SASO offers a modified-vehicle inspection. Reported fees are 300 SAR without a change of vehicle characteristics and 500 SAR with one. Passing the periodic MVPI (Fahas) does not replace it. The process is: request at Muroor → referral → payment → appointment → inspection → approval. — same secondary sources as above; **fees unverified against SASO or MVPI official pages**
- Muroor rules (as reported): a vehicle de-registered because it is wrecked (تالفة) cannot be re-registered. Wrecked vehicles are dropped via a licensed scrapyard (تشليح) or crusher, and the plates and registration are surrendered. — [Almuraba](https://www.almuraba.net/?p=591261); [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/8gg3zf)
- A repair permit (إذن إصلاح) can be issued through Absher without visiting Muroor. — [Almuraba](https://www.almuraba.net/%d9%83%d9%85-%d8%b1%d8%b3%d9%88%d9%85-%d8%a7%d8%b3%d9%82%d8%a7%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%81%d8%a9-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d8%b9/) (2022-23 era reporting)
- MVPI announcements page (official periodic inspection operator). — [mvpi.com.sa announcements](https://mvpi.com.sa/en/announcements)
- VIN cloning (using one car's identity to cover another car) leads to confiscation where it is discovered. This is general background on why mixed-VIN structures draw scrutiny. — [VIN cloning (Wikipedia mirror)](https://wiki.riteme.site/wiki/VIN_cloning); [Carz.com.my](https://www.carz.com.my/2023/06/vehicle-theft-syndicate-replicates-serial-numbers-on-stolen-engines)

### Inferences
- **Method [ESTIMATE, general trade practice]:** a donor rear clip is cut at a joint line (often the rocker, B-pillar or roof and the floor ahead of the rear seat). It is fitted on a jig, with sleeves or backing inserts at the cuts, then welded and sealed. On a modern unibody this crosses B-pillar and rocker boron or UHSS that Ford says cannot be sectioned. That makes a full clip non-compliant with Ford's procedures and doubtful for crash safety, especially for the rear-seat and fuel-tank zones.
- **Safer legal path [ESTIMATE]:** use new Ford OE structural panels (rails, floor, quarters) installed at factory seams or Ford-documented section points. Get the Absher repair permit before work starts. Keep the original VIN-bearing structure. Keep invoices, before/after measurement printouts and photos for the Muroor, SASO, Fahas and insurer.
- **If a donor clip is used anyway [ESTIMATE]:** it may count as a "structural modification" requiring the Muroor procedure and SASO inspection. Any donor VIN stamp or label must be dealt with openly, never hidden. Get the Muroor's written position first.
- **Insurance and resale [ESTIMATE]:** Saudi insurers and buyers commonly check accident history (for example via Najm, Absher vehicle reports and the Fahas history). A visible structural or clip repair will sharply cut resale value. Some insurers may decline comprehensive cover or require an inspection. I found no cited Saudi source on this.

### Gaps
- No official Muroor or SASO text found on installing a rear section from another VIN, or on how "salvage" is defined for imported US auction cars.
- I could not verify the SASO fee figures on an official page.
- No Saudi insurer policy text on structurally repaired or clipped vehicles was found.
- Whether this car is a US import with a salvage title (which affects SASO/customs conformity) was not researched here.

## 5. DIY tools and costs

### Takeaway
The core DIY kit is the OBDLink EX plus a FORScan Extended license (about $60 + $20-50). Add a battery maintainer or power supply, a floor jack, stands, a transmission jack, torque wrenches, a crimp kit and a multimeter. A rough total is about $1,000-1,800 (3,750-6,750 SAR) [ESTIMATE].

### Cited Findings
- OBDLink EX costs $59.95 in the US and AED 379 in the UAE. It lets FORScan reach all CAN buses (HS and MS-CAN) through an electronic switch. — [OBDLink EX product page](https://www.obdlink.com/products/obdlink-ex/); [Desertcart AE](https://www.desertcart.ae/products/175613666-obdlink-ex.md)
- FORScan Extended license: about $20 a year, about $50 lifetime (third-party figures). — [OBDAdvisor](https://obdadvisor.com/forscan-review/)
- The EX hardware has not been refreshed in about 7 years. FORScan coverage for 2025+ is "best-effort". — [OBDAdvisor EX review](https://obdadvisor.com/obdlink-ex-adapter-review/)

### Inferences (**[ESTIMATE]**, typical KSA retail)
- Windows laptop (if needed): 1,500-3,000 SAR
- Battery maintainer / 13.5 V power supply (needed for programming): 300-900 SAR
- 3-ton low-profile floor jack plus 4 x 6-ton jack stands: 600-1,200 SAR

- 3/8" and 1/2" torque wrenches plus a metric socket and breaker-bar set: 600-1,500 SAR

## 6. Rough cash flow per phase

### Takeaway
All figures here are **[ESTIMATE]**, labor only plus small items. Major parts (Dark Horse IRS/diff, rails, quarters, harness, lights, BSM radars, glass) are covered by other researchers and excluded. Owner DIY mainly cuts the Phase 3, 5 and 6 labor lines. Structural work and calibration remain pro costs.

### Cited Findings
- Owner reports suggest US rear-collision estimates for 2024 GTs well above $11K once rails are involved, which shows how large the structural line can be. — [Mustang7G](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3284420)

### Inferences (**[ESTIMATE]**, SAR, labor and consumables, excluding major parts)
| Phase | Pro labor if outsourced | With owner DIY | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Diagnose, power, key/PATS | 2,500-6,000 | 2,000-5,000 (tools ~600 + key job) | Do first; it decides whether the car is economical |
| 2 Structural (bench, rails, floor, quarters, C-pillar) | 15,000-35,000 | same (pro only) | Biggest cash outflow; get Absher repair permit before starting |
| 3 Mechanical bolt-up (subframe, IRS, diff, suspension, exhaust) | 2,000-5,000 | 500-1,500 | DIY-friendly after structure is verified |
| 4 Electrical (rear harness) | 1,500-5,000 | 800-3,000 | Splice repair cheaper than a new harness; follow a Ford diagram |
| 5 Body prep, paint, matte wrap | 10,000-20,000 | 9,000-18,000 | Wrap is pro work for a matte finish |
| 6 Trim, lights, glass, calibration, alignment, final scan | 2,500-6,000 | 1,800-4,500 | Calibration and glass bonding stay pro |
| Legal/inspection (Muroor, SASO, Fahas) | ~500-1,500 | same | SASO modified-vehicle fee reported at 300-500 SAR (unverified) |
| **Total labor (excl. major parts)** | **~34,000-78,000 SAR (~$9,000-21,000)** | **~30,000-68,000 SAR (~$8,000-18,000)** | Hold a 20-30% contingency |

- Cash-flow sequencing: spend small on Phase 1 first (about 1-3% of budget) to confirm the key, the odometer and that the modules are alive. Then make a go/no-go decision before committing roughly 40-50% of total spend to Phase 2.
