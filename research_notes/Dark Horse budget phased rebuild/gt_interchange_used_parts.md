# S650 Mustang GT / EcoBoost to Dark Horse Parts Interchange and Used GT Part Prices (2024-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-06. Currency: 1 USD = 3.75 SAR. Anything marked **[ESTIMATE]** is my inference, not a sourced figure.

Research limits: WebFetch could not open mustang7g.com, fordpartsgiant.com, fordpartsdeal.com or redlineautoparts.com from this session because DNS was blocked. Those findings come from search-engine snippets of the pages, not from full page reads. I found no Ford parts catalog page that lists GT and Dark Horse part numbers side by side for body structure. Treat every "shared" claim as something to confirm by VIN with a Ford parts counter. In the Gulf region that means Al Jazirah Ford (KSA), or a US dealer using the Dark Horse VIN.

## 1. Body structure (quarters, rails, trunk floor, decklid, rear body panel, C-pillar, rear glass, body-in-white)

### Takeaway
No source says the Dark Horse has a unique rear body structure. Ford's new-part quarter panel for the 2024-2025 coupe (PR3Z-6327841-A) is listed only as "Coupe", with no trim split. Aftermarket decklids are sold as one part for EcoBoost, GT and Dark Horse. The rear-end differences that sources describe are bolt-on items: the spoiler, badge, valance/diffuser, exhaust tips and the rear axle cooler. A GT or EcoBoost coupe donor is therefore very likely a valid source of rear sheet metal. This is not proven at the part-number level.

### Cited Findings
- New OEM quarter panel assembly PR3Z-6327841-A is described as a rear body-side quarter panel for 2024-2025 Mustang **Coupe**. Sale price $1,574.95 (MSRP $2,243.52), which is about SAR 5,906 (MSRP SAR 8,413). The listing says "No application/vehicle fitment information available", mentions a fuel pocket, and notes the "Part has related component that cannot be reused/reinstalled" — [Ford OEM Parts Direct](https://www.fordoempartsdirect.com/oem-parts/ford-quarter-panel-assembly-pr3z6327841a)
- LMR says the rear differences between Dark Horse and GT are "very subtle", mainly the spoiler, badge, valance and exhaust tips — [LMR: Dark Horse vs GT](https://lmr.com/products/dark-horse-vs-gt-whats-the-difference)
- An Anderson Composites carbon decklid ($1,529.10) is listed as fitting S650 EcoBoost, GT and Dark Horse. That implies a common decklid shape — [FFPerformance](https://ffperformance.co/product/anderson-composites-2024-ford-mustang-double-sided-carbon-fiber-type-oe-rear-decklid)
- A parts retailer says Dark Horse gets its own fascia, grille mesh, center-exit rear bumper and unique wing, with the Coyote V8 shared. Quarter panels are not named as unique — [FFPerformance S650 catalog](https://ffperformance.co/shop/ford/mustang/2024-2026-mustang/page/21/)
- A Ford-oriented extension part (quarter panel extension PR3Z-6328058-A) exists as a separate small part. Fitment details could not be fetched — [Ford Parts Giant](https://www.fordpartsgiant.com/parts/ford-extension-quarter-panel-re_pr3z-6328058-a.html)
- A recycler listing shows a used 2024 Mustang **coupe** left quarter panel assembly, Grade A, 11k miles. No price was shown — [URG/AutoPartSearch](https://demo10.autopartsearch.com/parts/FORD/MUSTANG/888R/2024/QUARTER_PANEL_ASSY/1001995017)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** The Dark Horse is a trim of the same S650 coupe body built on the same Flat Rock line. The rear rails, trunk floor, quarters, C-pillar (part of the quarter/body-side), rear body panel and rear glass are very likely shared with GT and EcoBoost coupes. Any trim difference is likely limited to holes and brackets: spoiler mount holes in the decklid, valance or diffuser mounting points, and diff-cooler plumbing brackets.
- **[ESTIMATE]** Convertible parts will not interchange with the coupe structure. Take donors from **coupes** only.
- Ford notes "related component that cannot be reused", so a new quarter panel probably needs new single-use clips, adhesives or brackets. Sectioning a donor quarter at OEM-recommended sectioning locations avoids buying the full $1,575 panel.
- The left C-pillar on a Mustang coupe is part of the body-side outer/quarter. A donor quarter cut generously at the roof/A-B pillar sectioning points would cover it.

### Gaps
- I found no Ford-catalog side-by-side comparison of GT and Dark Horse part numbers for rear rails, trunk pan, rear body panel, wheelhouses or rear glass. The Ford catalog sites were blocked from fetch.
- I found no source on whether the Dark Horse has extra factory structural reinforcement at the rear. The Dark Horse front has unique bracing and coolers, but nothing was found for the rear.
- No Ford Collision Repair (OEM sectioning) procedure for the S650 was found in this session.

## 2. Rear bumper / diffuser and trunk spoiler

### Takeaway
The rear valance/diffuser and wing are Dark Horse-specific. The Dark Horse uses a unique center/lower-exit valance cutout sized for 5" tips and the rear axle cooler. A GT rear bumper cover and diffuser should physically mount on the same body, since the cover itself appears shared. The fit is not confirmed. A forum poster warned that a GT diffuser and exhaust on a Dark Horse might clash with the differential cooler. The GT spoiler or no spoiler on a GT decklid is a cosmetic choice. The Dark Horse PP spoiler is sold by Ford Performance for GT and EcoBoost.

### Cited Findings
- The FFP catalog lists a center-exit cutout as unique to Dark Horse, with dual-exit cutouts on other trims — [FFPerformance S650 catalog](https://ffperformance.co/shop/ford/mustang/2024-2026-mustang/page/3/)
- Forum reports say a Dark Horse diffuser on a GT should fit. One poster warned a GT diffuser and exhaust on a Dark Horse "may cause clearance issues with the differential cooler" but was unsure. Another says the Dark Horse valance differs because of the coolers, aero design and tip size — [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3290775); [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3486006)
- One GT-swap post says a plastic brace between the bumper and rear valance has tabs that must be cut off to install — [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3456479)
- Some aftermarket diffusers fit GT and EcoBoost but **not** Dark Horse, for example FFP's finned diffuser. That confirms the Dark Horse lower rear differs — [FFPerformance finned diffuser](https://ffperformance.co/product/2024-2026-ford-mustang-s650-finned-rear-bumper-diffuser/)
- Ford Performance sells the Dark Horse Performance Pack spoiler with Gurney flap (M-16600-S65PPG) for 2024-2026 S650 GT and EcoBoost, with hardware and instructions — [Steeda](https://www.steeda.com/ford-performance-M-16600-S65PPG-mustang-dark-horse-pp-spoiler-w/-gurney-flap)
- Aftermarket Dark Horse-style spoilers bolt into the existing holes on cars that have a factory spoiler. Cars without one need drilling, and templates are supplied — [AmericanMuscle SpeedForm DH spoiler](https://www.americanmuscle.com/speedform-mustang-dark-horse-style-rear-spoiler-unpainted-445941.html); [AmericanMuscle MP Concepts](https://www.americanmuscle.com/mp-concepts-mustang-dark-horse-style-rear-spoiler-485597.html)
- Ford Performance exhaust systems are reported to include the Dark Horse valance — [Mustang7G thread](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/threads/active-exhaust-dark-horse-vs-gt.159222)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** Cheapest path: take the GT rear bumper cover, GT valance/diffuser and GT exhaust from the donor as one matched set, so the tip cutouts match. Then confirm clearance to the Dark Horse diff cooler, or omit the cooler (see section 3). Mixing a Dark Horse valance with a GT exhaust, or the reverse, will misalign the tips.
- **[ESTIMATE]** If the donor decklid has spoiler holes in a different pattern than the Dark Horse wing, either fit the donor's spoiler or fill the holes. Since the Dark Horse decklid is crushed anyway, using the donor decklid with its own spoiler is the simplest option.

### Gaps
- No OEM part numbers were found for the Dark Horse versus GT rear bumper cover. Whether the cover itself, separate from the valance, is shared remains unconfirmed.

## 3. Differential, subframe, halfshafts, knuckles, rear brakes

### Takeaway
The Dark Horse uses a Torsen LSD, 3.73 with the manual and 3.55 with the auto, plus a factory rear axle cooler. The GT Performance Pack uses a Torsen 3.73 (manual). Sources say the GT PP rear brakes, 4-piston Brembo calipers and rotors, are identical to the Dark Horse's. A GT PP manual axle would mechanically match a Dark Horse manual. A GT auto 3.55 Torsen would match a Dark Horse auto. A non-PP GT open or Traction-Lok 3.15 unit would bolt in but changes the final drive, so the PCM would need recalibrating for the ratio. The Dark Horse cooler is an extra system: a PCM-controlled pump, relay and sensor in Ford's design. If the donor axle has no cooler provisions, expect a DTC unless the cooler is transferred or the configuration is changed.

### Cited Findings
- Dark Horse spec: 3.55 Torsen LSD with the automatic, 3.73 Torsen with the manual — [Ford.com 2025 Dark Horse](https://www.ford.com/cars/mustang/2025/models/dark-horse-premium/)
- The Dark Horse has a standard rear axle cooler, transmission cooler (manual only; the auto omits it), auxiliary engine oil cooler and brake ducts — [Hagerty](https://www.hagerty.com/media/?p=252981)
- The GT Performance Package includes a "TORSEN Differential with 3.73 Axle Ratio (Manual)" — [Ford Middle East packages](https://www.me.ford.com/en/are/cars/mustang/features/packages.html)
- A Tasca parts listing ties the 3.73 differential case entry to "With differential cooler" and Torsen. This is a parts-fitment listing — [Tasca Parts](https://www.tascaparts.com/v-2023-ford-mustang--gt-premium--5-0l-v8-gas/universals-and-rear-axle--oil-cooler)
- In Ford's factory differential-cooler design (S550 workshop manual), the PCM monitors diff oil temperature and switches an electric pump through a relay to pump oil through a cooler — [S550 Workshop Manual 205-00](https://www.reichwein.it/manuals/Mustang_2014/Workshop_Manual_Wiring_Diagrams/Resources/Service_Manual/seb50003.htm)
- One forum member found a 2024 GT Premium had no diff cooler. Another asked whether adding the cooler harness to a GT requires as-built changes. This suggests the cooler is trim/option-coded — [Mustang7G post](https://mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3355588); [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3435497)
- The Ford Performance Super 8.8 Torsen (M-4204-MT) is listed for Mustang GT and Dark Horse and requires 34-spline axles. It is not for 3.15 gears. Forum advice is to swap the whole pumpkin, and changing ratio needs the PCM recalibrated — [Ford Performance M-4204-MT](https://performanceparts.ford.com/part/M-4204-MT); [Mustang6G thread](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/upgrade-to-torsen-differential.195573/)
- Rear brakes: a forum post says the Dark Horse and GT PP have identical front and rear calipers and rear rotors (6-piston front, 4-piston rear Brembo). Only the front rotors differ: the Dark Horse uses 2-piece, the GT PP 1-piece, and they interchange. An earlier 2022 speculation said otherwise — [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3205515); [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3190638)
- Aftermarket rear rotors are sold for Dark Horse, EcoBoost and GT with the factory 4-piston Brembo rear — [CJ Pony Parts BR198P](https://www.cjponyparts.com/brake-rotor-pair-mustang-gt-dark-horse-premium/p/BR198P/)
- Halfshafts: no source confirms that Dark Horse halfshafts are unique. Forum posters speculated they match the GT350's. A reply attributed to a Ford engineer suggested GT350 shafts would be a "moderate upgrade" over the Dark Horse's, which implies the Dark Horse uses standard-style shafts — [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3342554)
- The Dark Horse has a bigger rear sway bar and new shocks compared with the GT — [Top Gear](https://www.topgear.com/car-reviews/usa/ford/mustang-dark-horse-review-2023)
- A used S650 rear subframe was listed at $499.99 (about SAR 1,875), taken from a donor described as a "2024 GT Dark Horse coupe" with about 6k miles. The seller groups GT and Dark Horse together — [Redline Auto Parts (search snippet)](https://www.redlineautoparts.com/ford/2024-current-s650-mustang/2024-2025-ford-mustang-gt-5-0-s650-oem-rear-subframe-crossmember-fm201)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** Best donor: a GT **Performance Pack** with the **same transmission** as the Dark Horse. That gives the same ratio, a Torsen and the same Brembo rear brakes, so the brakes are plug-and-play. A GT auto donor (3.55 Torsen per the brief) suits a Dark Horse auto.
- **[ESTIMATE]** If the donor ratio differs from the Dark Horse's (for example 3.15), the speedometer, ABS and traction control need the tire/axle ratio updated in the as-built data (FORScan or dealer IDS/FDRS). This is a programming job, not a hardware problem.
- **[ESTIMATE]** If the donor has no factory diff cooler, either move the Dark Horse cooler, pump and harness pigtail over (if salvageable), or have the as-built changed to disable the cooler. Otherwise a pump/sensor-circuit DTC is likely. This is unverified for S650.
- **[ESTIMATE]** A non-PP GT has smaller rear brakes, which would not clear or match the Dark Horse 19" wheels' expectations. Use PP rear brakes, or keep the Dark Horse brakes if they survived.

### Gaps
- No catalog confirmation was found that Dark Horse and GT PP rear knuckles, subframe, toe links and halfshafts share part numbers.
- No confirmed S650 procedure was found for running a Dark Horse without its diff cooler.

## 4. Suspension: MagneRide on GT PP vs Dark Horse; non-MagneRide shocks

### Takeaway
The Dark Horse has MagneRide standard. Forum evidence suggests the MagneRide shocks and struts carried over to S650 and are shared hardware, with Dark Horse and Handling Pack differences sitting in the calibration. This is not confirmed by part number. Fitting non-MagneRide GT shocks to a MagneRide car without a delete or simulator kit throws suspension faults. S550 owners reported a "Suspension Failure – Speeds Limited to 80 MPH" message. Plug-in delete kits exist and some are listed for 2024-2025 Dark Horse.

### Cited Findings
- The Dark Horse comes with MagneRide and new shocks compared with the GT — [Top Gear](https://www.topgear.com/car-reviews/usa/ford/mustang-dark-horse-review-2023)
- On Mustang7G, one member said S650 MagneRide struts and shocks are carryover with only the computer upgraded, and that non-MagneRide units are new. Another member said "They are not" carryover. Others note S650 MagneRide calibrations are revised — [Mustang7G thread](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/threads/2024-magneride-delete-disable-modules.164012); [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3220384)
- One forum user said the Handling Pack Dark Horse has "a more aggressive calibration for track focused magneride". This is anecdotal — [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3339865)
- S550 GT MagneRide part numbers are JR3C-18B060-UB (front) and KR3C-18W003-TA (rear). Suffixes vary — [Mustang6G post](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3113975)
- An AmericanMuscle MagneRide shock and height-sim kit is listed for 2018-2026 GT and Dark Horse — [AmericanMuscle](https://www.americanmuscle.com/mustang-magneride-shock-height-sim-full-vehicle-kit-ford-mustang-sim-full.html)
- The Mont Motorsports SDE MagneRide/ride-height delete kit is listed for 2024-2025 Mustang GT and Dark Horse, and claims to avoid dash errors — [Mont Motorsports](https://montmotorsports.com/sde-mag-ride-ride-height-simulator-delete-kit-ford-mustang-gt350-gt500-2024-mustang-gt-darkhorse)
- The KellTrac plug-in delete costs $399.99 (about SAR 1,500), is listed for 2015-2025, and is for non-MagneRide shocks only — [Beefcake Racing / KellTrac](https://www.beefcakeracing.com/kelltrac-kti-mrd6g-4)
- On a 2018 car, removing the struts and disconnecting the sensors gave "Suspension Failure - Speeds Limited to 80 MPH". One owner said a $200 SDE kit worked only intermittently. No working FORScan disable method was found — [Mustang6G MagneRide code removal](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/magneride-code-removal.149155/)
- Ford's MagneRide Handling Pack fits only MagneRide cars and excludes Mach 1 because of trim-specific calibration. This shows Ford ties MagneRide calibration to the trim — [Ford Performance M-9602-M](https://PerformanceParts.Ford.com/part/M-9602-M)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** Rear shocks from a MagneRide GT PP donor are the lowest-risk replacement, likely the same hardware. The Dark Horse modules keep the Dark Horse calibration.
- **[ESTIMATE]** Non-MagneRide GT shocks are cheap. On a Dark Horse they need a delete/simulator kit ($200-$400, SAR 750-1,500), and some kits are reported unreliable. They also reduce the car's resale and authenticity. Mixing MagneRide fronts with passive rears is not advisable.

### Gaps
- No Ford part-number confirmation that GT PP and Dark Horse rear MagneRide dampers are identical.
- Rear ride-height sensors and their brackets are typically on the rear suspension, and could be damaged in this crash. No S650 part numbers or prices were found for them.

## 5. Exhaust

### Takeaway
The Dark Horse has active valve exhaust standard. Its rear section differs from the GT active exhaust at least in the tips (5" vs 4"), the tip position and layout, and possibly the hangers and resonator. The underbody pipes appear to share the GT layout. A GT active catback, or the GT's valved mufflers, would physically fit the Dark Horse body. The tips would not match the Dark Horse valance, so fit the GT valance with it. Whether a GT active exhaust bolts up exactly, including the valve actuator connectors, is not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Forum: the main difference may be the tip size, 4" on GT and 5" on Dark Horse. Hangers before the muffler are oriented differently on Borla systems. A poster recalled a different resonator. All of this is unverified — [Mustang7G: active exhaust DH vs GT](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/threads/active-exhaust-dark-horse-vs-gt.159222)
- All 2024-2026 GTs use the same 2.75-inch exhaust layout with optional Active Valve Performance Exhaust. The Dark Horse has active exhaust standard — [LMR 2024 Mustang exhaust](https://lmr.com/products/2024-mustang-exhaust); [LMR DH vs GT](https://lmr.com/products/dark-horse-vs-gt-whats-the-difference)
- Borla sells separate Dark Horse exhaust part numbers, which implies different fitment at the rear — [Borla Dark Horse exhaust](https://www.borla.com/2024-2025-ford-mustang-dark-horse-exhaust-systems)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** Taking the donor GT's active catback together with the donor's valance and bumper is the cleanest match. A non-active GT exhaust would leave the Dark Horse valve circuits unplugged, which may set DTCs unless the valves are simulated or the as-built is changed.

### Gaps
- No used S650 GT active catback prices were found in this session.

## 6. Rear wiring harnesses

### Takeaway
I found no source comparing S650 rear body harness part numbers by trim. Option content creates real differences. The Dark Horse has a rear diff cooler pump circuit, as shown in section 3, plus MagneRide, active exhaust valves, and possibly BLIS/cross-traffic and rear-camera options. A GT donor harness must come from a car with the same option content, or the Dark Horse-only circuits must be spliced in.

### Cited Findings
- Adding the diff-cooler harness to a GT raised the question of as-built changes. This implies the cooler circuit is not on every GT harness — [Mustang7G post](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3435497)
- Ford's factory diff cooler system uses a PCM-driven relay, pump and temperature sensor (S550 manual), so it needs dedicated wiring — [S550 Workshop Manual 205-00](https://www.reichwein.it/manuals/Mustang_2014/Workshop_Manual_Wiring_Diagrams/Resources/Service_Manual/seb50003.htm)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** Prefer a GT **Premium + Performance Pack + active exhaust + MagneRide** donor to maximize harness match. Alternatively, repair the Dark Horse's own cut harness with OEM-style splices (Ford permits harness repair with its repair kits), which is often cheaper than replacing a whole body harness.

### Gaps
- No S650 harness part numbers or trim breakdowns were found. Confirm by VIN or harness label.

- **[ESTIMATE]** Buying a whole front-hit 2024 GT (ideally a PP coupe with the same transmission) at about $12k-$16k plus Copart fees (about $800-$1,500) is the most practical source. It provides a matched rear clip: quarters, rails, trunk pan, decklid, rear glass, rear bumper and valance, tail lamps, the IRS with axle, halfshafts and brakes, the active exhaust and the rear harness. The remaining GT front parts and engine can be resold. Total landed cost in KSA would be roughly $14k-$19k before shipping. Shipping a car from the US to KSA plus Saudi customs (about 5% duty plus 15% VAT) would add several thousand dollars. That last figure is unsourced; another researcher may cover it.
- **[ESTIMATE]** Buying the parts separately (two quarters cut from donors, a rear diff, a subframe, a pair of halfshafts, a pair of tail lamps, an exhaust) would likely cost $4k-$7k in US parts alone. That excludes the trunk floor, rails and harness, which are hard to find separately.
