## Q1. China (AliExpress, Alibaba, DHgate, Made-in-China, Taobao): what exists, prices, quality, shipping and SASO/SABER rules

### Takeaway
Chinese sellers clearly make S650 cosmetic and bolt-on parts: aero, diffusers, lips, carbon hoods and spoilers, trim, tail-light covers, clone key fobs. Many listings exclude the Dark Horse or Performance Pack bumpers, so check trim fitment. I found no evidence of Chinese S650 structural sheet metal (quarter panels, rails, trunk floor). Importing auto parts to KSA can trigger SABER, especially for lighting, glass, brakes and seatbelts. That is the main hidden risk of buying from China.

- Form Lighting S650 tails plug into the factory connector but are "compatible with USDM models only." This matters if the Saudi car is a GCC-spec car — [LMR Form Lighting](https://lmr.com/item/FL-0087/form-lighting-mustang-sequential-led-taillights-clear-24-25)
- Quality reputation (anecdotal): a mustang6g owner fitting Chinese-made S650 headlights said they worked out of the box but had alignment and fitment issues. Other forum posts say Chinese aftermarket quality varies widely and that even OEM housings may be Chinese-made — [mustang6g thread](https://www.mustang6g.com/forums/threads/installing-s650-headlights-on-s550-fitment-problem.196335/), [TheMustangSource](https://themustangsource.com/forums/f657/question-about-winjet-products-550953)
- Key fob: an Italian 2024 Dark Horse owner bought a cheap AliExpress clone fob (lock/unlock/start/trunk/panic). It worked after FORScan PCM coding, with frequency caveats: EU = 433 MHz, check which frequency your car uses. Another user said welcome lighting didn't work with the Chinese key — [mustang7g forum](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3336998). Legit alternatives: ILCO PRX-FORD-4B10 aftermarket fob for 2023–2025 Mustang — [Smallwood Lock](https://smallwoodlock.com/key-fobs/ilco-2023-2025-ford-mustang-key-fob-prx-ford-4b10/); used OEM 5-button S650 fob — [Redline Auto Parts](https://www.redlineautoparts.com/ford/2024-current-s650-mustang/2024-2025-ford-mustang-s650-5-button-key-fob-automatic-oem-used-fm202/)
- SASO regulation: the technical regulation for auto spare parts covers braking systems, lighting, glass, seat belts and others. Importers fall into categories (original parts from authorized distributors vs commercial/aftermarket). Certificates are issued by SASO-accredited bodies (e.g., TÜV SÜD), not by SASO itself — [TÜV SÜD](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-ae/services/product-certification/saso-saber-product-certification-in-saudi-arabia), [SEMA News on Saudi buyers](https://www.sema.org/news-media/magazine/2020/31/selling-saudi-arabian-buyers)
- SABER requires a Product CoC (PCoC) plus a Shipment CoC (SCoC). Shipments without an SCoC can be required to be re-exported. Auto spare parts get a "categorized conformity assessment" (grouped under one PCoC) — [HQTS](https://www.hqts.com/?p=240092), [TÜV SÜD](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-ae/services/product-certification/saso-saber-product-certification-in-saudi-arabia)
- Vehicle spare parts are listed as regulated products cleared through SABER (KPMG, 2020). Aramex's Saudi customs page lists auto spare parts among items that may need a conformity certificate — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/sa/en/home/insights/2020/10/customs-alert-clearance-of-consumer-goods-through-saber-platform.html), [Aramex customs SA](https://www.aramex.com/customs/SA)
- For personal imports, the only SABER certificate required is a shipment certificate (general guidance, not parts-specific) — [Al Tamimi](https://turtl.tamimi.com/story/law-update-issue-376-transport-and-insurance-focus/page/11)

### Inferences
- EST: Chinese parts make sense for cosmetic, non-safety items: diffuser, lips, spoiler, carbon or replica hood, interior trim pieces, tail-light covers, maybe aftermarket tail-light assemblies and the key fob. At typical China pricing, these often cost 30–60% of US retail plus shipping. This is not verified per item.
- EST: Bulky items (hood, bumper, trunk lid) from China ship by sea/LCL or by consolidated "special line" air freight. Expect 2–6 weeks. Freight is driven by volume, not weight, so a hood's shipping may approach the part's price.
- Lighting and glass are exactly the SASO-regulated categories. A small AliExpress parcel often clears via courier with VAT only, but a customs hold demanding a SABER certificate is a real risk for tail lights and glass. Buying these locally from a Saudi dealer or shop that already holds the certificates avoids it.
- Dark Horse fronts and rears are unique (DH-specific fascia/grille). Many cheap China parts target GT/EcoBoost and say "not for Dark Horse." For bumpers, grille and lips, insist on DH-specific fitment.
- No evidence of Chinese S650 quarter panels, rails, trunk floors, or MagneRide shocks. Treat those as OEM or used-OEM only.

### Gaps
- Direct AliExpress/Alibaba/DHgate/Made-in-China/Taobao prices for S650 tail lights, bumpers, grille, exhaust and trim could not be retrieved (listing pages not indexed or reachable). The user should search directly with terms like "Mustang 2024 S650 Dark Horse" plus the part name. On Taobao, search "福特野马 2024 S650" via agents such as Superbuy/CSSBuy.
- No sourced China-to-KSA shipping rates or transit times for oversized parts.
- No clarity on whether personal (non-commercial) parcels of tail lights or glass are routinely stopped for SABER. Confirm with ZATCA/FASAH or a customs broker.
- No found Chinese MagneRide replacement; aftermarket non-MagneRide coilovers exist from Western brands but were not researched here.

## Q2. Gulf used-parts markets (Riyadh/Jeddah/Dammam تشليح, Haraj, UAE Sharjah, Kuwait)

### Takeaway
Haraj.com.sa has many 2024 Mustang whole-car listings, but searches surfaced Mustang تشليح (part-out) ads mostly for older generations (2005–2014). S650 parts in Saudi scrapyards appear scarce or not indexed. The UAE has plentiful 2024 Mustangs and salvage auctions in Sharjah and Dubai (Marhaba). That makes the UAE the most promising Gulf source for a donor car or used S650 panels, but I found no priced S650 part listings.

### Inferences
- EST: Because S650s are only 2–3 years old, few have reached Saudi تشليح yards. The best bet is posting a "مطلوب" (wanted) ad on Haraj: "مطلوب قطع موستنج 2024 2025 دارك هورس" with part names (ربع خلفي, شنطة, صدام خلفي, اسطبات, دبل, عكس). Also ask Al-Sulay and Al-Hair yards directly by phone or WhatsApp. Used Gulf prices for nearly new parts are often quoted at 40–60% of agency price (EST, not verified).
- EST: A salvage front-damaged Mustang bought at a UAE or US auction as a donor could supply most of the rear (quarters, trunk floor, lid, IRS subframe, diff, halfshafts, tail lights, rear glass). This may be cheaper than buying those items individually. A GT donor works for most rear structure, but Dark Horse–specific items differ: the diff/Torsen and gearing, the MagneRide (optional on GT), and the rear fascia/diffuser.
- Note: importing used parts and cut panels from the UAE into KSA by land is GCC intra-trade. It typically carries no 5% duty when the goods are of GCC origin, but US-origin used parts may be treated differently. EST/unverified.

## Q3. US used parts shipped to KSA (eBay + forwarder, LKQ/car-part.com) and customs

### Takeaway
US used OEM S650 parts are available and fairly priced: a used quarter panel assembly at ~$727, rear subframe at ~$500, GT Torsen diff ~$800–1,000, used tail lights $125–375. Small and dense parts (diff, fob, lights, harness) ship economically by forwarder. Long panels (quarter panel, bumper, trunk lid) get billed on volumetric weight and are very expensive by air, so they need ocean consolidation. Saudi charges 15% VAT on everything. Shipments under 1,000 SAR total (goods + shipping + insurance) are duty-exempt. The duty rate on auto parts above that is commonly 5%, but one source cites 8.5% for HS 8708.99, so verify.

### Cited Findings
- New OEM S650 quarter panel assembly PR3Z-6327841-A: MSRP $2,243.52, discounted to $1,574.95 (coupe) — [Ford OEM Parts Direct](https://www.fordoempartsdirect.com/oem-parts/ford-quarter-panel-assembly-pr3z6327841a)
- Used 2024 Mustang quarter panel assembly listed by a recycler at $727 — [autopartsearch recycler demo](https://demo10.autopartsearch.com/parts/FORD/MUSTANG/888R/2024/QUARTER_PANEL_ASSY/1001995017)
- Used 2024–2025 GT S650 rear subframe crossmember: $499.99 — [Redline Auto Parts](https://www.redlineautoparts.com/ford/2024-current-s650-mustang/2024-2025-ford-mustang-gt-5-0-s650-oem-rear-subframe-crossmember-fm201)
- Used 2024 GT Torsen 3.55 diff/carrier listings: about $807.50–$999.99 (asking prices) — [eBay](https://ebay.com/itm/156880168248), [eBay category](https://br.ebay.com/b/Genuine-OEM-Differentials-Assemblies-Parts-for-Ford-Mustang/262244/bn_32351166)
- Used 2024–2025 Mustang tail lights: $125 (used OEM passenger side), $249.99–$300 (eBay), $375 (PartRequest, 2024–2026 RH LED) — [eBay category](https://www.ebay.com/b/Tail-Light-Assemblies-for-2024-Ford-Mustang/33716/bn_7122910428), [PartRequest](https://www.partrequest.com/catalog/tail-lights/ford/ford-mustang)
- Dimensional weight: bumpers, fenders and hoods are "dimensional weight nightmares." Example: a 15 lb front bumper at 60×18×12 in is billed by size — [atoship](https://atoship.com/blog/auto-parts-ecommerce-shipping-heavy-oversized)
- MyUS sample: a 12 lb, 35×25×8 in package to Saudi Arabia costs about $296–$322 — [MyUS pricing](https://myus.com/pricing/)
- Aramex volumetric weight = L×W×H (cm) / 5000. A Shop & Ship user reported ~90 SAR/kg excluding VAT (undated, anecdotal). Shop & Ship applies max weight/value limits per destination — [Aramex FAQ](https://www.aramex.com/faqs?page=2), [Expat.com forum](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/middle-east/saudi-arabia/806210-shop-ship-aramex.html), [Shop&Ship limits](https://shopandship.com/faq/high-value-and-maximum-weight)
- ZATCA: e-store purchases from abroad are customs-duty exempt if total value (goods + shipping + insurance) is under 1,000 SAR. VAT of 15% still applies — [ZATCA e-stores](https://www.zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs-individual/e-stores-customers.aspx), [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/611390/SAUDI-ARABIA/Customs-No-duty-for-shipments-valued-below-SR1000)
- Duty rate on parts (HS 8708): sources conflict. A commercial calculator cites 8.5% for 8708.99 on some pages and 0–5% on others. No official schedule was retrieved — [dutydecoder US→KSA](https://dutydecoder.com/calculate/us-to-saudi-arabia-automotive-tariffs/)

### Inferences
- EST calculation: an S650 quarter panel crated at roughly 190×100×40 cm has a volumetric weight of 190×100×40/5000 = 152 kg. At ~90 SAR/kg air, that is ~13,700 SAR ($3,650), far more than the part. Same for a rear bumper (~180×50×40 cm → 72 kg vol → ~6,500 SAR). Large panels therefore only make sense by ocean LCL or consolidated sea freight (forwarders such as Shipito, Planet Express or US-based Saudi consolidators offer sea). EST sea LCL for 1–2 m³: roughly $300–800 plus Saudi port/clearance fees. Unverified; get quotes.
- EST landed cost formula: (part + US shipping + intl freight + insurance) × (1 + duty 5% [or 8.5%]) × 1.15 VAT, plus clearance/broker fees. Example: $727 used quarter panel + $600 sea freight → ~$1,327 × 1.05 × 1.15 ≈ $1,602 (~6,000 SAR) EST.
- Best US-forwarder candidates are dense, small, high-value items: diff/carrier, halfshafts (long but thin), MagneRide shocks (check DH-specific part numbers), wiring harness sections, key fob, tail lights, interior trim, ECU/modules. Subframe (~$500) is heavy but compact-ish; borderline by air, better by sea consolidation.
- car-part.com and LKQ (via its online store) search US recyclers by part and year. Many recyclers won't ship internationally, so use a forwarder's US address.

- Official Saudi duty rate for HS 8708 not confirmed. Saudi raised many tariffs in 2020, so verify on the ZATCA tariff search before budgeting.

## Q5. Which parts are worth buying cheap or copied vs must be OEM

### Takeaway
Buy cheap (China or used): cosmetic, non-structural and non-restraint items. Insist on OEM or used-OEM for structure (quarter panels, rails, trunk floor, subframe), restraints (airbags, seatbelts, related modules), brakes, MagneRide dampers and driveline. Glass and lighting can be aftermarket only if they meet SASO and the fit is right.

### Cited Findings
- SASO's auto spare parts regulation specifically covers braking systems, lighting, glass and seat belts. These are the safety-critical categories needing conformity — [TÜV SÜD](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-ae/services/product-certification/saso-saber-product-certification-in-saudi-arabia)
- The OEM quarter panel listing notes a related component "cannot be reused/reinstalled." Ford structural repairs require specific one-time-use parts — [Ford OEM Parts Direct](https://www.fordoempartsdirect.com/oem-parts/ford-quarter-panel-assembly-pr3z6327841a)
- Chinese parts often exclude Dark Horse fitment (e.g., front lip "not suitable for... Dark Horse") — [eBay](https://www.ebay.com/itm/285897004889)
- Clone fob works but some functions (welcome lighting) may not, and it needs FORScan coding — [mustang7g](https://www.mustang7g.com/forums/goto/post?id=3336998)

### Inferences (EST, recommendation tiers)
- **Cheap/China OK:** diffuser, lips, spoiler, aero, interior trim and covers, tail-light trim/covers, cosmetic hood (if not used as a crash-structure replacement; latch and hinge fit matter), exhaust tips and muffler/axle-back (aftermarket or used US), key fob (clone or ILCO, with programming caveats).
- **Used OEM preferred (US/UAE/donor car):** quarter panels, trunk floor, rear rails (cut from a donor by a qualified body shop), trunk lid, rear bumper cover and absorber, tail lights, rear glass and door glass (used OEM or local certified aftermarket glass), IRS subframe, diff, halfshafts, wiring harness sections, MagneRide shocks (DH-specific part numbers).
- **New OEM only:** airbags, seatbelts/pretensioners, restraint control module and crash sensors (often must be replaced after deployment), brake hydraulic parts, structural one-time-use fasteners and adhesives per Ford repair procedures.
- Hood: the Dark Horse hood and front structure are crash-relevant (hood latch, pedestrian-protection behaviour in some markets). A used-OEM hood is the safe budget choice; a carbon China hood is cosmetic and its fitment is risky.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on quality of Chinese S650 glass, exhaust or MagneRide alternatives.
- No sourced Ford collision repair position statement retrieved in this session; the report writer should cross-reference other researchers' notes on Ford structural repair requirements.
