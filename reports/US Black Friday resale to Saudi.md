# Skip the AirPods, chase what Saudi lacks

Almost none of the US Black Friday 2026 deals found so far make money once they are landed in Saudi Arabia. The only clearly profitable lines in this research have **no official Saudi distributor and no US Black Friday discount yet**: the **Steam Deck OLED** (about **+SAR 690 per unit, ~19% margin**, at the normal US price) and, more thinly, the **Meta Quest 3 512GB** (about **+SAR 170, ~6%**). A high-end **RTX 5090** is a speculative third if a US unit can be bought under about **$3,350**. Headline deals such as **AirPods Pro 3 at $199**, **Watch Ultra 4 at $699.99**, **AirPods Max 2 at $449**, **MacBook Air M5 at $1,099**, Switch 2 bundles and PS5 Pro offers all lose **SAR 270 to 1,100 per unit**. Three things kill them. Apple sells directly in the Kingdom, so the price gap is small. The **15% import VAT** applies to every parcel, and **5% duty** kicks in above SAR 1,000. Saudi White Friday (around **20–30 November**) cuts local prices in the same week the US goods ship. Dyson hair tools, perfume, supplements and drones are no-gos for compliance or physical reasons, not just margin. Every number below comes from search-result snippets, not opened pages, so treat this as a shortlist to verify, not a buy list. The single highest-value step this week is to check Haraj, Noon and Amazon.sa for real Steam Deck and Quest sale prices before spending a riyal.

## Taxes and freight add 25–40% before you sell anything

The landed-cost model uses the rules the notes could source. **15% VAT applies to every import regardless of value** ([DHL regulatory update](https://mydhl.express.dhl/at/en/help-and-support/customs-clearance-advice/customs-regulatory-updates/africa-middle-east.html)). VAT is charged on goods plus shipping, insurance and duty ([Fuuffy](https://www.fuuffy.com/en/tax-and-duty/SAUDI%20ARABIA)). **Customs duty is waived only when the consignment, shipping included, is SAR 1,000 or less** ([ZATCA](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/customs_individual/e-stores-customers.aspx); [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/611390/SAUDI-ARABIA/Customs-No-duty-for-shipments-valued-below-SR1000)). Above that, the general rate is 5%, with 0–15% by HS code. The rates for consoles, phones, perfume and hair dryers were not confirmed against the ZATCA tariff, and third-party calculators conflict (0% for smartphones, 6.5% for perfume) ([CalcMyTariff](https://www.calcmytariff.com/country/saudi-arabia/consumer-electronics); [Hijri-calendars](https://hijri-calendars.com/customs-duty-calculator.php?lang=en)). Each declaration of SAR 1,000 or less carries a **SAR 15 fee** ([Saudi Export Pro](https://saudiexportpro.com/رسوم-الجمارك-على-الشحنات-الشخصية/)). Above that, Aramex charges 0.15% (minimum SAR 15) plus **USD 10** ([Stackry](https://www.stackry.com/duties-and-taxes/saudi-arabia)), and carriers may add SAR 20–100 in disbursement fees. The model uses SAR 55.

Freight is the least certain input. MyUS's October 2026 sample for a **1 lb parcel to Saudi Arabia runs from $19.51 (budget) to $21.87 (Aramex) to $42.25 (DHL Express)** ([MyUS](https://www.myus.com/stores/how-to-shop-apple-and-ship/saudi-arabia/)). Shipito adds a processing fee of about $3.25 per package ([DutyGlobal](https://dutyglobal.com/forwarders/shipito)). Billing uses the greater of actual or volumetric weight (divisor 139 imperial, 5000 metric) ([Boxit4me](https://www.boxit4me.com/blog/us-to-saudi-shipping-cost-examples/)). Bulky console and Dyson boxes are punished. Beyond the first pound, the model assumes **$25 + $9 per additional lb + $3 processing**. That is an extrapolation, not a quoted rate, and the shipped weights are general product knowledge, not sourced. Use a forwarder in a no-sales-tax state to avoid the 6–10% US sales tax: **Forwardme (Delaware) ships to Saudi via DHL, FedEx and Aramex** ([Forwardme](https://www.forwardme.com/shipping/us-to-saudi-arabia)), Stackry is in New Hampshire ([Stackry](https://www.stackry.com/)) and Planet Express is in Oregon ([Planet Express](https://planetexpress.com/)). A seller below the **SAR 375,000** VAT-registration threshold cannot reclaim import VAT ([Skylight](https://skylightchat.com/en/resources/saudi-vat-ecommerce-guide)), so the 15% is a full cost.

## Compliance rules remove whole categories before margin does

**Corded US appliances are disqualified.** Dyson representatives say US machines are designed solely for 110–120V and that converters shorten lifespan and void warranty ([Best Buy Q&A](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/questions/dyson-v11-torque-drive-cordless-vacuum-blue-nickel/6331929/question/606701cc-006e-339f-9c3e-bdb6a59ed253); [Dyson Community](https://community.dyson.com/general-discussion-126/dyson-airwrap-how-to-use-120volt-in-euraisia-962)). The same logic applies to Ninja and Shark corded appliances and to the 120V chargers in Home Depot's Milwaukee/DeWalt BOGO kits. USB-powered electronics (Steam Deck, Quest, headphones) are generally universal-voltage, but check every adapter label. **Drones are a hard stop.** GACA says customs clearance cannot be requested without an approved registration for drones of 250g–25kg, and seizures have occurred ([GACA FAQ](https://uas.gaca.gov.sa/uas/faq); [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/1482511)), so ignore the DJI doorbusters Adorama expects on 27 November ([Adorama](https://www.adorama.com/black-friday-cyber-monday/l)). **Perfume and cosmetics** fall under SFDA eCosma notification plus a certificate of conformity, with a resident representative required for foreign makers ([SGS](https://www.sgs.com/-/media/sgscorp/documents/corporate/brochures/sgs-gis-pca-saudi-arabia-sfda-datasheet-a4-en-20-v13.cdn.en.pdf)). Couriers also treat alcohol-based perfume as a restricted flammable ([Fuuffy](https://www.fuuffy.com/en/article/Popular%20Shipping%20Routes/182/hong-kong-to-saudi-arabia)). So even Ulta's 50%-off fragrance deals ([NBC Select](https://www.nbcnews.com/select/shopping/ulta-early-black-friday-sale-2026-rcna605751)) are a trap.

**SABER is the scaling ceiling for electronics.** Personal imports reportedly need only a shipment certificate. Commercial imports need a Product CoC per model and a Shipment CoC per batch before arrival, and mismatched model numbers are a common reason for rejection ([HQTS](https://www.hqts.com/saber-certificate-guide/); [Al Tamimi](https://turtl.tamimi.com/story/law-update-issue-376-transport-and-insurance-focus/page/11)). Wireless approval has moved from CST into SABER ([UL](https://www.ul.com/insights/saudi-arabia-wireless-regulatory-updates)). Multiple identical units in one shipment can be flagged as commercial ([Planet Express](https://planetexpress.com/?p=4618)). Splitting parcels to stay under SAR 1,000 saves duty but not VAT, and systematic splitting looks like evasion. The practical ceiling is a handful of units per category per shipment, each with its own invoice. The traveller route has the same limit: Article 103 exempts personal effects only when they are "not in commercial quantities" ([ZATCA airport guide](https://eservices.zatca.gov.sa/sites/sc/en/CustomsGuideNew/CustomsGuideAirPorts/Pages/Page02.aspx)), and the often-cited SAR 3,000 cap is unconfirmed for 2026 ([House of Saud](https://houseofsaud.com/travel/saudi-customs-rules/)).

**Warranty is the hidden discount on Apple and Sony goods.** Community reports say US Apple warranties are not honoured in Saudi Arabia ([Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256133222)). Jarir services only through the manufacturer or its legal Saudi agent ([Jarir](https://www.jarir.com/sa-en/warranty_policy)), and local retailers market "Middle East version" units explicitly ([Carrefour KSA](https://www.carrefourksa.com/mafsau/ar/smart-watches-wearables/apple-watch-ultra-3-gps-cellular-49mm-middle-east-version-black-titanium-case-with-black-ocean-band/p/2817000000026)). That is why the table prices US units below local retail. eSIM-only iPhones are not a problem, because Saudi iPhone 17 models are eSIM-only too ([MacRumors forums](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/iphone-17-and-iphone-17-pro-models-are-esim-only-in-these-countries.2464993)), but any phone must be carrier-unlocked. Buying is its own hurdle. Apple refuses forwarder addresses, and Best Buy and other retailers reportedly cancel foreign-card or forwarder orders ([Apple Community](https://discussions-cn-prz.apple.com/thread/254991858); [Comgateway](https://www.comgateway.com/blogs/i-nearly-lost-thousands-on-a-google-pixel-9-pro-fold-because-i-didn-t-use-a-us-freight-forwarder)). Budget for cancellations and use a forwarder's assisted-purchase service or a US card.

## Sell on Haraj and Instagram from mid-December, with a CR

For an occasional batch, the cheapest channels are **Haraj, at a 1% fee paid on the honour system** ([Haraj FAQ](https://haraj.com.sa/en/faq)), and **OpenSooq, which charges no sales commission** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/id654456967?l=en-gb)). Add Instagram, Snapchat and WhatsApp for pre-orders and repeat buyers. Noon KSA no longer accepts freelance certificates from new sellers ([Noon](https://helpcenter.noon.partners/en/category/onboarding-and-registration/documents-required-to-sell-on-noon)). Amazon.sa charges referral fees of about 7–10.5%, with a 30% new-seller discount for accounts opened 1 July–31 December 2026 ([Amazon.sa T&Cs](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/41/SA_Referral_Fee_Campaign/Amazonsa_NewSellersFeeReductionTermsandConditionsEnglish.pdf)). That fee alone would erase the Quest margin. Legally, the Ministry of Commerce expects online and social sellers to hold a commercial registration or Maroof listing ([Ministry of Commerce](https://mc.gov.sa/en/mediacenter/News/Pages/12-06-17-01.aspx)). E-Commerce Law fines reach **SAR 1 million** ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/retail/saudi-arabia-tightens-up-ecommerce-law-with-stiff-penalties-1.73327592)), and a unified CR costs about **SAR 200 per year plus chamber fees** ([Etqan Law Firm](https://etqanlawfirm-sa.com/en/?p=23004)). Get the CR before taking deposits.

Timing favours patience. Amazon.sa's 2025 White Friday ran **20–30 November** with "up to 70%" off Apple, Sony and Bose ([Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/ampArticle/2174404)), and Salla lists White Friday 2026 on 27 November ([Salla](https://salla.com/en/tools/seasons-calendar/seasons/white-friday-2026)). Goods bought on US Black Friday land in December, after local prices have already dropped. Selling into the pre-Ramadan gifting run works better: Ramadan is expected to start around **8 February 2027**, with Eid al-Fitr around 9–10 March ([Saudi Life Guide](https://saudilifeguide.com/ramadan-2027-saudi-arabia-dates-eid-holidays/)). The cleanest way to remove inventory risk is a pre-sell. Post a fixed SAR price and an ETA, take a deposit, and buy only what is reserved. No source documents how Saudi buyers respond to this, so start small.

## Conclusion

The arbitrage that worked in older Gulf "suitcase trade" stories, buying Apple and branded goods cheaply in America, has been closed by Apple's direct Saudi presence, universal 15% import VAT and a simultaneous Saudi White Friday. What remains is a **distribution gap, not a discount gap**: products that Valve, Meta and to some extent NVIDIA do not sell through official Saudi channels. That flips the usual Black Friday logic. The best item on this list needs no Black Friday deal at all, and the question that decides profit is not the US sale price but what a Saudi buyer actually pays for a sealed grey unit, a figure no research note verified.

Treat every number here as provisional.
