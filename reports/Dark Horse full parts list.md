# Mustang Dark Horse 2024 Coupe: full repair parts list

**Car:** 2024 Ford Mustang Dark Horse coupe (S650). It is the car with heavy rear-end damage listed on mazadat.sa.
**Scope:** the left quarter panel is replaced. The right quarter panel and the hood are repaired. The airbags are OK. One rear corner has no wheel, tire, brakes or hub.

**How to read this list**
- Prices are US online dealer prices: sale price first, MSRP in brackets. SAR = USD × 3.75. Shipping, 5% duty and 15% VAT are not included.
- ✅ = confirmed for 2024, or the site showed "Fits Your Vehicle".
- ⚠️ = number not verified, or the sources conflict.
- ❓ = number or price not found.

Confirm every number against the VIN with Al Jazirah Ford before ordering. Gulf-spec cars can use different suffixes.
